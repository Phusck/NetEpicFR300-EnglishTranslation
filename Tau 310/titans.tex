\section{Titans}

\subsection{KX139 Ta'unar Supremacy Armour}

The T'au ``Supremacy'' Battlesuit is larger than any other current class of Battlesuit. It is intended to face a threat that the T'au Empire had not yet known: the defence of worlds belonging to their growing domain against the counter-expansion forces of other races. Lacking the manoeuvrability of other Battlesuits, the KX139 Ta'unar Supremacy Armour was designed for static defence, with massively destructive long-range weapons.

Faced with a growing number of enemy heavy assault elements, from Imperial Knights to the alien monstrosities of Tyranid Hive Fleets, T'au pilots were often outclassed. The Earth Caste engineer Fio'o Ke'lshan Sho'aun developed the KX139 to put in place a dynamic but stoic defence against such enemies.

The Ta'unar is equipped with 1 Primary Weapon and 2 Secondary Weapons, plus hull guns, and has the \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Fire on the Move}, and \textbf{Deflector Shields (2)} abilities. In addition, it possesses a Smart Missile system.
