\section{Special Abilities}

\subsection{Drone}

Units with the Drone ability have the Inorganic ability and cannot capture objectives.

\subsection{Markerlight (X cm)}

Markerlights may be used in combination with another shooting activation. If you decide to do so, follow the procedure below. The entire detachment must use its Markerlights at the same time:

\begin{itemize}
    \item For each base in the detachment with this ability, make a To-Hit roll of 3+ with a range of X~cm, as for a standard shot.
    \item Repeat this procedure for every detachment using its Markerlight.
    \item If the shooting detachment can see the designated bases, for each successful hit the shooting detachment may gain a $+1$ To-Hit bonus on one of its attack dice, to be declared before those dice are rolled.
    \item If the shooting detachment cannot see the designated bases, it may fire using the Seeker Missiles rule.
\end{itemize}

The detachment with the Markerlights is not activated and may act later if it has not already done so, but it may not use its Markerlights more than once per turn. Markerlights do not grant a To-Hit bonus to weapons that use a template. If the target is a structure, the Markerlight is placed at its centre. Bases with this ability may use their weapons and their Markerlights at the same time. The Markerlight may be fired before the other weapons, which then benefit from any bonus.

\subsection{Seeker Missiles}

Seeker Missiles may be fired like any other normal weapon, and may also be fired at a detachment that has been marked by a Markerlight without needing Line of Sight to it.

\subsection{Smart Missiles}

Weapons with the Smart Missiles ability may target (with no deviation or penalty) bases out of the firer's Line of Sight that are visible to a base from this codex. This ability cannot be used during Intercept Fire.

\subsection{Deflector Shield}

When a Deflector Shield is hit by an enemy shot, it makes a 3+f save. If the save is passed, the shot has no effect. If the shield's save fails, the shield is destroyed and the hit is cancelled. Deflector Shields grant a Psychic Save (4+). They can be destroyed only by shots with an AP modifier of $-1$ or better.

\subsection{Kroot}

All Kroot bases gain $+1$ to their AF if they fight in forests or woods and cannot be transported.

When a Kroot detachment has no Shaper within 12~cm, it acts instinctively and seeks cover. They may receive only Forced March orders and move towards the nearest forest. If there is no forest, they move towards the nearest cover instead. Once in cover they act normally but must remain there.

\section{Psychic Powers}

\subsection{Tiger Shark}

The Tiger Shark does not have to land to disembark its Drones and may drop Attack Drone bases at any point during its movement.

\subsection{Shield Drones}

When you purchase a Shield Drone support formation, you may attach each Drone to a detachment (except Flyers, Titans, and Praetorians), with a maximum of 4 Drones per detachment. This is done at deployment, before both armies are revealed. The cost of the Shield Drones is scored by the opponent as soon as they are purchased. When the detachment is hit by a shot, before the save, one Shield Drone is destroyed for each point of damage suffered. If there are not enough Drones to stop all the damage, the target is hit by the remaining damage. They do not function against lance attacks, which ignore shields, or against psychic attacks.
