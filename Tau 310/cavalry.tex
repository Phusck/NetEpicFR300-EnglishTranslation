\section{Cavalry}

\subsection{Piranha}

The Piranha is a lightly armoured antigrav that serves as a firing platform that is both fast and manoeuvrable. Gifted with an excellent thrust-to-weight ratio and a high-performance antigrav motor, flown in tandem, the Piranha glides just above the surface of the ground, following the contours of the terrain closely to make the best use of cover. In addition to the main weapon mounted in its nose, the Piranha carries two Drones, which can remain docked to add their firepower, or detach to carry out some other mission in AI mode.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav} ability.

\subsection{Tetra}

The Tetra is a long-range, light, fast antigrav reconnaissance speeder, used by Pathfinders for infiltration and long-range reconnaissance missions. Pathfinder squads often operate well ahead of their cadre, using Tetras to get into position, then camouflaging their vehicles and operating on foot in complete discretion. Once the reconnaissance mission is complete, they return to their speeders and rejoin their own forces at full speed.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Infiltration}, and \textbf{Markerlight (90 cm)} abilities.
