\section{Infantry}

\subsection{Kroot Carnivores}

The Kroot operate independently on the battlefield. They silently take position on the flanks, in dense forests or undergrowth if they have the opportunity. Once posted, they take careful aim at their targets and wait for the right moment to spring their ambush. When an ideal prey finally presents itself, they unleash a devastating volley. Once the enemy is disorganised and weakened, the Kroot burst from cover and launch themselves into the assault in a few agile bounds. The fighting that follows is invariably violent and bloody, for the Kroot are formidable opponents in close combat, undeniably more dangerous than most humans.

Kroot Carnivores have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Camouflage}, and \textbf{Kroot} abilities.

\subsection{Kroot Hounds}

As their name indicates, the Kroot Hound is a canine creature of the Kroot kind, which has evolved into a savage and agile attack beast, very fierce in combat and known for its bad temper. It is a highly intelligent dog, in a bestial way, and capable of understanding its masters' instructions. They are also very capricious, and it is not uncommon for a Hound to devour its master if the latter lets their attention slip. The Kroot charged with keeping the Kroot Hounds in line are often covered in scars, but they develop a close bond with their charges over time and act on the ground as one.

Kroot Hounds have the \textbf{Infiltration} and \textbf{Kroot} abilities.

\subsection{Pathfinders}

Pathfinders are reconnaissance troops, and the eyes and ears of their Commanders, far more effectively than any Drone. The first role of Pathfinders is not to engage the enemy, but to optimise the effectiveness of the rest of the Cadre. Using specially adapted antigrav Devilfish or Tetras, Pathfinders operate as a vanguard in search of enemy forces. They keep a close watch on their adversaries while remaining skilfully hidden, and report details of their nature and disposition thanks to highly encrypted and almost undetectable communication systems.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} and \textbf{Markerlight (75 cm)} abilities.

\subsection{MV71 Sniper Drones}

\subsection{Ethereal}

The Ethereals form the ruling Caste of T'au society, and are born to counsel and guide their race along the path destined for it. They embody both the nobility and the clergy, and the deference shown to them is almost mystical. For important decisions, a wise Ethereal will listen to the elders of each Caste, although they remain sovereign in their choices. The Caste of Ethereals is bound to a precise role: to guide the other Castes so that they work together for the Greater Good. An Ethereal must be a consummate leader.

An Ethereal has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Charismatic (12 cm)} abilities. An Ethereal may use one of its powers each turn.

\subsection{XV15/XV25 Stealth Battlesuit}

Stealth teams are special units that operate as ``lone wolves'' ahead of the army, with a certain degree of independence from the Cadre. They wage a secret war, infiltrating behind enemy lines in search of vulnerable targets. To reach enemy territory undetected, Stealth teams use camouflage technology based on light refraction, built into their Battlesuits. Auxiliary fields muffle sounds and mask them from thermal detectors, which allows them to go as far as possible into enemy territory in order to spring coordinated ambushes.

They have the \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Hard to Hit}, \textbf{Fire on the Move}, \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Markerlight (60 cm)}, and \textbf{Advanced Camouflage} abilities.

\subsection{Vespid Stingwings}

The piercing drone of a Vespid's wings is a sound impossible to forget, even if few victims survive to describe such an experience. Indeed, that noise comes only seconds before the Neutron Blasters unleash devastating shots. The Vespid are an insectoid species that has joined the T'au Empire, and their mobile units, the famous Vespid Stingwings, serve as auxiliaries alongside the Fire Caste, and they are valued for their ability to cross broken terrain quickly. The Vespid excel in a role as a mobile reserve or flanking attack unit, although the T'au mainly use the communion helm provided to the Ka'Bri'Dan to coordinate their actions with those of the rest of the hunting group.

They have the \textbf{Jump Packs} and \textbf{Hard to Hit} abilities.

\subsection{Fire Warriors}

To be born of the Fire Caste is to be born for war. Generations of selective breeding have made these T'au the strongest and most enduring. As soon as they can walk, these warriors-in-the-making enter the academies of the Fire Caste, institutions whose sole purpose is to provide soldiers to serve the expanding T'au Empire. Aspirants are rigorously prepared, mentally and physically, to become Fire Warriors, the professional infantry that forms the backbone of each Sept's Hunter Cadres. Most of these warriors are gathered into Strike Teams, the common and terribly effective core of a typical Sept army.

\subsection{Gue'vesa}

The Gue'vesa, literally ``human helpers'' in the T'au tongue, are human warriors who have made the cause of the Greater Good their own. These humans, often the descendants of troops captured or abandoned during the aborted Damocles Crusade, live and fight today alongside the T'au. For them, destiny has dictated that the Imperial Creed and the rule of Adeptus Terra be replaced by loyalty to the collectivist T'au empire and to the ruling Caste of Ethereals. Human auxiliary troops are a fairly common sight along the western fringes of T'au space, in particular on worlds contested during the Damocles Crusade. The T'au proved a more formidable enemy than expected for the Imperial forces and, when the offensive was halted, they were forced to withdraw in response to the far greater threat represented by the arrival of Hive Fleet Behemoth in neighbouring sectors.

\subsection{Kroot Shaper}

Kroot kindreds are commanded by mysterious beings called Shapers. Their task is to negotiate mercenary contracts and coordinate their strategies with those of their employers, and this prestigious position allows them to obtain more sophisticated equipment than that of their kin. Shapers who fight regularly alongside the T'au often carry Pulse Rifles instead of the primitive armament of the Carnivores they lead. But more importantly, they play a vital role within Kroot society that shapes the fate of the entire species, for Shapers are also responsible for the genetic development of their kind.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{HQ}, and \textbf{Attached Character} abilities.

\subsection{Fireblade}

Fireblades are the most seasoned infantry of their cadre, and have declined the opportunity to don a Battlesuit because of their mastery of the art of fighting on foot. They are excellent leaders, whose great experience allows them to maximise the damage inflicted by their fire. Whether it is a matter of breaking the impetus of an Ork horde or bringing down the Space Marines of the Imperium through their thick armour, none knows the qualities of a Fire Warrior better than a Fireblade.

They have the \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Markerlight (60 cm)} abilities.
