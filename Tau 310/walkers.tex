\subsection{Knarloc Rider}

The Kroot are known for capturing and raising young Knarlocs to form elite mounted units. They mainly use their razor-sharp claws, their keen beaks, and the powerful muscles of their legs to tear their prey apart. The Kroot who ride them carry Kroot rifles to fire from advantageous positions where the Knarlocs can take them. Riding a Knarloc is also considered a social statement.

\subsection{XV8 Crisis Battlesuits}

The XV8 Crisis team is the most common T'au Battlesuit formation, for it crystallises the T'au approach to war: mobility, flexibility, and outrageous firepower. This kind of elite force often forms the spearhead of a Hunter Cadre; alternatively, it may be kept in reserve under the Commander's orders, in order to halt the enemy's advance, or to inflict a decisive blow that will break its resistance once and for all. Crisis teams generally consist of two to three warriors (who can sometimes act in isolation), trained and equipped to fulfil all kinds of functions in the field. For example, against large numbers, a Crisis team will favour Burst Cannons, for their rate of fire is optimal for scything down masses of light infantry. Against heavily armoured targets, Plasma Rifles are preferable for their ability to pierce ceramite, while the Fusion Blaster is the best anti-tank option.

They have the \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Fire on the Move}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.

\subsection{XV9 Hazard Battlesuits}

A new weapon in the arsenal of the T'au Empire, the XV9 ``Hazard'' Close Support Armour is a sophisticated and powerful Battlesuit that has aroused some controversy among the more traditionalist elements of the Earth Caste. Although it is without doubt powerful, its armament has a shorter range and is more complex than any that have preceded it, requiring the skills of a veteran to master it in combat. The XV9 itself demands an enormous expenditure of resources to be produced in useful numbers, resources which, according to some, would be better used elsewhere to build many examples of more proven models. The results obtained in service by the XV9 have converted many Fire Caste war leaders to its cause.

They have the \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Fire on the Move}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities. The plasma flamers of the Mk I variant have the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

\subsection{XV88 Broadside Battlesuits}

The decision to combine the deadly Railgun technology of the Hammerhead tank with the versatility of the XV8 Crisis Battlesuit produced the XV88 Broadside. Designed to provide long-range support, XV88 Broadside teams have surpassed all expectations, becoming essential components of Hunter Cadres and anti-tank units of legendary reputation. Coupled with a powerful particle accelerator, the Railguns release projectiles capable of penetrating the strongest bunkers, or even passing straight through them. The weapon that equips the XV88 Broadside is certainly not as massive as the Railgun mounted on the Hammerhead, but it remains impressive in range and destructive power.

They have the \textbf{Reflex Fire} ability. The weapons of the Mk II variant have the \textbf{Smart Missiles} ability.

\subsection{Krootox}

A salutary example of the evolutionary dead end of the Kroot, Krootox are peaceful massive herbivores, far more powerful than normal Kroot, born of kindreds that fed on the flesh of particularly muscular and barbarous beasts. These families locked themselves into a downward spiral of evolution, losing their intelligence and with it their ability to forge their own genetic future. The result is an extremely muscular and oversized variant of Kroot physiology, whose mind can scarcely display anything but unreasonable brutality. Were it not for the fact that the Kroot found a use for the Krootox, the lineage might have died out generations ago, but instead they are commonly observed among Carnivore kindreds in the T'au Empire and elsewhere.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} and \textbf{Kroot} abilities.

\subsection{XV95 Ghostkeel Battlesuits}

Huge, deadly, and almost impossible to detect until it attacks, the XV95 Ghostkeel ranks among the most effective terror weapons in the galaxy. These Battlesuits are several times the size of a Fire Warrior, and are fitted with a set of repulsion motors as well as effective weapons, not to mention multiple support systems, high-technology devices, and a veritable arsenal of electronic warfare. Ghostkeel Battlesuits can shred armoured squadrons and massacre infantry formations by springing deadly ambushes. The storm of fire they then unleash is such that the enemy has the impression of being attacked by an entire army bursting from an unexpected direction.
