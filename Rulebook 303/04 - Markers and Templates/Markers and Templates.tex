\section{Markers and Templates}

The following templates and markers are used during a game of NetEpic. They are placed on the battlefield to represent areas of effect, orders, and other game states.

\subsection{Templates}

\begin{description}
    \item[Flame Templates:] Teardrop-shaped templates used by flame weapons. A rangeless flame template is placed with its narrow tip over the centre of the firing base. A ranged flame template is placed with its narrow end towards the firing base and its central axis pointing towards the target.

    \item[7.5~cm Templates:] Circular templates with a diameter of 7.5~cm, used by weapons with the \textit{Template (7.5~cm)} ability.

    \item[12~cm Templates:] Circular templates with a diameter of 12~cm, used by weapons with the \textit{Template (12~cm)} ability and when resolving a Titan Fall.
\end{description}

\subsection{Markers}

\begin{description}
    \item[Shields:] These markers record remaining Energy Shields on a base with the \textit{Energy Shields} ability.

    \item[Elite:] These markers identify bases with the \textit{Elite} ability.

    \item[Wounds:] These markers record remaining Wounds on bases that have more than one Wound.

    \item[Buff / Debuff:] These markers record temporary bonuses and penalties applied to bases or detachments.

    \item[Structure Points:] These markers record remaining Wounds on structures.

    \item[Orders:] These counters indicate the orders assigned to detachments: Advance, Charge, Forced March, First Fire, Fall Back, and Without Orders. Orders are placed face down during the Strategy Phase and revealed when the detachment is activated.

    \item[Immobilised:] These markers identify bases that have become immobilised.
\end{description}
