\section{Introduction to NetEpic}

\subsection{A New Rulebook Format}

What you are holding in your hands, or viewing on your screen, is not merely the work of a few passionate players, but of an entire community. NetEpic Palladium builds upon its illustrious predecessors, NetEpic Gold and Epic Space Marine/Titan Legions. Many people have contributed in their own way to the continued development of the rules.

Version 3 of the Palladium rulebook has evolved not only in its content, but also in its format. This book uses a different structure from the previous edition to make the rules clearer and more accessible. It follows a thematic approach, first explaining the sequence of a game turn and then covering other aspects of the game in dedicated sections. We hope that this makes the rules as clear and accessible as possible.

\subsection{Where Can I Find Miniatures?}

Finding miniatures for NetEpic is not particularly difficult. First, there is the second-hand market. Visit the French Discord server listed above, where you will find a classified advertisements section. Some members are selling miniatures while others are looking to buy them. Do not hesitate to post what you are looking for; someone will probably be able to help. There are also several Facebook groups listed on \url{https://netepicfr.com}.
There are also several independent miniature manufacturers. 
A number of them are listed on \url{https://netepicfr.com} or on the Discord server. 
Some manufacturers offer nearly identical miniatures, while others produce excellent proxies.

Finally, there is 3D printing. Once again, the website and Discord server can help you find suitable models and may even help you have them printed if you do not own a 3D printer.

So do not hesitate---get started and play!

\subsection{Intent-Based Play}

NetEpic is a game that values good humour and fair play. The objective is not to crush your opponent, but to enjoy spending time with another player. One of the best ways to achieve this is through what is known as \textit{intent-based play}.

Intent-based play is a way of playing in which players clearly state what they are trying to accomplish with their units. The purpose is not to circumvent the rules, but to make the game flow more smoothly and remain clearer and more enjoyable for both players.

For example, a player might declare:

\begin{quote}
    ``My Predator detachment moves so that it remains outside the line of sight of your Eldar Titan.''
\end{quote}

or:

\begin{quote}
    ``I place my unit exactly 10~cm from the objective so that it can control it without exposing my miniatures to a charge from your Warbikes.''
\end{quote}

Conversely, if one player declares an intention and the other player has a unit or ability that could prevent that intention from being fulfilled, the latter should point this out. Intent-based play therefore relies on communication and mutual trust.

This approach has several advantages:

\begin{itemize}
    \item It reduces disagreements concerning measurements and lines of sight.
    \item It speeds up the game.
    \item It limits ``gotcha'' moments caused by poor visibility or an accidental movement error.
\end{itemize}

At its heart, intent-based play is based on a simple idea: in a strategy game, victory should primarily be determined by the players' tactical decisions---not by a few misinterpreted millimetres around a terrain feature.

We therefore encourage you to play fairly and to use intent-based play. It will only make your games more enjoyable and interesting.
