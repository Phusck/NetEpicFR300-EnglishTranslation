\section{Infantry}

\subsection{Fusiliers}

The Fusiliers are the troops employed by the Exodites. In civilian life they live on the Baron's lands and must repay him by fighting in his army whenever he judges it necessary.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability.

\subsection{Scouts}

The Scouts are the standard Exodite outriders. They are formidable snipers and are quick to delay enemy armies with exemplary efficiency.

The Scouts are a remarkable asset for the Exodites.

They have the \textbf{Scout}, \textbf{Free Deployment}, \textbf{Sniper}, and \textbf{Camouflage} abilities.
