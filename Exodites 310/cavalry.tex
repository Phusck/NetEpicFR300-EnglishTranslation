\section{Cavalry}

\subsection{Travois}

An Exodite Travois is an antigrav platform drawn by a dragon. It is used for a single purpose: to deploy the weapon quickly, fire, and then move again.

All Travois have the \textbf{Fire on the Move} and \textbf{Cannot Be Transported} abilities. In addition, the Star Cannon Battery has the \textbf{AA} ability, and the Missile Launcher has the \textbf{Artillery} and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} abilities.

\subsection{Dragon Knights}

Dragon Knights are the most common troops in a Baron's service. They are generally the young members of the clan whose role, in peacetime, is to guard the herds. In battle they ride the most common and easiest mount, because they are not trained to other beasts. Dragon Knights carry a Shard Carbine and a wraithbone shield.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability.

\begin{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Lethosaur Knights}

Lethosaurs are smaller than Dragons, with chameleon-like skin. They are ridden by scouts who need to close with the enemy while avoiding direct contact.

\subsection{Baron}

Barons may be the lords of their territory or a member of that family, often a son seeking to prove himself. The Baron does not merely command the army; he owns it. It is composed of his retainers and the people who live on his lands. The forces at his disposal symbolise his status and his wealth. Barons are armed with a Laser Lance and equipped with a wraithbone shield.

Barons have the \textbf{Leader}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Lance (5+/AP $-1$)} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
20 cm & 4+/6+f & +6 & Attached & Shuriken Pistol & 20 cm & 2 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Pterosaur Knights}

Pterosaurs are the only true flying creatures on most Exodite worlds. Pterosaur Knights are used as reconnaissance troops when other scouts are hindered by the terrain. Their main role is to swoop down on the enemy and bring support where it is needed.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav} and \textbf{Deep Strike (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Raptor Knights}

Raptors are extremely agile insectivorous bipeds, large enough for a rider but of slight build. They are the most common scouts in Exodite armies and are also used as fighters because of their excellent reflexes and vicious temperament.

They have the \textbf{Scout} and \textbf{Infiltration} abilities.

\subsection{Dragoons}

Dragoons are drawn from the finest soldiers of the Baron's household. They are most commonly employed to break enemy lines, but they are just as formidable in the assault with pistol and lance. Dragoons are armed with a Laser Lance and a wraithbone shield.

\subsection{Visionary}

Exodite Visionaries have the powers of the spirits at their disposal and are among the psykers of greatest potential in the galaxy. Because their race is in tune with psychic energies and respectful of the spirits of the dead, they are feared by the other Eldar. Visionaries wear spirit armour modelled from the wraithbone of the world-spirit itself.

Visionaries have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)} abilities. In addition, a Visionary may use one of his powers each turn.

20 cm & 6+f & +4 & Attached & Shuriken Pistol & 20 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Vyper Transport}

The Vyper Transport is an adaptation of the Vyper created for the Exodites' needs, imitating the Venom transport used by the Harlequins. The shuriken cannon has been replaced by an open transport deck, turning it into a light transport.

The Vyper Transport has the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Transport (1)}, \textbf{Attached Transport}, and \textbf{Open-Topped} abilities.

\subsection{Salamander}

The fire-spitting Salamander holds great importance in Exodite myth, having much in common with the traditional dragons of Eldar legend. Their breath has enormous destructive power, immolating unwary targets.

It has the \textbf{Infiltration} ability. Its weapon has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.
