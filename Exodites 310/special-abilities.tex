\section{Special Abilities}

\subsection{Holo-Field}

Some units use a protection system known as a Holo-Field. This device wreaks havoc upon targeting systems and makes the unit difficult to hit. The field's dispersion and the distortion it produces depend on both the mass of the unit and the speed at which it is moving.

A Holo-Field grants \textit{Protection (X+)} against all shooting, depending on the order of the base it protects.

\section{Psychic Powers}

\subsection{Visionary}

\subsubsection{The Executioner [Combat Phase]}

The Visionary projects his mind out of his body and launches an attack against his enemies. Nominate an enemy base within 45~cm and in line of sight. Immediately resolve an assault against it. This is a Psychic Attack with an AF of $+4$. Psychic Saves must be made before the assault is resolved in order to cancel the effect. The Visionary receives the usual bonuses for previous attackers and also counts among the previous attackers for the target's subsequent opponents. The attack counts as an assault by a Class 2 base.

\subsubsection{Fortune [Movement Phase, upon activation]}

The Visionary looks into the near future to predict enemy attacks. Nominate an allied detachment within 12~cm. That detachment gains 3 Fortune counters, which function as Elite counters for this turn.

\subsubsection{Heal [Movement Phase, upon activation]}

The Visionary may try to heal wounded bodies. He gains the \textit{Medic} ability until the end of the turn.
