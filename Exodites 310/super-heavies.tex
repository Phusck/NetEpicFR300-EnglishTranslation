\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Megadon}

Megadons are enormous omnivorous reptiles. The Exodites use this species because of its relative intelligence. The Megadon answers simple orders from the driver on the howdah on the creature's back. The military application of such beasts is obvious: in battle they make excellent mobile heavy-weapon platforms, combining long-ranged artillery with a terrifying close-combat capability. The problem with Megadons is that they are still animals, and if they are badly wounded they do not always obey the driver.

Megadons have the \textbf{All-Round Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, and \textbf{DR (5+)} abilities.

The Assault version also has the \textbf{Transport (6)} and \textbf{Open-Topped} abilities. Its Heavy Thermal Lance has the \textbf{Turret} and \textbf{Damage (+1)} abilities. Its Flamers have the \textbf{Turret} and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} abilities.

The Support version's Twin Star Lance has the \textbf{Turret} and \textbf{Damage (+1)} abilities.
