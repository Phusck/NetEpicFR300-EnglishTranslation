\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Scout}

If the Exodites are your primary army, choose one of the following abilities at the beginning of the battle, before objectives are placed.

\begin{itemize}
    \item For every complete 3 detachments with the \textit{Scout} ability, the Exodite player chooses one Class 1--3 detachment. That detachment gains \textit{Infiltration} (the chosen detachments need not have the \textit{Scout} ability themselves).

    In addition, the player gains 2 orders in reserve as though the army had a detachment with the \textit{Master Strategist} ability.

    \item Exodite Class 1 and 2 bases gain the \textit{Camouflage} ability for Turn 1 only.

    \item The Exodite player may place one forest for every complete 2 detachments with the \textit{Scout} ability, to a maximum of 3 forest areas, at the same time as fortifications. Each feature may be no larger than 10~cm by 10~cm and may be placed anywhere on the battlefield except on another terrain feature.

    \item For every complete 2 detachments with the \textit{Scout} ability, the Exodite player chooses one opposing Class 1--4 detachment deployed on the table. That detachment must receive an Advance order on Turn 1.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Forester}

Exodites suffer no movement penalties when they move through forests.
