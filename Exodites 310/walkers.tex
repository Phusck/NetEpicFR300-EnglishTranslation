\section{Walkers}

\subsection{Combat Walkers}

The Asuryani use many forms of bipedal war machine. The smallest and lightest are designated Combat Walkers. These one-man walkers resemble the Sentinels of the Imperial Guard, but they are far more agile, because the machine's sophisticated gyroscopic stabilisation and articulated feet allow it to cross the most broken ground at speed. They also have more firepower with which to destroy the enemy. The role of these machines is to conduct aggressive reconnaissance. Once the enemy is located, the Combat Walkers hasten to pin it in place.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability.

\subsection{Reconnaissance Walkers}

The craftworlds use jetbikes for reconnaissance and rapid attacks. The Exodites use reconnaissance walkers, able to withstand the sudden storms that sweep the verdant steppes and to pass through dense undergrowth. They are more lightly armed than Combat Walkers and do not have their protective energy shields. Combined with their speed, this makes them ideal for lightning attacks.

They have the \textbf{Scout} and \textbf{Free Deployment} abilities.
