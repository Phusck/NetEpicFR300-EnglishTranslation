\subsection{Artisan}

Artisans are builders who design machines, structures, spacecraft, weapons, and other military equipment. An Artisan generally begins any project by examining the records to see whether something similar has already been attempted; if so, the previous plans are used, with an adaptation if necessary. Artisans exercise overall control over the vast workforce of Servitors on a given Forge World.

The Artisan has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Artisan} abilities. In addition his Graviton Pistol has the \textbf{Graviton} ability.

\subsection{Sicarian Ruststalkers}

The Ruststalkers of the Skitarii legions fall upon their enemies in a frenzy. Their transonic blades become a blur as they cut like gladiators' weapons, passing through armour as if it offered no more resistance than a hologram. The Ruststalkers were originally cybernetic assassins, sent across the Red Planet to cut down feral Servitors and undesirables excommunicated by the Cult Mechanicus. They proved so effective that they were reused and organised into groups called killclades. Since then the manufacture of Ruststalkers has spread to every Forge World, and these infantry have been in the front line of many battles.

Sicarian Ruststalkers have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability.

\subsection{Electro-Priests}

Also called Luminen or ``sparks of life'', Electro-Priests form sub-orders within the hierarchy of the Tech-Priests. Fanatics of the Motive Force, the third component of the Machine God's trinity, they believe that all life and all movement are set in motion by that ineffable divinity. The cybernetic grafts practised on the Electro-Priests' nervous systems allow them to channel electricity into everything their copper-plated palms touch, plunging them into an ecstatic frenzy. At the height of his religious madness, the devotee destroys the enemies of the Machine God with living lightning, or drains their vital energy as a Catachan mega-leech sucks blood.

They have the \textbf{Elite (1)} ability. Their Electro-Lance has the \textbf{Lance (5+ / AP 0)} and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} abilities.

\subsection{Hastarii}

The Hastarii are a vital element for the Skitarii, combining the heavy firepower of far larger machines with armoured cyborg bodies modified to carry such destructive weapons to the front lines. Their right arm is replaced by a gyro-stabilised weapon mount plugged directly into the cortical circuit, giving them a singular accuracy despite the need for advanced dorsal generators, as heavy as they are difficult to manufacture. Hastarii Exterminators are armed with eradication beamers, projecting a short-range cone of atomising energy, while Hastarii Fusiliers pierce vehicles and scramble their systems with precise shots from their neutron lasers. Both variants are highly effective in their dedicated task, but complex and costly to create, so they are rarely deployed when their loss would not be justified.

\subsection{Logis}

The Logis form the corps of logisticians and analysts who indirectly control the resources of a Forge World. \subsection{Magos}

The Magi are adherents of the Cult of the Machine who have served the Machine God for so long and with such devotion that the greater part of their bodies is now composed of machines. Over decades and centuries of service, a Magos's limbs and organs have been replaced one by one with cybernetic spare parts that do not weaken, never tire, and escape the ravages of time. The further he advances along this path, the more mechanical parts a Magos accumulates. The biological remnant of the oldest among them is finally reduced to a tiny scrap of grey matter, lodged in the depths of their armoured cortex, lost in a mass of cables, ports, and ventilation ducts. As a servant of the Omnissiah progresses in the Quest for Knowledge, he alters far more than his physical appearance. As his body is rebuilt, his mind is modified. Many of them follow such lines of research, setting aside every other consideration, and sometimes spend entire decades cloistered in their laboratoriums in order to study a remnant of technology from a civilisation long extinct.

The Magos has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Versatile} abilities. In addition his Grav-gun has the \textbf{Graviton} ability.

\subsection{Skitarii Vanguard}

The Skitarii Vanguard, nicknamed rad-troopers, are so steeped in toxic energy that their mere proximity is fatal. During the many civil wars of the Cult Mechanicus, the Vanguard learned to endure these deadly emissions and to make them their weapons. The level of radiation emitted by the Vanguard's equipment is such that it is enough to be near them to feel its weakening effects; being in the same barracks is a death sentence for a non-Skitarii. The Vanguard themselves are theoretically protected under their brigandine, but the rare times they remove their helmets, their missing teeth, their baldness, and their ravaged skin betray the horrible truth. They accomplish their duty with stoicism and in solemn silence, fighting to the last in the name of their mechanical god. The most blessed of the Skitarii pass a threshold called the Crux Mechanicus, when their body is more metal than flesh, and then become Skitarii Alphas. Some reach the rank of Alpha Primus, overseers who operate independently for years if necessary, as solid and reliable as the titanium, steel, and iron that replace their flesh.

\subsection{Pteraxii}

\subsection{Skitarii Rangers}

The Skitarii are the infantry of the Adeptus Mechanicus. They are deployed in mass to defend their Forge World, to keep the enemy from approaching the feet of the gigantic Titans, or sent across the galaxy to march in the front line of expeditions that will bring new knowledge back to their Tech-Priest masters. Thanks to these intensive modifications, the Skitarii excel in every type of environment. Each legion comes from a hyper-industrialised Forge World: even extreme doses of radiation are nothing to those who have walked such toxic desolations. The Skitarii will stop only once their task is accomplished. Whatever the dangers, the march of the Skitarii never falters.

\subsection{Secutarii}

The principal formation of the Secutarii on the battlefield is the phalanx of Hoplites, a defensive force that is augmented and equipped specifically to resist infantry assaults and defy the odds on the most violent and dangerous battlefields. Their strength comes both from their resolute discipline in combat and from the crackling energy barriers generated by their interlocking Kyropatris field generators. The power of these surgically implanted generators is channelled and amplified by their shields, which allow them to resist phenomenal quantities of fire. Their counter-attack is deadly, their Arc Lances being used to set the battlefield ablaze in murderous torrents of artificial lightning. Their inhuman clinical training and cogitator-assisted combat protocols allow them to operate both in dispersed skirmish lines to escort the god-machines, or to advance in close ranks to annihilate resistance.

The Secutarii -- Hoplites have the \textbf{Dodge (5+)} ability. Their Arc Lance has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

\subsection{Servitors}

The total number of Servitors of a single Forge World generally amounts to tens or even hundreds of millions. Many were once wanted criminals --- toughs from every stratum of the Imperium, from hive gangers to agents of the Inquisition who have simply seen too much. Smuggled in during exchanges of raw material between the planets of the Imperium and the Forge Worlds, cargoes of human freight are not rare, and represent another source of flesh that can be put to good use by the Tech-Priests. One begins by erasing the specimen's memory by a chemical lobotomy, so that its personality is no more than a blank page, in theory at least, for it is not rare for residual effects to persist. Then its arms are cut off and replaced with weapons or tools suited to the new role its masters have assigned it. The process is extremely painful --- the grafts, the welding of flesh, and the fixing of cables to the muscles are bloody work --- but after all, redemption implies sacrifices. And no sacrifice is too great for the Omnissiah.

\subsection{Tech-thralls}

A Tech-thrall is a cybernetic servitor created by the Mechanicum at the time of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. These cyborgs were created as needs required, by replacing certain parts of the subject's organic anatomy, central nervous system, and physiology with basic implants. In the case of the Legio Cybernetica, Tech-thralls were generally created from captured prisoners and criminals requisitioned from the local authorities. Considered by their masters as beings of a rank inferior even to Servitors, the ultimate fate of most Tech-thralls was to end their lives recycled into Servitor components, their implants being taken to be used in future thralls, their bodies giving out under the incessant labour to which they were subjected.

\subsection{Thallax}

The cyborgs of the Mechanicum's Ordo Reductor are heavily reinforced, and the Thallax are distinguished from Skitarii regiments both by their purpose and by the unique degree of their augmentics. The Lorica Thallax that envelops them was developed from the same energy-armour technology that led to the development of the Legiones Astartes' power armour, but to an entirely different purpose. The Lorica encompasses the principal organs, the nervous system, and the brain, but has replaced the skeleton and limbs with armoured mechanical systems powered by an internal reactor heart. The suffering induced by this process, combined with the replacement of the usual human sensory apparatus, proved so traumatic that it required the surgical excision of the centres of pain and emotion, making the Thallax a cold and calculating killing machine, but one that retains a certain independence of human thought, although for some within the Mechanicus this borders on abomination. Conversion into a Thallax is often reserved for Forge Guard, Skitarii fallen in battle, and subjects obtained from more mysterious sources by the unfathomable and sinister Ordo Reductor.

They have the \textbf{Jump Packs} ability.
