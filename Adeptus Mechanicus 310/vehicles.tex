Archaeopter Fusilaves, the artillery version, drop carpets of tectomagnetic bombs causing seismic shockwaves. \subsection{Archaeopter Transvector}

The ancient design of the Archaeopter was judged holy enough to fulfil secondary roles. Archaeopter Transvectors carry out exploration tasks, ferrying agents behind enemy lines or into the heart of battle. An ideal aerial transport, the Transvector is employed in numbers in Exploration fleets for reconnaissance and rapid extraction in hostile environments. They are incredibly agile, their implanted pilots able to alter the shape of the wings according to atmospheric conditions, while controlling their Cognis heavy stubbers to scythe down approaching enemies.

The Archaeopter Transvector has the \textbf{Floater} and \textbf{Transport (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Skorpius Disintegrator}

Skorpius Disintegrators are swift assault tanks bristling with heavy weapons. As individual support vehicles or gathered in waves of dozens of elements, Disintegrators have a high rate of fire. Salvos of missiles lift off in pre-programmed waves, and the clatter of reloading is heard even before the first detonations. The tanks' principal armament can vary. Some carry a Belleros Energy Cannon, whose projectiles are propelled by the same gas as the vehicle, superheated and reinjected; they explode on impact in a ball of spectroscopic energy.

The Skorpius Disintegrator's missile launchers have the \textbf{Artillery} ability.

\subsection{Karacnos}

The Karacnos assault tank is a type of tank used by the Adeptus Mechanicus. It is a variant of the Triaros armoured carrier dedicated to assault operations. Its transport compartments have been replaced with heavy weapons, notably the Karacnos's mortar battery. This mortar fires radioactive warheads designed to sweep an area of all organic life. The vehicle was also equipped with Hunter-Killer missiles and was protected both by heavy armour and an ion shield.

\subsection{Krios}

A pattern originating from Mars, the Krios uses many integrated systems well beyond the reach of most STC construction or maintenance facilities. Thus, despite its age and proven existence in the Mechanicum's arsenals, it has never served outside the Taghmata and the Skitarius. Comparable in many respects, in terms of function and tactical role, to the Predator Tank more widely used by the Astartes, it does not have a flexible turret mount for its principal armament. Unlike more conventional armoured vehicles, its durability is assured by interlocking defensive energy fields (and in some cases by powerful anti-missile shield generators) rather than by physical armour, which gives it a deceptive appearance.

\subsection{Krios Venator}

This variant of the Krios adapts the tank into a powerful anti-personnel firing platform by replacing its Lightning Cannon with a Pulsar-Fusil. This ancient and complex weapon system is a relic of the Dark Age of Technology, and its secrets are jealously guarded by those who possess them. It is a multi-chamber particle-beam cannon designed to fire volleys of destructive energy bolts that explode on contact with their target, tearing armoured vehicles apart by repeated strikes.

Their Pulsar-Fusil has the \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-5$/1)} ability.

\subsection{Onager Dunecrawler Scout}

The Onager Dunecrawler Scout houses two crew members who share a symbiotic relationship with their Onager. The gunner is a Skitarii Ranger, whose formidable aim is improved by a panoply of autosenses. The driver is a Skitarii Vanguard, his resistance to radiation allowing him to immerse himself in an electro-amniotic tank that offers him a direct link with the machine spirit. These drivers are used as simple batteries because they are easily replaceable; it is enough to insert a new Vanguard into the electrode-filled soup to confer on the Onager a new period of life. The Skitarii crew accept their fate without complaint, for serving the Machine God is reward enough.

Its Eradication Beamer has two possible firing modes and it may choose one per turn. Both firing modes have the \textbf{Turret} ability. The Autocannon also has the \textbf{Turret} ability. The Onager Dunecrawler Scout has the \textbf{Infiltration} and \textbf{Walker} abilities.

\subsection{Skorpius Dunerider}

The seismic tumult of roaring engines and the wreaths of fluorescent smog announce the deployment of a Forge World's tanks and hover transports. A cushion of hyper-reactive gas pushes these angular war engines above dangerous terrain, while the pressure under their chassis flattens the earth. Most Forge Worlds are toxic dumps --- the result of millennia of industrial overproduction --- and the Skorpius vehicle was developed to cross the acid marshes of those planets. Under a pressurised hood of reinforced nanofibre, an alchemical gas is atomised by half-forgotten processes, creating the impulse and spitting a swirl of smoke.

The Skorpius Dunerider has the \textbf{Transport (2)} and \textbf{Attached Transport} abilities.

\subsection{Termite}

A specialised transport vehicle, the Termite is capable of deploying a complete platoon of warriors on the battlefield while bypassing enemy fortifications and sentries. It is able to dig tunnels rapidly through the densest materials at speeds comparable to those of surface transport engines, thanks to its mill which uses a set of flux-cutters and phase-shield generators to aid its progress. Once at its target location, it emerges on the surface, or even directly into the lower level of macro-fortifications, scattering nearby enemies with its fusion cutters before disgorging its deadly cargo.

It has the \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Attached Transport}, and \textbf{Transport (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Triaros}

The Triaros was the principal armoured transport of the Mechanicum Taghmata on the battlefield at the time of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. Drawn from the most common STC patterns, it relies on a number of unique technologies held as arcana by the Autokrator Magos and which have never been disclosed for wider Imperial use, even for the Legiones Astartes. Designed to be used in hostile environments, it incorporates multiple galvanic traction systems, and its defences consist not only of raw protection but also of several layers of techno-canticle armour and energy shields, particularly around its seemingly exposed control platform, as well as independently animated weapon Servitors. Its large storage bay can be hermetically sealed if necessary, allowing the Triaros to be used in hard vacuum and on the surface of lifeless worlds. Its internal bay is equipped with numerous specialised fittings for recharging, cold-storage, and the maintenance of technical installations and light automata as needed. Its most remarkable feature, however, is a powerful reactor-powered shock ram mounted at the front, which can be used to smash and incinerate anything it touches.

The Triaros may transport Robots (which count as 2 bases as usual). It has the \textbf{Transport (4)}, \textbf{Attached Transport}, and \textbf{Protection (4+) to the front} abilities.
