\section{Cavalry}

\subsection{Kataphron Breachers}

In battle, Kataphron Breachers charge on the order of their Tech-Priest masters, playing the role of living shield and ram. They were originally designed to break enemy lines and fortifications. As they approach opposing formations, volleys rain down on the Breachers to little effect; the thick overlapping plates that cover their organic components make them resistant to firearms. When their proximity alarm sounds, the Breachers reply with helical discharges from their torsion cannons. While the enemy falls back, the Breachers accelerate and smash the line, crushing and burning with their mechanical, electrified claws. Even if Kataphron Breachers are formidable, they can be easily replaced and gathered by the thousand if necessary. All Forge Worlds manufacture dozens of such warrior constructs every day.

\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Kataphron Destroyers}

The Destroyer's two forelimbs are replaced with heavy weapons. This configuration allows it to accomplish its role as a mobile heavy-weapon platform, a task it carries out with exceptional efficiency. A cannon of advanced design is mounted on one of its flanks, ranging from the dreaded Grav-cannon, a weapon so powerful that it can flatten even Aeldari wraithbone ghost-constructs in an instant. Every Servitor bears an identification number, and Battle Servitors are no exception. These numbers become their only semblance of identity. Their actions are directed by their limited programming, for only defective Servitors possess a consciousness of themselves. The Kataphron Destroyer's optics are designed to concentrate on the enemy, allowing the construct to identify and eliminate viable targets, but owing to the limits of their programme, many incidents of friendly fire have been recorded.

The Kataphron Destroyer's weapon has the \textbf{Graviton} ability.

\subsection{Serberys}

It is a great honour to be promoted from the Skitarii manipules into the Serberys corps. They ride swift quadruped constructs that allow them to negotiate the most broken terrain with ease, while the riders are held in place by magnetic locks and gyro-stabilisers.

The Serberys have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability. Their weapon has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

\subsection{Vultarax}

The Vultarax is a strato-automata much used by the Mechanicum at the start of the wars of the Horus Heresy. It is a multi-role war engine, heavily armed and fitted with sophisticated sensor equipment that is capable of operating in hostile environments. It proved to be the keystone of the Mechanicum's Explorator Expedition forces, both as a scout and as a rapid-intervention unit in open combat. Their armament, armour, and targeting system make the Vultarax a formidable force on the battlefield. The mechadendrites that emerge from its body can also be transformed into power blades, which makes the Vultarax incredibly dangerous if it decides to remain hovering above its target.

The Vultarax has the \textbf{Skimmer} ability. Its weapon has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.
