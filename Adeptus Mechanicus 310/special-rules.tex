\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Machine God}

Any detachment within 45~cm of a Titan or an Ordinatus (not Minoris) from its army may reroll failed Morale tests. If a Titan or an Ordinatus (not Minoris) is destroyed, all detachments within 30~cm of the Titan or Ordinatus must take a Morale test immediately.

\subsection{Allies}

You may take up to 25\% allies from the Imperial Knights or Titan Legions codex.
