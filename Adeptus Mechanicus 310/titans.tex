\section{Light Titans}

\subsection{Warhound Titan}

Warhounds form the smallest class of Titans. They are the eyes and ears of the Titan Legios and, during campaigns, range ahead of the main groups. Faster and more agile than Reavers or Warlords, they lack their armour and firepower, however. Like all Princeps, Warhound commanders perceive and react through their Titan's control sensors and neural links. Their daring is proverbial. Warhounds often operate in pairs, so that they can outpace and outflank larger Titans they may encounter. In a major engagement, Warhounds operate on the flanks and seek to engage enemy tanks and infantry rather than other Titans, where their Vulcan Mega-Bolters, Inferno Guns, and Turbo-Lasers (all lighter Titan weapons) can still inflict immense damage.

The Warhound Titan must buy 2 weapons. It has the \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{Extended Coherency (25 cm)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (2)}, and \textbf{Agile} abilities.

\section{Line Titans}

\subsection{Reaver Titan}

The Reaver is a very old Titan pattern, and if it is not as heavily armed as the Warlord (which forms the backbone of the Titan Legios' Battle Titans), or as fast and agile as the Warhound, it remains a powerful war machine in its own right. It is protected by thick adamantium armour plates and endowed with devastating firepower. Like its larger cousin, the Reaver is regarded as a god-machine of destruction, an incarnation of the Machine God's power, thrice blessed by the Tech-Priest of the Adeptus Mechanicus before committing to battle. Each Reaver is commanded by a Princeps, who is connected directly to the Titan's Cerebral Impulse Unit. He is assisted by two crew members, holding the rank of Moderati, who control the Titan's movement and weapons. The Reaver's power source is its plasma reactor, which is maintained by a Tech-Priest who carefully manages the Titan's available power supply and is in turn assisted in his complex task by Servitors connected directly into the Titan's systems. The Reaver comprises three weapon mounts. Its carapace weapon is the equivalent of a smaller Warhound-sized weapon, while its arms carry the heavier weapons --- up to Warlord size. The largest Titan weapons, such as Quake Cannons, are still beyond a Reaver's load capacity. The Mars pattern is said to be the oldest Titan archetype still in service, its origins even preceding the Great Crusade.

The Reaver Titan must buy three weapons (two arm weapons and one carapace weapon) and may buy 1 Banner option and 1 Head. It has the \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (4)} abilities.

\subsection{Warbringer Titan}

The Warbringer Nemesis Titan is an immense war machine. It is designed to destroy enemy Titans at range and to serve as a fire-support platform. It sits between the more common Reaver and Warlord classes. Its mass and firepower often place it on the front lines of the battlefield alongside the various classes of Battle Titans. Its defence batteries protect it adequately against aerial attacks. Within the Titan Legios, it has the reputation of having a restless Machine Spirit. Sensor artefacts and other aberrant phenomena are commonplace aboard such a god-machine. The Tech-Priests who serve these machines praise the complex mysteries that led to this creation, and many among the Magi of the Machine God consider it an honour to be assigned to such a machine.

The Warbringer Titan must buy two arm weapons and choose one carapace weapon (either a Volcano Cannon or a Quake Cannon, the cost of the dorsal weapon already being included in the Titan's cost) and may buy 1 Head. The Cerberus Cannons have the \textbf{AA} ability. The Warbringer Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (7)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (5)} abilities.

\subsection{Warlord Titan}

The Warlord Battle Titan has always been the heart of the Titan Legios; indeed its design predates the Imperium itself. Some Warlords have been in service since the Dark Age of Technology, and many others date from the Age of Strife. These ancient war machines are carefully preserved and maintained by the Tech-Priests, so that energy still surges through their powerful limbs when the correct litanies are performed. The Warlord is a heavy Titan intended to fight on the most active front lines. It is protected by six Shield generators and one of the thickest armours that can be seen on a Battle Titan. The Warlord can mount up to four independent principal weapon systems and several secondary weapon systems to increase its short-range firepower. The great longevity of the Warlord Titan has led to a plethora of specialised equipment designed for it. Some Titan Legios are almost entirely composed of Warlord Titans equipped with siege-warfare devices and are used only to demolish fortresses and cities that resist the Emperor's will.

The Warlord Titan must buy four weapons (two arm weapons and two carapace weapons) and may buy 2 Banner options INC and 1 Head. The Warlord Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (9)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (6)} abilities.
