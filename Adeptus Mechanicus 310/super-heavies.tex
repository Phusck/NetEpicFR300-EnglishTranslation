\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Ordinatus Minoris}

The legendary macro-machines of the Centurio Ordinatus are rare and truly obscure weapon systems, of staggering power, ranging from the unique and colossal Ordinatus Primaris, such as Mars and Endymion, to the smaller but still gargantuan Ordinatus Minoris, such as the Ulator, Sagittar, and Urkallac classes, which nevertheless remain among the most powerful war machines available to the Imperium. Although they are not truly unique, like the war machines of the Ordinatus Primaris, the Minoris macro-machines were still rarer than the Battle Titans of the Titan Legios, and only a handful of Forge Worlds had the resources or even the doctrinal blessing and religious authority to produce or maintain such incarnations of the Omnissiah's divine wrath. There are four versions of the Ordinatus Minoris.

The Grav-cannon version is equipped with a Graviton Destroyer that has the \textbf{Graviton} and \textbf{Damage (+1)} abilities.

The Multiple Rocket Launcher version is equipped with a Multiple Rocket Launcher that has the \textbf{Artillery} ability and two firing modes. It may choose between each of the two modes each turn. The Penetration mode has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} ability, while the Saturation mode uses a \textbf{Template (12 cm)}.

The Quake Cannon version is equipped with a Quake Cannon that has the \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/3)} ability and uses a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)}.

All versions have the \textbf{Wounds (2)} and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities.

\subsection{Mole}

The Mole is an intermediate-sized tunneler able to transport up to 60 men from underground and emerge in the heart of the enemy ranks to pour troops as close as possible to the fighting. Once it has come out of the earth, it can bring fire support to the disembarked troops thanks to its medium-range armaments.

Its Multiple Launcher has the \textbf{Artillery} ability. The Mole has the \textbf{Attached Transport}, \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, and \textbf{Transport (12)} abilities.
