\subsection{Ordinatus Armageddon}

The Ordinatus Armageddon first spilled blood during a massive Chaos attack attempting to invade the planet of the same name. Khorne Banelords, terrible Battle Titans, and Daemon Engines destroyed many cities, but a great genius of the Adeptus Mechanicus, Geronimus Undersen, modified an Ordinatus as a means of defence against the enemy war machines. Undersen mounted an enormous spacecraft weapon on a terrestrial chassis, thus giving the Imperial Guard an engine whose power was capable of destroying the most heavily armoured enemies. In the battle that followed for control of Hive Tartarus, Armageddon destroyed no fewer than four Chaos Titans and two detachments of Daemon Engines. Deprived of the firepower of those engines, the Chaos assaults broke against the walls of Hive Tartarus. The Ordinatus Armageddon is feared, and its reputation is that of a machine able to bring down any opposition.

The Powerful Shot of the Nova Cannon has the \textbf{Damage (+3)} and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-5$/2)} abilities. The Diffuse Shot of the Nova Cannon has the \textbf{Damage (+1)} ability. The Ordinatus Armageddon has the \textbf{DR (6+)}, \textbf{Wounds (5)}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (4)} abilities.

\subsection{Ordinatus Golgotha}

After the crushing defeat Ghazghkull Thraka suffered on Armageddon, he set off again towards the stars. His sworn enemy, Commissar Yarrick, pursued him to the world of Golgotha. The Commissar was captured, and in order to humiliate his relentless enemy, Ghazghkull did not kill him, but decided to imprison him. That was without counting on Yarrick's inexhaustible resources; he managed to escape and later returned to Golgotha accompanied by a punitive force. The first combats were unfavourable to the Imperium, whose armies were overwhelmed by the multitude of Orks. In an attempt to crush the immense mobs, the Ordinatus Golgotha was constructed. Golgotha's terrifying Hellfire Missiles devastated the Ork ranks. The latter were routed and driven from the world. Since then, the Ordinatus Golgotha has been seen on every front, in battles fought against enemies in numerical superiority.

This Ordinatus carries 6 Hellfire Missiles and may fire one or two per turn. The Hellfire Missiles use a \textbf{Template (12 cm)} and are \textbf{Artillery}. You may fire only 6 missiles per game. The Ordinatus Golgotha has the \textbf{Wounds (5)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (4)}, and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities.

\subsection{Ordinatus Mars}

During the Horus Heresy, the worlds of the Adeptus Mechanicus were torn by civil war. The rebel armies, led by Magos Solomon Abbadon, turned against the Emperor in order to overwhelm the masters of Mars. From his citadel of Jericho in the mountains, he sent his forces to attack the factories and installations of those who had remained loyal to the Emperor. The Imperium organised reprisals and dispatched an armada to annihilate Abbadon's worshippers. This proved impossible, for the fortress of Jericho was protected by an extraterrestrial energy system discovered during the Great Crusade. The central bastion was built of the hardest metal and could withstand the most violent bombardments. The Ordinatus Mars uses a unique technology to produce a sound wave that reverberates across the whole battlefield. When that vibration struck the walls of fortress Jericho, they began to tremble and fell to dust. The infamous metal tower toppled and collapsed like vulgar paper. Thousands of techno-guards charged through the breach, and Solomon Abbadon was captured and immediately executed for his treachery.

The Ordinatus Mars uses a Sonic Disruptor that uses a \textbf{Template (Flamer)}. Place the template at range and in line of sight of the Ordinatus, with the point of the template directed towards the Ordinatus. Bases are then hit on a 3+ with no save, with the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/2)} abilities. It has the \textbf{Wounds (5)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (4)}, and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities.
