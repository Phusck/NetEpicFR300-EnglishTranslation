\section{Off-Table Artillery}

Off-Table Artillery may represent long-range weapon batteries deployed well away from the combat zone, or orbital or naval artillery. See the rulebook for more details on their use.

A Fragmentation Barrage uses a \textbf{Template (12 cm)} and has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} and \textbf{Off-Table Artillery} abilities.

A Macro Barrage uses a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)} and has the \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/3)}, \textbf{Off-Table Artillery}, and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-2$)} abilities.

A Laser Lance has the \textbf{Damage (+1D3)} and \textbf{Off-Table Artillery} abilities.
