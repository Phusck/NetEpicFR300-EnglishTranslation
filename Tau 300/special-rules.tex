\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Off-Table Artillery and Forward Observers}

The T'au do not use Forward Observers. Instead, the T'au use their Markerlights to guide off-table fire. Off-Table Artillery may only target units that have a Markerlight token. Only one Off-Table Artillery shot may be attempted per Markerlight token per turn.

\subsection{Drones}

Drone formations are Limited Formations. You may take only two of them for each Company Formation in your army. In addition, you may not take more DX6 Remora Fighter formations than other Flyer formations.

\subsection{Ethereal}

\subsubsection{Firestorm [Movement Phase]}

Detachments from this codex of class 1 or 2 on a First Fire order within 12~cm of the Ethereal may reroll their missed To-Hit rolls.

\subsubsection{Zephyr's Grace [Movement Phase]}

Detachments from this codex of class 1 and 2 within 12~cm of the Ethereal gain a bonus of $+5$~cm to their total movement.

\subsubsection{Tide Aura [Movement Phase]}

Detachments from this codex of class 1 and 2 within 12~cm of the Ethereal gain a fixed 6+ save, or if they already have one, improve it by 1.
