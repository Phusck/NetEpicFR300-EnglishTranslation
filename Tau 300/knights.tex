\section{Knights}

\subsection{XV104 Riptide Battlesuit}

The XV104 Riptide is a marvel of the Earth Caste, a heavy Battlesuit capable of annihilating tanks, infantry formations, and fortified positions while dodging or withstanding enemy fire. The Riptide is twice as tall as an XV8 Crisis Battlesuit, but its ease of movement is closer to the latter than to Imperial walkers of a comparable size. Although the Riptide is enormous and fitted with various support systems and weapons, it remains a Battlesuit. Indeed, it is piloted by only a single warrior, who is nonetheless able to unleash precise fire and move with incredible tactical efficiency.

It has the \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Fire on the Move}, and \textbf{Reflex Fire} abilities. Its missiles have the \textbf{Smart Missiles} ability.

\subsection{KV128 Stormsurge Battlesuit}

KV128 Stormsurges are colossi of destruction able to annihilate tank columns or defeat super-heavy walkers with a single salvo. Sent to the front of the war against the Imperium, which continues to grow, these mobile bastions have quickly proved their usefulness against infantry hordes and the enemy's armoured masses. Although slow, a single Stormsurge can reverse the course of a battle in a few seconds thanks to its phenomenal firepower. The Stormsurge is the first of a new series of war machines called ballistic suits. Developed by the famous Earth Caste scientist Fio'o Bork'an Ishu'ron, this enormous bipedal firing platform is a response to the enemy's super-heavy war machines.

It has the \textbf{Wounds (3)} and \textbf{Reflex Fire} abilities. A Stormsurge may fire a maximum of 4 Cluster Rockets, which have the \textbf{Seeker Missiles} and \textbf{Limited Munitions (4)} abilities. Its Pulse Driver Cannon has the \textbf{Damage (+1)} ability. Its Destroyer Missile has the \textbf{Seeker Missiles} ability. Finally it possesses a Smart Missile system.

\subsection{XV107 R'varna Battlesuit}

A development of the Riptide Battlesuit, the T'au XV107 R'varna Battlesuit sacrifices mobility in favour of heavier armour and increased firepower in the form of two Pulse Cannons, experimental weapon systems that fire projectiles capable of saturating the target area in a deadly storm of plasma pulses. The stoic Earth Caste of Ke'lshan was almost forced to abandon its plans for developing the R'varna Battlesuit because of the monumental cost of these new engines of war.

It has the \textbf{Wounds (2)} and \textbf{Fire on the Move} abilities, and its weapon uses a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)} that \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)}.

\subsection{XV109 Y'vahra Battlesuit}

Hastily deployed after the success of the R'varna Battlesuit, the XV109 Y'vahra is a class 10 Battlesuit designed for devastating shock attacks. To facilitate this role, the Y'vahra is equipped with a triple Plasma Cannon capable of vaporising the hardest ceramite, and an electromagnetic pulse cannon designed to disable enemy war machines. The complex network of vectored thrusters built into the Y'vahra's impressive armour allows it to cross the battlefield gracefully, striking the greatest concentration of enemies and taking a heavy toll in lives before flying off.

It has the \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Fire on the Move}, and \textbf{Reflex Fire} abilities. Its flamer uses a \textbf{Flame Template} that \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)}.
