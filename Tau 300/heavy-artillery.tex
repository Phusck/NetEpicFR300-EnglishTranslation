\section{Heavy Artillery}

\subsection{Off-Table Artillery}

It may represent long-range weapon batteries deployed well away from the combat zone or artillery fire coming from vessels in orbit. Off-Table Artillery has a cost in Destruction Points rather than army points. Usually launched from orbit by T'au cruisers, T'au Off-Table Artillery is guided by Markerlights. Even if these shots are not called by Forward Observers, you must pass a communication test of 3+ in order to use Off-Table Artillery. Only one Off-Table Artillery shot may be used on the point concerned, even if the communication test is failed.

Gravitic tracer missiles are launched from gravitic launchers and inflict enormous damage on impact with the target. Gravitic submunition missiles are used by the T'au to suppress heavily entrenched enemy forces.

Gravitic Missiles follow the \textbf{Seeker Missiles} rules and \textbf{Damage (+1)}.

Submunition Missiles have the \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/2)}, \textbf{Seeker Missiles}, \textbf{Template (12 cm)}, and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} abilities.
