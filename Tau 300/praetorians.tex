\section{Praetorians}

\subsection{Moray}

The Moray is a unit recently introduced into T'au armies in order to meet the need for heavy fire support without having to deploy a Manta missile ship. The Moray is quite similar to the Manta in appearance, but is much smaller and cannot transport troops, while remaining a substantial striking force. Its main weapon is either a heavy Railgun, a more powerful version of the Railgun mounted on T'au tanks, or a battery of heavy ion cannons firing in unison. It is a mix between a heavy high-altitude antigrav and a slow aircraft, just like the larger Manta missile ship, and can be considered the T'au answer to enemy super-heavy tanks and reconnaissance Titans.

A Moray has the \textbf{Floating}, \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, and \textbf{Deflector Shields (2)} abilities. It is equipped with Burst Cannons in a turret and Tracer Missiles that have the \textbf{Seeker Missiles} ability. The variant with a Railgun has two firing modes. The first is a powerful shot with \textbf{Damage (+2)}. The second is a submunition shell that uses a \textbf{Template (12 cm)} and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/2)}.

\subsection{Manta}

The Manta is the T'au super-heavy vessel. It is so large that it is in fact a small spacecraft. The Manta is the closest thing to the Titans of the Imperium or the Gargants of the Orks, with comparable firepower. It is well armed and armoured for front-line use, where it is intended to deploy and fight as an attack ship. A Manta has formidable firepower. Its main weapons are two Heavy Railguns, which fire a fin-stabilised shell for greater lift in the atmosphere.

The Manta has the \textbf{Floating}, \textbf{Wounds (8)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Deflector Shields (3)}, and \textbf{Transport (24)} abilities. It is equipped with Burst Cannons in a turret. Its Railgun has two firing modes. The first is a powerful shot with \textbf{Damage (+2)}. The second is a submunition shell that uses a \textbf{Template (12 cm)} and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/2)}. Finally, its Tracer Missiles have the \textbf{Seeker Missiles} ability.
