\section{Titans}

\subsection{Tomb Guardians}

Tomb Guardians resemble giant Canoptek Spyders armed with claws and a larger version of the particle projector. They are the war machines most frequently used by the Necrons. Activated when a necropolis is threatened by Gargants or enemies of Titan class, they remain active until the Tomb World is fully awakened, waiting for a Necron Lord to direct them.

The Tomb Guardian must choose two weapons when the list is created.

The Tomb Guardian has the \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (5)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, \textbf{Reinforced Necrodermis}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{Heavy Antigrav}, and \textbf{Organic Metal (2)} abilities.
