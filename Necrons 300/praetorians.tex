\section{Praetorians}

\subsection{Abattoirs}

The Abattoir is a gigantic structure like a hill, moved onto worlds the Necrons have decided to ``purify''. Dropped from low orbit, it resembles a floating pyramid with thousands of tentacles under its structure. The Abattoir is exceedingly rare, one of the few relics of the war against the Old Ones. An Abattoir in action is a terrifying thing, clawing the ground beneath it with its tentacles and firing energy rays at every living thing. An attack that must be resolved on the head/bridge is resolved on the crystal for the Abattoir.

The Abattoir has the \textbf{Wounds (9)}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Reinforced Necrodermis}, \textbf{Heavy Antigrav}, \textbf{Engine of Destruction}, \textbf{Organic Metal (3)}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, and \textbf{4 Portal (1 class 1 to 2 detachment)} abilities.

Harvesters: the Harvesters may be used in two different ways:

\begin{itemize}
    \item To shoot, in which case they have the \textbf{Turret} and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} abilities.
    \item For assault, in which case each grants the \textbf{Parry} or \textbf{Entangle} ability, $+2$ AF, and \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}. Each also increases the DR by 1.
\end{itemize}

Scarab Swarm: the Abattoir may fire 4 Scarab swarms per turn with the \textbf{Turret} and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} abilities.

\subsection{War Barges}

The War Barge is very rare in Necron armies, used only during massive invasions of a world. The War Barge is a miracle of technology, able to teleport itself and carrying a large Portal, acting as an entry point for a complete Necron army. The existence of War Barges proves that no world is safe from a Necron invasion.

The War Barge has the \textbf{Wounds (5)}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Reinforced Necrodermis}, \textbf{Heavy Antigrav}, \textbf{Organic Metal (3)}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Deep Strike (1)}, \textbf{Dimensional Translocation}, and \textbf{Portal (5 class 1 to 3 detachments)} abilities. The Gauss Cannons have the \textbf{Turret} ability.

\subsection{Aeonic Orbs}

The Aeonic Orb is so rare that its existence is only a rumour. Epitome of Necron technology, it is supposed to contain the power of a star in a confinement chamber, the structure being carried by an antigrav platform. A small opening in the field is enough to send a plasma ray annihilating everything in its path. Scholars can only speculate on the type of targets it was built to destroy.

An attack that must be resolved on the head/bridge is resolved on Fire Control for the Aeonic Orb.

The Aeonic Orb has the \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Reinforced Necrodermis}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Heavy Antigrav}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Engine of Destruction}, \textbf{Aeonic Orb}, and \textbf{Organic Metal (3)} abilities.

The Solar Burner \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} and is mounted on a \textbf{Turret}. Solar Flare -- Light Fire and Powerful Fire have the \textbf{Damage (+2)}, \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-5$/3)}, and \textbf{Disruption} abilities.
