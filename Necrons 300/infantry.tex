\section{Infantry}

\subsection{Cryptek}

Even though they do not belong to the ranks of the nobility, they play an essential role in Necron affairs, for they maintain the strange machines of the Tomb Worlds. Each Cryptek is affiliated to a Conclave specialised in one aspect of their scientific arcana, but all can use incredible forces that less evolved races call ``magic'': slowing time, driving minds to madness, or simply reducing armour to a heap of ashes. Unfortunately the Conclaves fight for prestige and to weaken the others. This behaviour leads Crypteks to fight among themselves during campaigns: in this way, not only can each Cryptek keep an eye on his rivals but also demonstrate the superiority of his own discipline at the heart of battle.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Resurrection Orb}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities. He may use only one of his weapons per turn. In addition he may use one of his powers per turn.

\subsection{Flayed Ones}

The Flayed Ones were Necrons long ago, but they were the victims of the curse of one of the last C'tan, Llandu'gor the Flayer. The curse acts slowly and reveals itself only after millennia, so many Tomb Worlds are now affected. The Flayed Ones suffer from an unquenchable thirst for flesh and blood. They sometimes clothe themselves in scraps of flesh from their last victims in order to hide, a terrifying sight to behold. Incarnation of the madness awaiting every Pharaoh, the Necron nobility detests them --- but recognises their usefulness in breaking enemy lines. Able to sense bloodthirsty opportunities better than any commander, the Flayed Ones are thus the first lines on the battlefield and withdraw into their secret cache only once their macabre feast is finished.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Fear}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, and \textbf{Outcasts} abilities.

\subsection{Hexmark Destroyers}

Hexmark Destroyers were once Deathmarks. Surging from their dimensional oubliettes like predators in ambush, these hunched monsters unleash fatal salvos upon the hostile. Moreover, their independent targeting optics and optimised firing patterns leave their prey no chance.

They have the \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)} and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities. Their Enmitic Disintegrator \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)}.

\subsection{Lychguard}

They are still the guardians of the nobility, supposed incorruptible and dedicated to their task until death, but this became true according to their legend only once their transfer into a Necron body was complete. Lychguard have the same bodily envelope as the nobles and use deadly close-combat weapons, such as two-handed warscythes or hyperphase swords and dispersion shields.

\subsection{Pariahs}

Pariahs, a rare Necron experiment, were created just before the Great Sleep, through the application of biotransference to non-Necrontyr slaves. Thanks to obedience protocols they fought alongside their masters, radiating a supernatural aura that greatly troubled their enemies. Their numbers diminish over time, for Pariahs are unable to self-repair like other Necrons. Each Pariah uses a unique combination of warscythes and energy weapons.

They have the \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, \textbf{Psychic Abomination}, and \textbf{Soulless} abilities.

\subsection{Triarch Praetorians}

They are the lieutenants and heralds of the Necron kings, whose duty is to preserve the Necron dynasties, which is why they chose not to enter the stasis of the Great Sleep. Guardians of the Tomb Worlds and witnesses to what the galaxy has become, the Tyranid threat on Apotheosis forced them to awaken and unite the Necrons once more. The Praetorians choose the appropriate moment to strike, participating only if the fight merits it in their eyes. With the Rod of Covenant they carry, they can surely make the difference.

They have the \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, and \textbf{Jump Packs} abilities.

\subsection{Necron Lords}

Coming from the ranks of the Nemesors and Pharaohs, they are the leaders of Necron forces and last heirs of the Necrontyr. They are filled with ambition, anger, and resentments. Perfect commanders, they strengthen nearby Necrons thanks to advanced equipment such as the Resurrection Orb. Necron Lords have numerous titles that indicate their rank in the nobility as well as the prestige of the Tomb World from which they originate. Some of them embrace the Destroyer cause and are then mounted on the same chassis as them.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Resurrection Orb}, and \textbf{Leader} abilities.

\subsection{Deathmarks}

Hunting their targets well before the battle, they were hunters and assassins before becoming Necrons, their mechanical bodies having perfected them, removing any need to sleep or breathe and granting them eternal vigilance and a cold and deadly precision. In other words, once hunted by Deathmarks, there is no way out.
