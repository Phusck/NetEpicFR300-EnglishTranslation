\section{Knights}

\subsection{C'tan Shards}

C'tan Shards are the remnants of the souls of the magical creatures that created the Necrons. Necron nobles release the Shards reluctantly and only in precise circumstances. A Shard remains under constant surveillance. Should it rebel, turn its attention elsewhere, or should the integrity of its necrodermis prison be compromised, it can be instantly teleported into a stasis chamber where it will languish for several centuries.

Each Shard has the \textbf{Containment Failure}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Psychic Save (2+)}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{Inorganic}, and \textbf{Reinforced Necrodermis} abilities. The available Shards are described below.

\subsection{Deceiver}

The Deceiver is a master of the art of manipulation. He uses charm and subtlety to mask his words, which are a mixture of lies and half-truths. This gift was responsible for the loss of numerous planets, and the fall of great leaders of men. The Deceiver was surely responsible for the war between the Necrontyr and the Old Ones by fanning the hatred these servants felt.

It also has the \textbf{Dark Presence ($-2$, 20~cm)}, \textbf{Dread (0)}, \textbf{Redeployment (1D3)}, and \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault} abilities. The Deceiver may use 1 power among the C'tan Shard powers per turn.

\subsection{Tesseract Vault}

A Necron Tesseract Vault imprisons a Transcendent C'tan within its walls, in order to use its phenomenal powers as a weapon of mass destruction. The C'tan Shard is indeed able to reduce enemies to dust, set the battlefield ablaze, or trigger earthquakes with a simple gesture.

This Shard may use 2 powers per turn among the C'tan Shard powers.

\subsection{Nightbringer}

The Nightbringer is a sadistic god capable of pulverising the stars; he is death incarnate. He delights in inflicting pain and suffering, on the one hand to feed, but also simply because it is permitted to him. Death sits in the depths of his gaze, and his mighty scythe is guilty of the loss of entire civilisations. Having one day invested a spacecraft with part of his power, he destroyed an entire solar system and drank countless souls.

It also has the \textbf{Damage (+3) in assault} and \textbf{DR (5+)} abilities. The Nightbringer may use 1 power among the C'tan Shard powers per turn in addition to \textbf{Deadly Gas}.

\subsection{Transcendent C'tan}

Transcendent C'tan are the most dangerous of their kind. Each of them is an aggregate of several shards, whether a dozen or a thousand of them, whose power far exceeds that of the sum of its components.

It also has the \textbf{World Pain}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, \textbf{Duellist}, \textbf{DR (4+)}, and \textbf{Parry} abilities. The Transcendent C'tan may use 1 power among the C'tan Shard powers per turn.

\subsection{Void Dragon}

Void Dragon Shards fill the air around them with a distorted and lashing sound. With a gesture they can unmake the war machines of inferior species, then draw the thus-disintegrated matter to themselves, like the gravitational well of a black hole, in order to reconstitute their constantly fluctuating physical envelope.

It also has the \textbf{Damage (+2) in assault} and \textbf{Absorption} abilities. The Void Dragon may use 1 power among the C'tan Shard powers per turn in addition to its \textbf{Voltaic Storm} power.

\subsection{Tomb Golems}

They resemble Necron warriors of an antique model and overfly the Necron hordes. Rarely present in Necron raids, they are more often encountered by unlucky explorers who discover a Tomb World. Their oversized weapons are not built like the lances of Imperial knights, having other uses besides the battlefield, probably related to Tomb World maintenance. However, their imposing and dreadful carcass advances slowly toward the enemy, leaving only desolation behind them. Tomb Golems are armed with the Eldritch Cannon, an enormous rifle delivering a short but powerful flaming shot.

The Tomb Golem has the \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{DR (6+)}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.
