\section{Cavalry}

\subsection{Destroyers}

The only reason for the Destroyers' existence is to eliminate life. While other Necrons await the day they will be living again, the Destroyers have fully embraced their mechanical form, fusing their arms with their weapons for better accuracy and exchanging their legs for a hover platform to increase their mobility. Their Gauss Cannon and sophisticated targeting system sow destruction in their wake.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
30 cm & 5+ & +1 & -- & Gauss Cannon & 45 cm & 1 & 4+ & $-1$
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Canoptek Acanthrites}

Canoptek Acanthrites are often encountered at the vanguard of Necron assault spearheads and extermination campaigns, and their numbers vary from a mere handful to vast hosts of these odious automatic locusts, capable of reducing a city to rubble and massacring its inhabitants in a pitiless tide of destruction.

\subsection{Destroyer Lords}

Destroyer Lords are the leaders of Destroyer Phalanxes and owe obedience only to their fierce hatred of all sentient creatures. While Destroyers have ranged weapons of various types, Destroyer Lords reject them and have only a Staff of Light, for they want to see the fear in the eyes of the enemies they kill.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Resurrection Orb}, and \textbf{Leader} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
30 cm & 4+/5+f & +5 & -- & Staff of Light & 30 cm & 2 & 4+ & $-2$
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Canoptek Spyders}

Also known as Tomb Spyders, these are large spider-like robots charged with maintaining Necron complexes. The task would seem impossible without the help of Scarab swarms, small beetle-shaped robots that Canoptek Spyders can generate endlessly to assist them. In combat the Scarabs have another use, supporting other Necron units and engaging nearby enemies. There are two types of Canoptek Spyders, armed differently.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (5+)}, \textbf{Scarab Generator}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.

\subsection{Ophydian Destroyers}

Repugnant to other Necrons, Ophydian Destroyers recall the servile Canoptek Wraiths and the infamous Flayed Ones because of their bodies optimised for murder. Able to make phased movements, they set ambushes to cut their prey to pieces.

\subsection{Tomb Blades}

Tomb Blades count among the few units developed after the creation of the Necrons. This light antigrav is a small Necron jetbike fitted with Tesla weapons and an artificial intelligence to aid the weak reflexes of its Necron pilot. This gives a fast antigrav capable of great accelerations and very tight turns that no living creature could endure.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Hard to Hit}, \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
30 cm & 5+ & +2 & -- & Twin Tesla Carbines & 30 cm & 2 & 4+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Canoptek Wraiths}

Overflying the battlefield, Wraiths do not awaken like other Necrons, these ghostly creatures being in charge of Tomb World maintenance. They can phase partially or totally out of reality, an ability that allows them to repair complex mechanisms without being dismantled, which is a deadly advantage on a battlefield, their weapons passing through most known armours.

They have the \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.
