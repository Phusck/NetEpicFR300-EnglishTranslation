\section{Vehicles}

\subsection{Pentasaur}

These herbivores take their name from the cluster of horns upon their heavily armoured heads. In addition to their bony carapace they have very thick hide, a high tolerance for pain, and a surprising number of redundant internal organs. Combined with a slow nervous system, these traits render them almost insensible to damage. The Exodites mount a twin Maelstrom Laser upon their backs and use them as the equivalent of a tank destroyer.

All Pentasaur weapons have the \textbf{Turret} ability. The Maelstrom Missile Launcher version has the \textbf{Battery}, \textbf{Artillery}, and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} abilities. The Flamer of the Thermal Lance version has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.
