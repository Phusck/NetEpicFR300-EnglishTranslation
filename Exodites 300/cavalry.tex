\section{Cavalry}

\subsection{Baron}

Barons may be the lords of their territory or a member of the lord's family, often a son seeking to prove himself. The Baron does not merely command the army: he owns it. It is composed of his retainers and of the people who dwell upon his lands. The forces at his disposal symbolise his status and his wealth. Barons are armed with a laser lance and equipped with a wraithbone shield.

Barons have the \textbf{Leader}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Lance (5+/AP $-1$)} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
20 cm & 4+/6+f & +6 & Attached & Shuriken Pistol & 20 cm & 2 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Dragon Knights}

Dragon Knights are the most common troops in a Baron's service. They are generally the young members of the clan whose peacetime role is to watch over the herds. In battle they ride the most common and easiest mount, for they are not trained to other steeds. Dragon Knights are armed with a laser lance and a wraithbone shield.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability.

\begin{unitstatstable}
20 cm & -- & +2 & 6 & Shard Carbine and Lance & 45 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Lethosaur Knights}

Lethosaurs are smaller than Dragons, with chameleon-like skin. They are ridden by scouts who need to close with the enemy while avoiding direct contact.

They have the \textbf{Scout} and \textbf{Infiltration} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
25 cm & -- & +1 & 6 & Plasma Carbine & 30 cm & 1 & 4+ & $-1$
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Pterosaur Knights}

Pterosaurs are the only truly flying creatures on most Exodite worlds. Pterosaur Knights are used as reconnaissance troops when other scouts are hindered by the terrain. Their principal role is to swoop down upon the enemy and bring support where it is needed.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav} and \textbf{Deep Strike (2)} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
25 cm & -- & +2 & 6 & Shard Carbine & 45 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Raptor Knights}

Raptors are extremely agile insectivorous bipeds, large enough for a rider but of slight build. They are the most common scouts of Exodite armies and are also used as combatants thanks to their excellent reflexes and vicious temperament.

They have the \textbf{Scout} and \textbf{Infiltration} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
25 cm & -- & +3 & 6 & Sword and Pistol & 20 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Dragoons}

Dragoons are formed from the finest soldiers of the Baron's household. They are most commonly employed to break enemy lines, but they are equally formidable in shooting with their plasma carbines, which can pierce armour. Dragoons are armed with a laser lance and a wraithbone shield.

Dragoons have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Lance (5+/AP 0)} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
20 cm & 5+ & +4 & 5 & Pistol and Lance & 20 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0

\subsection{Salamander}

The fire-breathing Salamander holds great importance in Exodite myth, having much in common with the traditional dragons of Eldar legend. Their breath has enormous destructive power, immolating unwary targets.

It has the \textbf{Infiltration} ability. Its weapon has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

15 cm & -- & +1 & 6 & Fire Breath & 45 cm & 2 & 4+ & 0

\subsection{Visionary}

Exodite Visionaries have the powers of the spirits at their disposal and are among the Psykers of greatest potential in the galaxy. Because their race is in phase with psychic energies and respectful of the spirits of the dead, they are feared by other Eldar. Visionaries wear spirit armour fashioned from the wraithbone of the world-spirit itself.

Visionaries have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)} abilities. In addition, a Visionary may use one of its powers each turn.

\begin{unitstatstable}
20 cm & 6+f & +4 & Attached & Shuriken Pistol & 20 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Vyper Transport}

The Vyper Transport is an adaptation of the Vyper created for the Exodites' needs, imitating the Venom transport used by the Harlequins. The shuriken cannon has been replaced by an open transport deck, turning it into a light transport.

The Vyper Transport has the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Transport (1)}, \textbf{Attached Transport}, and \textbf{Open-Topped} abilities.

35 cm & 6+ & +1 & Attached & Shuriken Catapult & 20 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}
