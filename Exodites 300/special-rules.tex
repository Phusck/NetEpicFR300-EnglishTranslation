\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Scout}

If the Exodites form your primary army, choose one of the following abilities at the start of the battle, before objectives are placed.

\begin{itemize}
    \item For every complete 3 detachments with the \textit{Scout} ability, the Exodite player chooses one Class 1 to 3 detachment that gains the \textit{Infiltration} ability (the chosen detachments need not themselves have the \textit{Scout} ability).

    In addition, the player gains 2 orders in reserve as if the army had a detachment with the \textit{Master Strategist} ability.

    \item Exodite Class 1 and 2 bases gain the \textit{Camouflage} ability for turn 1 only.

    \item The Exodite player may place one forest for every complete 2 detachments with the \textit{Scout} ability, to a maximum of 3 forest areas, at the same time as fortifications. Each feature may measure no more than $10 \times 10$~cm and may be placed anywhere on the battlefield except on another terrain feature.

    \item For every complete 2 detachments with the \textit{Scout} ability, the Exodite player chooses one enemy Class 1 to 4 detachment, which must receive an Advance order on turn 1.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Forester}

Exodites suffer no movement penalty when they move through forests.
