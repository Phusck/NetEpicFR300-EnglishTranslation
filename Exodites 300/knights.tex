\section{Knights}

\subsection{Carnosaur}

It is the fiercest of all the Dragons ridden by the Exodites. The Carnosaur can reach 10 metres in height and is a terrifying spectacle to behold. It is a predator: it stalks its prey and kills without mercy. The beast walks upon its powerful hind legs and uses its long tail for balance, which means it can move at astonishing speed for its size. The Carnosaur has a large reptilian head, and its long teeth are sharp enough to tear easily through flesh and bone. They are used to spread terror through the enemy ranks; ridden by two brave warriors, they charge in, causing carnage and terrible destruction.

Carnosaurs have the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Fear}, and \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault} abilities.

\subsection{Bright Knights}

These Knights resemble great horses with a humanoid torso in place of the head. They exist in two versions. The Bright Stalker is equipped with a Bright Lance and a Pulse Laser.

The Bright Stallion has the \textbf{Lance (3+/AP $-2$)} ability. Both versions have the \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{DR (6+)}, and \textbf{Holo-Field} abilities.

\subsection{Fire Knights}

These are bipedal Knights with two arms and various weapons. The Fire Knight exists in three versions. The Fire Gale is equipped with two Bright Lances, while the Fire Reaper uses a Reaper Laser and a Scatter Laser, and the Fire Storm is equipped with a Missile Launcher.

The Fire Storm's Missile Launcher has the \textbf{Artillery} ability. All versions have the \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{DR (6+)}, and \textbf{Holo-Field} abilities.
