\section{Infantry}

\subsection{Fusiliers}

Fusiliers are the troops employed by the Exodites. In civilian life they dwell upon the baron's lands and must repay him by fighting in his army whenever he deems it necessary.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration} ability.

\subsection{Scouts}

Scouts are the Exodites' standard scouts. They are formidable snipers and are quick to delay enemy armies with exemplary efficiency.

Scouts are a remarkable asset for the Exodites.

They have the \textbf{Scout}, \textbf{Free Deployment}, \textbf{Sniper}, and \textbf{Camouflage} abilities.

\subsection{Travois}

An Exodite Travois is an anti-grav platform drawn by a dragon. It is used for a single purpose: to deploy the weapon rapidly, fire, and then move once more.

All Travois have the \textbf{Fire on the Move} ability. In addition, the Travois with a Starcannon Battery has the \textbf{AA} ability, and the Travois with a Missile Launcher is Artillery and has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} ability. Travois cannot be transported.
