\subsection{Shining Spears}

In Aeldari mythology, Kaela Mensha Khaine carried a great spear with which he could kill any enemy with a single blow. The Aspect Warriors of the Shining Spears temple have chosen this weapon as their source of inspiration. They are the only Asuryani Aspect Warriors to ride Jetbikes, which of course allows them to strike with the speed and ferocity of the serpent. They are armed with a long and powerful Laser Lance capable of seriously weakening the enemy before engaging it in assault.

Shining Spears have the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Lance (4+/AP $-1$)}, and \textbf{Elite (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Jetbikes}

The Guardians who pilot Aeldari Jetbikes are called Wind Riders. Their machines are so formidable that a skilled rider can cover several hundred metres in a few heartbeats before shredding his enemies with the pair of Shuriken Catapults that give the Jetbike its deadly reputation. Wind Riders take pride in their mastery of these impressive craft. The fairing of each Jetbike thus bears the heraldry of its Craftworld, sometimes with a place of honour for its squadron's blazon. The colours used are always bright and provocative.

They have the \textbf{Antigrav} ability.

\subsection{Vyper}

A military innovation of the artisans of Saim-Hann, Vypers are two-seater attack craft upon which various heavy weapons can be mounted. Their small size allows Vypers to pass through every Webway tunnel except the narrowest, and their prodigious armament lets them rival many tanks in firepower. The craft's speed alone gives it a viability superior to that of thick armour. That is why Vyper squadrons always move at a vertiginous pace when the fighting rages.

Vypers have the \textbf{Antigrav} ability.
