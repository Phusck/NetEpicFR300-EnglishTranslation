\section{The Swordwind}

The Aeldari of Biel-Tan --- the most warlike of the Craftworlds --- are consumed by bitterness. Despite their depleted ranks and their broken vessel, they continue their campaign of xenocide against every lesser race of the galaxy.

Of all the Aeldari, it is those of Biel-Tan who struggle most fiercely to recover their glory from before the Fall. In its quest to restore its race's galactic dominion, the people of Biel-Tan place a greater importance on the Warrior Path than the other Craftworlds. These Aeldari know that a new empire can be forged only in the crucible of war, strengthened on the anvil of combat and quenched in blood. As a result, Exarchs are far more numerous on Biel-Tan than elsewhere. The most dangerous among them form the Court of the Young King. The duties of this elite unit include the awakening of the Avatar, who rests at the heart of the Craftworld. Led by the incarnation of Khaine, the Aspect Warriors of Biel-Tan fight as if possessed.

Having long since abandoned the concepts of tolerance and mercy, their barely contained rage drives them to massacre anything that stands in their way.

Biel-Tan's war host is called the Bahzhakhain, which means the ``Swordwind'', but also the ``storm of blades'' or ``frozen leaves falling under the scythe'', depending on the pronunciation. The Swordwind strikes quickly and surely. Its tactics rest on a brutal, concentrated attack led by the Aspect Warriors, aimed at annihilating the opponent in a single blow. Its enemies are often defeated before they even realise they are under attack.

Long before the Fall, the Aeldari fertilised arid worlds so that in time they would become flourishing paradises. The Exodites colonised some of these planets, but the majority remained virgin. The inhabitants of Biel-Tan consider these worlds an essential link with their past and a vital source of resources: when the time comes to reclaim their inheritance, the Aeldari reconquest will set out from the paradisiacal Maiden Worlds and the Exodite planets.

Biel-Tan therefore regards even the slightest colonisation of these worlds by other races as a threat to the restoration of the Aeldari empire. Their prodigious ire is most often directed at the Orks, whose prolific, destructive, and invasive nature represents a grave threat to these paradisiacal worlds. Likewise, Imperial explorers sometimes land on one of these planets, and their successors often find of them only disjointed bones cleaned by the local predators.

It was during the defence of one of these Maiden Worlds that Biel-Tan was struck full force. The forces of Slaanesh, led by the Daemon Herald called the Masque, forced the Swordwind to fight on Ursulia, where an immense daemonic army awaited them. The Masque's ruses and artifices broke the seals of a Webway portal leading into Biel-Tan, and a daemonic horde struck at the heart of the Craftworld. There, Slaanesh's herald infiltrated the Infinity Circuit in order to contaminate the ancestral souls of the Craftworld. The daemonic invaders were destroyed only thanks to the intervention, in extremis, of the Ynnari, who used the dead of the Infinity Circuit to awaken Ynnead, the Whispering God.

The Craftworld is now broken. Some parts are still scorched and desolate, and its solar sails hang in tatters. The Bonesingers work tirelessly to keep the Craftworld running, and the ghost halls swell with the new wraithbone constructs animated by the souls of those the Infinity Circuit can no longer protect. Nearly half of Biel-Tan's inhabitants are gone: those who did not die have joined the ranks of the Ynnari, drawing the wrath of those who blame Yvraine for the catastrophe that has befallen the Craftworld. Those who remain cling more than ever to their orthodox methods, dedicating themselves with vigour to exterminating their enemies and establishing a new Aeldari civilisation that will rival their ancient empire.

The fate of Craftworld Biel-Tan was intimately bound to the precarious rise of the Ynnari. Since the crack in the wraithbone endoskeleton that formed its Infinity Circuit, the moon-sized Aeldari Craftworld had gradually fragmented. Biel-Tan now resembled a fleet more than a single vessel, its strength almost halved by the turmoil born on the paradisiacal but corrupted world of Ursulia. These Aeldari had plunged into civil war because of the dissensions Yvraine had aroused --- for a great part of those who had turned away from Biel-Tan had followed her to become Ynnari, aggravating the Craftworld's already critical situation.

Self-confidence, a quality the surviving Asuryani regarded as the greatest virtue, brought them salvation. The people of Biel-Tan, steeped in optimism, believed that the Aeldari empire would rise like a phoenix from its ashes after coming so close to extinction. Biel-Tan would soon show its strength and its capacity to spread its power across a domain even vaster than before.

During the invasion of the trio of Exodite worlds called the Three Sisters by the forces of Chaos, Biel-Tan went to war, led by three legendary Phoenix Lords: Baharroth, Fuegan, and Karandras. Baharroth and Fuegan received a warm welcome, for the arrival of a disciple of Asuryan always gave rise to great rejoicing, let alone two. But when Karandras appeared on Khazhar and scythed down a great part of the daemonic cavalry, a wave of unease spoiled the Asuryani's joy. According to prophecy, at the moment of the Rhana Dandra --- that final apocalyptic battle against Chaos that would bring the ruin of all --- the six Phoenix Lords would fight together. Would the three become six, a sign that the day of judgement was near?
