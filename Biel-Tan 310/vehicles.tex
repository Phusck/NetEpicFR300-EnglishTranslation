\section{Vehicles}

\subsection{Falcon}

The Falcon is the keystone of Asuryani armoured warfare. Supported by Fire Prisms, Firestorms, and Night Spinners, an armoured host first attacks at high speed to outflank and then encircle an enemy force. Striking at full speed and withdrawing before the enemy's anti-tank weapons can be deployed to face them, a flight of Falcons, sometimes called a Cloudstrike squadron, can quickly change position and strike again from another direction, breaking the enemy's defensive cohesion or its attack formations before the main Asuryani assault strikes.

Falcons have the \textbf{Antigrav} and \textbf{Transport (2)} abilities. Their Bright Lance has the \textbf{Turret} ability.

\subsection{Firestorm}

The Firestorm is the Asuryani's standard anti-aircraft vehicle, using the Falcon hull. It carries a complex set of three-barrel lasers in a single-seat turret that floods the sky above a grav-tank formation with laser fire.

The Firestorm has the \textbf{Antigrav} ability. Its Laser Battery has the \textbf{AA} ability.

\subsection{Crimson Hunter}

The Crimson Hunter was designed specifically to hunt and destroy aircraft of all types, including those of the Drukhari. The pilots train relentlessly, for they know that a single beam can make the difference between victory and defeat, despite their lightning reflexes.

\subsection{Hornet}

The Hornet is a small single-seat attack vehicle deployed by Aeldari armoured forces in a reconnaissance and rapid-raid role. It is found at the vanguard of Aeldari armoured attacks, preceding the main force of Falcons and Wave Serpents, often alongside other high-speed attack craft such as Jetbikes and Vypers. A versatile vehicle, the Hornet can be equipped to engage any target effectively.

\subsection{Lynx}

The Lynx is an elegant, heavily armoured craft, almost invisible to the Imperium's arcane detection devices, and it is the embodiment of the ancient Aeldari's advanced technological skills. Previously unknown until the appearance of Craftworld Mymeara's warhost on Betalis III, the Lynx is a Heavy Grav-Tank, superficially similar to the Scorpion, but which some within the Adeptus Mechanicus who have been authorised to access images of the craft by certain unorthodox elements of the Inquisition believe to be of an older design.

The Lynx has the \textbf{Antigrav} ability.

\subsection{Night Spinner}

The Night Spinner stands apart from all other artillery vehicles, for it fires neither explosive shells nor even dreadful energy discharges, but uses a far more subtle means to deliver death at a distance. The Night Spinner thus possesses a turret-mounted Doomweaver, an esoteric weapon that weaves deadly monofilament webs before projecting them. Anyone unlucky enough to find himself under a Night Spinner's web will be shredded by the monofilaments as gravity draws them gently downward. The web will eventually touch the ground, but before that it will form a deadly barrier that will dismember all who try to pass through it.

The Night Spinner has the \textbf{Antigrav} ability. Its Doomweaver has the \textbf{Web}, \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)}, and \textbf{Artillery} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
25 cm & 3+ & +1 & 6 & Doomweaver & 90 cm & 2 & 4+ & $-2$

\subsection{Fire Prism}

Despite its undeniable aesthetic, the Fire Prism neglects none of the murderous qualities associated with armour. Its distinctive weapon, the Prism Cannon, is the bane of the heavy tanks of less advanced races. This unique offensive system uses a two-stage firing process. A medium-power laser directs its beam into a crystal prism that amplifies the discharge in a fraction of a second. This energy can in turn be released as a concentrated ray capable of piercing a battle tank from side to side, or in diffuse form to annihilate entire infantry units. The most unusual use of this technological marvel, however, is to reduce the width of its laser discharge to a thin streak of light, whose concentrated energy is capable of paralysing a battle tank with a single shot.

The Fire Prism has the \textbf{Antigrav} ability. Its Prism Cannon has \textbf{Damage (+1)} and the \textbf{Fire Prism} ability.

\begin{unitstatstable}
25 cm & 3+ & +1 & 6 & Prism Cannon & 75 cm & 1 & 3+ & $-2$
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Nightwing}

The Asuryani front-line fighter is the dreaded Nightwing. Hated by all their enemies, it is an elegant and deadly craft, capable of outmatching almost every other aircraft in the sky. The Nightwing is the apex of Asuryani aeronautical technology, a superbly designed fighter-interceptor that gives Craftworld forces and Aeldari Corsair raids a decisive advantage in aerial combat. Using the Asuryani's sophisticated anti-grav technology, the Nightwing is capable of aerobatic feats that most Imperial Navy pilots consider impossible.

The Nightwing has the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Dodge (5+)}, \textbf{Interceptor}, and \textbf{Flyer} abilities.

\subsection{Unicorn}

Striking swiftly at the heart of the enemy ranks is the very essence of Eldar warfare. It is to that end that the Unicorn was created as a direct answer to the slowness of the Vibro Cannon. The Unicorn is a modified version of a Falcon with a Vibro Cannon mounted on its main axis.

The Unicorn has the \textbf{Antigrav} ability. Its Vibro Cannon has the \textbf{Vibro Cannon ($-2$)} ability.

\subsection{Wave Serpent}

The Wave Serpent is the principal troop transport of a Craftworld's armies. An elegant anti-gravity vehicle based on the Falcon chassis, the Wave Serpent sacrifices the Falcon's firepower for increased resilience. Its anti-grav motors are powerful enough for it to cross the battlefield in a few moments and, combined with the wide array of its heavy armament, the Wave Serpent becomes a magnificent asset on every theatre of operations. Operating alongside Falcons, flights of Wave Serpents rapidly transport squadrons of Aspect Warriors across the battlefield, well protected by their armoured hull and behind sophisticated energy fields.

The Wave Serpent has the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Attached Transport}, \textbf{Protection (4+) in the forward arc}, and \textbf{Transport (2)} abilities.

25 cm & 3+ & +1 & Attached & Pulse Laser & 45 cm & 1 & 4+ & $-1$

\subsection{Warp Hunter}

The rarest of the Falcon modifications ever encountered by the Imperium's fighting forces is the Warp Hunter, a high-speed assault craft that mounts a large D-Cannon through its hull. Like the Fire Prism and the Night Spinner, the Warp Hunter sacrifices its transport capacity for its change of armament. Among the Asuryani, the Warp Hunter is considered a rather archaic vehicle and, given the nature of its weapons, some Craftworlds seem reluctant to deploy a large number of them.

The Warp Hunter has the \textbf{Antigrav} ability. Its D-Cannon has the \textbf{Psychic Attack} and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-3$/2)} abilities.

25 cm & 3+ & +1 & 6 & D-Cannon & 75 cm & 1 & 4+ & Psi
\end{unitstatstable}
