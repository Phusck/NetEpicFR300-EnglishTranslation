\section{Titans}

\subsection{Warlock Titan}

A Warlock Titan contains the spirits of the powerful Asuryani Farseers and Seers, still preserved in its wraithbone core. These spirits of the dead are not powerless, and they can still fuse their psychic strength, creating a powerful war machine that combines the firepower and grace of the Phantom with the innate abilities of these leaders of the Aeldari race, to create the summit of psychic engineering.

This powerful psychic ability combined with the agility of Asuryani Titans and their sophisticated armament places the Warlock Titan at the apex of Aeldari achievement in the field of psychic engineering.

The Warlock Titan is equipped with two Wings and two Arm Weapons. The Warlock Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Holo-Field}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Prescience (12 cm)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Psychic Save (3+)} abilities. It may use one of its psychic powers per turn.

\subsection{Phantom Titan}

The Phantom is the largest of the Asuryani Titans. Tall, slender, and graceful, it is agile and fast compared with the armoured behemoths of the Imperium. Each of these powerful Titans is the apex of Aeldari war-art, built around a powerful psychic wraithbone core that allows the spirits it contains to circulate freely through the Titan, helping to guide the machine and its Steersman. The Steersman is an experienced master of his art. He controls the Titan from his cockpit, 25 metres above the battlefield. Locked in a state of deep trance, his mind blends with those of the wraithbone to create a single sentience, which in turn guides the Titan, allowing it almost to think, react, and manoeuvre at the speed of an Aspect Warrior. The great vanes at the rear of the Phantom project the Holo-Fields that surround it.

A Phantom Titan is equipped with two arm weapons and two Wings. The Phantom Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Holo-Field}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Agile} abilities.

\subsection{Revenant Titan}

The smallest of the Asuryani Titans is the Revenant. Built with a concern for grace and beauty compared with the more functional machines of the Imperium's Titan Legions, all Asuryani Titans are tall, slender, and graceful in their movements (by the standards of so imposing a war machine), which allows them to move rapidly and smoothly through the battle. To improve its combat speed, the Revenant is also equipped with advanced jump jets and gravitic motors that allow it to leap, disengaging from difficult terrain and advancing rapidly to harass the enemy, before withdrawing at the same speed.

The Revenant Titan has a fixed armament. Its two Pulsars may use two firing modes. In Concentrated mode, the Pulsar inflicts Multiple Hits (1D3) on a single target; it may instead make a Diffuse shot.

The Revenant Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Holo-Field}, \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Extended Coherency (25 cm)}, and \textbf{Agile} abilities.
