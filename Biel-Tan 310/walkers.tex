\section{Walkers}

\subsection{War Walkers}

The Asuryani use many forms of bipedal war machines. The smallest and lightest are designated War Walkers. These single-seat walkers resemble the Sentinels of the Imperial Guard, but they are much more agile, for the machine's sophisticated gyroscopic stabilisation and articulated feet allow it to cross the most broken terrain rapidly. They also have more firepower with which to destroy the enemy. The role of these machines is to conduct aggressive reconnaissance. Once the enemy is located, the War Walkers will hasten to pin it in place.

\subsection{Wasp Assault Walkers}

At once mobile and fast, yet endowed with excellent firepower, they are deployed with Corsair squadrons. To ease operations with the Corsairs' mobile aerial troops, the latter commonly use an adaptation of the standard war walker, fitted with jump jets. This allows the Walker to be deployed directly onto its target from a Vampire Raider flying at high altitude, falling to the ground among the other Corsairs. Once on the ground, it can still fire its jump jets to cross the battlefield in short bounds.

Wasp Assault Walkers have the \textbf{Jump Packs} ability.

\subsection{Wraithlord}

Wraithlords are agile and powerful beings that outclass their Wraithguard cousins in size. These colossal chassis are the jewel of their Craftworld, for they enjoy a resistance superior to adamantium thanks to the wraithbone of which they are made. Awakened by the Spiritseers' ritual processes, only a true hero of the Aeldari race has a spirit strong enough to inhabit the imposing hull of a Wraithlord.

Wraithlords exist in two versions: Support and Assault. The Assault version has the \textbf{Dodge (5+)} ability. Both versions have the \textbf{Spirit Stone} ability.
