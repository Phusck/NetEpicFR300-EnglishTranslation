\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Canoptek Tomb Sentinels}

Canoptek Tomb Stalkers and Canoptek Tomb Sentinels are enormous worm-like machines, using the same phasing technology as the Wraiths, but the similarities stop there. Their only role is to hunt and destroy invaders before they discover a buried necropolis. They travel underground even through the hardest layers of rock and strike as soon as they sense life. When a Tomb World is awakened, they remain a tool of choice for the Pharaoh to break enemy lines.

Canoptek Tomb Sentinels have the \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Deep Strike (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Monoliths}

Monoliths are both the first and the most frightening signs of a Necron invasion. These enormous pyramid-shaped war machines are far more dangerous than they appear, for their Portal allows Necron legions to exit.

Monoliths have the \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Heavy Antigrav}, \textbf{Portal (2 class 1 detachments)}, and \textbf{Deep Strike (1)} abilities. The Particle Whip, a Turret weapon, has two firing modes and may use only one per turn. The Gauss Flayers have the \textbf{Turret} ability.

\subsection{Seraptek Heavy Constructs}

Armed with formidable heavy weapons, the Seraptek can unleash energies capable of disintegrating both organic and mechanical matter. The rare people of the Imperium who have survived an encounter with a Seraptek have testified to its unnatural speed and agility. No one knows what this colossus of war was originally for, although few can deny the devastation it causes once on the battlefield.

It has the \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities. Its weapons have the \textbf{Turret} ability.

\subsection{Pylons}

These large defence platforms are scattered across the galaxy and resemble abandoned ruins or remain buried underground until they are activated. When they are, the planet is already ready for the harvest, cut off from any outside help, the pylons being the ultimate anti-aircraft units, capable of bringing down even vessels in low orbit.

They have the \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, and \textbf{Deep Strike (1)} abilities. One firing mode must be chosen for its Particle Accelerator, which has the \textbf{Damage (+1)} ability, and the AA mode has the \textbf{AA} ability.

\subsection{Obelisks}

Obelisks are strange constructions on a square base sharing the same crystal matrix as the Monoliths. The principal role of Obelisks is anti-aircraft defence. They create around them a zone of gravitic disturbance, making the passage of aircraft very dangerous; many crash in the vicinity of such constructions.

Obelisks have the \textbf{Heavy Antigrav}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Anti-Gravitic Matrix}, and \textbf{Deep Strike (1)} abilities.

The Tesla Sphere is on a Turret.
