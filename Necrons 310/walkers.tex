\section{Walkers}

\subsection{Triarch Stalkers}

Resembling gigantic arachnids, their multiple legs allow them to cover every type of terrain without having to slow like the clumsy walkers and tanks used by inferior races. The Stalkers have firepower that lets them negate enemy armour and are fast enough to withdraw before any reaction from the infantry.

They have the \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, \textbf{Targeting Relay}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities. Their Heat Ray \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)}.

\begin{unitstatstable}
20 cm & 3+/6+f & +3 & -- & Heat Ray & 45 cm & 1 & 4+ & $-2$
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Canoptek Doomstalkers}

Canoptek Doomstalkers advance with an unreal grace. Whether they pace the arsenals of their masters as tireless sentinels, or provide mobile fire support to Necron legions, the Doomstalkers annihilate whoever dares oppose them with the burning discharges of their doomsday blasters.

They have the \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, \textbf{Reflex Fire}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.

\subsection{Canoptek Reanimators}

Forcing their way across the battlefield, Canoptek Reanimators are called to repair the damage inflicted on a Tomb World during an enemy attack. Their Gauss Atomisers emit nano-scarabs that can repair Necrons. Organic enemies have less luck, groaning in agony as they are atomically torn apart and recombined in a hideous and bloody manner.

They have the \textbf{Reanimator}, \textbf{Reanimation Protocols (6+)}, \textbf{Reflex Fire}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.
