\section{Vehicles}

\subsection{Doomsday Arks}

Fitted with anti-infantry defence, the Doomsday Ark is not especially resilient. However, it perfectly embodies Necron battle philosophy (destroy the enemy before it has been able to do anything) thanks to its Doomsday Cannon, the most powerful weapon in the Necron arsenal except for legendary artefacts such as the Aeonic Orb.

The Doomsday Ark has the \textbf{Inorganic} and \textbf{Antigrav} abilities. The Doomsday Cannon has the \textbf{Doomsday Cannon} ability; its Diffuse mode uses a Template (7.5~cm) and Concentrated mode has the \textbf{Damage (+1)} ability.

\subsection{Ghost Arks}

Ghost Arks do not merely transport Necron warriors. Although their firepower is particularly sought after, these antigrav vehicles also act as mobile repair platforms, further reinforcing the threat posed by an almost indestructible infantry.

Ghost Arks may transport only Necron Warriors or Immortals and have the \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Transport (2)}, \textbf{Attached Transport}, and \textbf{Repair Platform} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}

\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Catacomb Command Barges}

Catacomb Command Barges carry Necron commanders with the principal interest of bringing them to the heart of the fighting. The Necrons do not believe in delegating responsibility; an assault must be led personally not only to ensure that the battle plan unfolds well but also to impress political rivals. Command Barges allow both a clear view from a retractable assault ramp and the commander to join the melee if he so wishes.

The Catacomb Command Barge has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Leader}, \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Resurrection Orb}, and \textbf{Inorganic} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
30 cm & 3+/5+f & +5 & -- & Tesla Cannon & 45 cm & 1 & 4+ & $-1$
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Doom Scythes}

They generally take off from Necron spacecraft floating in low orbit, but they can also emerge from a complex hidden underground on the Tomb World. They are equipped with a wide variety of anti-infantry and anti-tank weapons, but neither missiles nor guns firing conventional shells, only pure energy. Needing neither ammunition and piloted by a tireless crew, Doom Scythes can patrol for very long periods.

They have the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Dodge (5+)}, \textbf{Interceptor}, \textbf{Inorganic}, and \textbf{Flyer} abilities.

\subsection{Night Scythes}

Night Scythes resemble Doom Scythes closely enough, but their role is different. They replace part of their armament with an Invasion Beams device, allowing a Necron attack force to arrive in the midst of enemy troops from a dimensional pocket, an original Necron strategy to silence anti-aircraft batteries.

They have the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Dodge (5+)}, \textbf{Inorganic}, \textbf{Interceptor}, \textbf{Invasion Beams}, and \textbf{Flyer} abilities.

\subsection{Night Shrouds}

The Night Shroud is a bomber far more imposing than the Night Scythes or Doom Scythes alongside which it operates. Its origins go back to the very ancient battles of the War in Heaven. The Night Shroud carries an antique relic called a Death Sphere containing vessels imprisoning antimatter, allowing it to carry out a bombardment.

It has the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Inorganic}, and \textbf{Flyer} abilities, and its Death Spheres have the \textbf{Bombing (1)} and Template (7.5~cm) abilities.
