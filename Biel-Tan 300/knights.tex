\section{Knights}

\subsection{Avatar}

The Avatar is the living incarnation of war and awakens in times of conflict to aid the armies of the Asuryani against their enemies. Imbued with the spirit of the Bloody-Handed God, the formidable Aeldari God of War, the Avatar dominates the battlefield; it is a true mastodon of death, three times taller than a man, clad in a molten armour of baroque and ornamental style. In one hand it holds a flaming blade inlaid with powerful runes of death, while from the other a hooked fist constantly drips blood, a sinister reminder of Khaine's inextinguishable thirst for carnage and bloodshed.

It has the \textbf{Character}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Dodge (3+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Terror} abilities.

\subsection{Baron}

The baron is the lord of his world and often commands his army in battle. Barons are mounted in ancient destroyer armours, elaborately decorated with runes and banners. Their great experience makes them masters of their graceful giant war machine. They are formidable fighters in close combat.

Both Baron variants have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{DR (6+)}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Holo-Field} abilities. In addition the Baron Stallion has the \textbf{Lance (3+/AP $-2$)} ability.

\subsection{Wraithknight}

Several times the size of all reincarnated soldiers, Wraithlords included, the gigantic Wraithknights are agile enough to run through the ruins of a devastated city, leaping from pillar to pillar while hurling the Aeldari's enemies into oblivion with their esoteric armament. Each Wraithknight wields either a pair of Heavy Wraithcannons for the Support version, whose template is able to propel their targets entirely into the Warp, or a Ghostglaive for the Assault version.

The Assault version has the \textbf{Dodge (5+)} and \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault} abilities. Both versions have the \textbf{Wounds (2)} and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities.
