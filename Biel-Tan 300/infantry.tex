\subsection{Warlock}

These individuals walk the Path of the Seer with the most aggressive and warlike purpose possible. In battle the Warlocks gather in conclaves to concentrate their considerable power, or join an Aeldari unit to lead it to the front.

A Warlock has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Charismatic (12 cm)}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Prescience (7.5 cm)}, and \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)} abilities. The Warlock may use one of his powers each turn.

\subsection{Swooping Hawks}

The Swooping Hawks take their name from the formidable birds of prey of Aeldari myth that symbolise vengeance. In antiquity, the Aeldari believed that the soul of a murdered person could pass into a hawk, and that this bird could find the killer and pursue him so that all might be made aware of his crime. Swooping Hawks are the most mobile of all Aspect Warriors because they can fly. Their long wings are composed of feather-shaped lift plates and an anti-gravity jet. When they fly, their wings vibrate at such a speed that their colour becomes impossible to discern, and they emit a characteristic whistle. Their costume is the colour of the sky: this may be light or dark blue, or even dark grey or black, always with bands of strongly contrasting colours.

Swooping Hawks have the \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Hard to Hit}, \textbf{Elite (2)}, and \textbf{Jump Packs} abilities.

\subsection{Warp Spiders}

The Warp Spiders take their name from the tiny creatures that live inside the Dome of Crystal Seers among the wraithbone structures that form the heart and soul of the Craftworlds. These microscopic creatures can move anywhere in the Craftworlds, blending their bodies with the Infinity Circuits and rematerialising in another. They have mutated from the famous psycho-plastic of which the Craftworlds are made and have thus been able to develop in harmony with Aeldari civilisation, moving within the Infinity Circuits like the spirits of deceased Asuryani. In theory, a Chaos Daemon could find its way inside the wraithbone network, but its presence would be spotted at once. The tiny Warp Spiders make sure that does not happen by hunting down and destroying any fragment of foreign psychic energy. They function a little like white blood cells, neutralizing bacteria inside the body.

The Warp Spiders' weapons are deadly rapid-firing Death Spinners, particularly devastating. Warp Spiders have the \textbf{Elite (2)}, \textbf{Lightning Attack}, \textbf{Cannot Be Blocked}, and \textbf{Fire on the Move} abilities.

\subsection{Harlequins}

The Harlequins run, leap, and whirl, the blades and shots of their terrified enemies hissing around them to derisory effect. Preferring speed and skill to strength and resilience, the Harlequins wear holo-suits that fracture their outlines into a polychromatic blur, giving them the appearance of an intangible psychedelic tornado into which the enemy pours its fire in vain. But once they close, they prove they are no mirage.

\subsection{Autarch}

There is a path upon the Warrior Path that few take. The Aeldari expression for it can be translated as the Path of the Strategist or the Path of Command, and those who travel far enough along it receive the title of Autarch. Within a race that grows more warlike as the gulf of extinction draws nearer, the ability to coordinate the actions of the Craftworld's warhosts, fleets, and fighter squadrons is paramount. Such is the Autarch's mission: to master the myriad Asuryani tactics, and lead his kindred to victory.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Character}, \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Elite (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Howling Banshees}

Unlike most other Aspect Warriors, Howling Banshees are almost exclusively female, for it is said that the spirit of the Banshee is feminine. Banshees are fast and mobile, for their training is based essentially on these themes. Death, say the Aeldari, will know how to find you at the appointed hour, no matter where you hide. It is the same with the Banshees. They are armed with Shuriken Pistols and Power Swords and are experts in close combat. Their other weapon is the Banshee Mask, a death's-head helmet that houses a psychosonic amplifier. This artefact concentrates the bearer's rage and aggression and transforms her war cry into a violent psychic wave capable of paralysing the opponent.

Howling Banshees have the \textbf{Elite (2)} and \textbf{Banshee Cry (5+)} abilities.

\subsection{Fire Dragons}

The ritual weapon of these Aspect Warriors, the Fusion Gun, can transform reputedly impenetrable armour into a cloud of superheated vapour in less time than it takes to tell. Aeldari fusion weapons cause the target's molecules to vibrate so violently that they generate so much heat that it ignites before turning into molten liquid and then evaporating. With such weapons, Fire Dragons are perfectly capable of engaging and bringing down any opponent. They know, however, exactly what their role is in the warhost: to annihilate the most robust bastions and war machines the enemy has arrayed against the Asuryani.

Fire Dragons have the \textbf{Urban Combat}, \textbf{Elite (2)}, and \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault} abilities, and their weapon \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)}.

\subsection{Dark Reapers}

The Dark Reapers are the most sinister and destructive of the Asuryani Aspect Warriors. They represent the destructive Aspect of the God of War; their uniform, reminiscent of a skeleton, represents the Harvester of Souls (death). Their armour is black or very deep midnight blue; it incorporates heavy protective plates that also ballast them to ensure their stability when firing. It includes long-range targeting sensor systems and allows the Dark Reapers to fire with great accuracy at targets moving at high speed. The tactical role of the Dark Reapers is to provide long-range covering fire. They usually take position in cover, often to the rear of the Asuryani deployment, and choose their targets. Their accuracy allows them to dominate the battlefield, pinning or destroying enemy units.

Dark Reapers have the \textbf{Elite (2)} and \textbf{Reflex Fire} abilities.

\subsection{Exarchs}

When an Aspect Warrior loses the ability to dissociate himself from his killer self, he is said to have become lost upon the Warrior Path and then becomes an Exarch. An Aspect Warrior does not become an Exarch overnight, but the exaltation of combat, repeated many times, can act as a powerful psychotrope. Exarchs are incorporated into an Aspect Warrior detachment.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.

\subsection{Wraithguard}

These infantrymen are made of wraithbone, an extremely solid material, and they dominate by their stature the Farseers who lead them into battle. Their design enjoys the same organic refinement as that of other Aeldari constructs, and they are often adorned with stylised runes and gems. These mechanical soldiers contrast strongly with their flesh-and-blood counterparts, however, for they lack the Aeldari's celerity. Wraithguard march with the inexorability of death, their sepulchral advance punctuated only by the sound of reality itself being torn by their weapons.

Wraithguard have the \textbf{Spirit Stone} ability.

\subsection{Guardians}

At the heart of an ever more hostile galaxy, Aspect Warriors are not the only ones to take up arms. When a Craftworld's call sounds, its Guardians answer. Composed of citizens who normally walk a more peaceful Path, this highly trained militia, equipped with advanced gear, rivals the professional armies of lesser races. The perpetual struggle for the survival of the Aeldari race holds a cruel irony: hosts raised to defend the populations, whose fighters are in large part civilians. \subsection{Wraithblades}

The most feared Wraithguard are called Wraithblades. When the call to arms sounds, these beings are awakened by the most experienced Farseers. Their Spirit Stones gleam as the bitterness of the dead bathes their skeleton. Once kindled, the anger that animates these artificial bodies can be drowned only in the blood of their enemies. No other wraithbone construct is as eager to throw itself upon the enemy as a Wraithblade, which draws upon its pent-up rage as it charges into the melee. They wield a pair of Ghostswords -- weapons animated by the rudimentary consciousness contained in the Spirit Stones set in their guards. The swords' supernatural power is added to the formidable strength of the wraithbone constructs; when they fall, the blades leave glowing furrows in the air before cutting their targets with merciless precision.

They have the \textbf{Spirit Stone} and \textbf{Dodge (5+)} abilities.

\subsection{Rangers}

These Asuryani are both incomparable scouts and crack shots. They travel across the galaxy, exploring distant worlds and making contact with other races. They roam far from their Craftworlds, even if most of them remain loyal to their origins, and are often charged by the Farseers with long and complex missions requiring cunning and subtlety, so that they may continue to serve their people usefully. In combat they act in small groups and take hidden paths through the Webway in order to take position at strategic points. When battle breaks out they emerge: concealed in their environment, their long sniper rifles then prove deadly even against the best-protected troops, for Rangers know how to target the eyepieces and neck joints of armour. Although their rifles allow precise shots from impressive distances, they are cumbersome and their rate of fire is low. So that every shot counts, Rangers will identify priority targets among the enemy ranks.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Camouflage}, and \textbf{Sniper} abilities.

Of all Aeldari weapons it is the most typical, and as the archetype of the Aeldari warrior, a Dire Avenger must carry a Shuriken Catapult.

Vibration cannons firing at the same target benefit from the effects of the previous shot and increase their penetrating power.

\subsection{Shadow Spectres}

For an Asuryani warrior, death in the mortal realm is not a release from the endless war waged by the survivors of this race. They are so few that the spirits of elite Asuryani warriors must often continue to fight, forever trapped in the twilight realm between life and death. Rather than enter the Infinity Circuit of their Craftworld, their Spirit Stones are used to infuse life into wraithbone constructs. It is this Aspect of Asuryani warfare that the Shadow Spectres illustrate, Khaine's promise that even the end of a mortal existence cannot be an escape from war.

Shadow Spectres have the \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Hard to Hit}, \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, and \textbf{Elite (2)} abilities.
