\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Phoenix}

The Phoenix has many characteristics in common with the Nightwing, but it lacks the fighter's top-end straight-line speed and manoeuvrability; instead it carries a greater payload of weapons for its role as a ground-attack aircraft. Despite its loss of speed, the Phoenix is nonetheless an exceptional aircraft, capable of mixing it with most enemy fighters and holding its own at the top.

The Phoenix has the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Flyer}, and \textbf{Wounds (2)} abilities and is Super-heavy. Its Bombs have the \textbf{Bombing (2)} ability, and the plasma bombs use a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)} and have the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability. You must choose one bomb type per turn.

\subsection{Cobra}

The Cobra is built on the same chassis as the Scorpion, and uses the same anti-grav motors, but rearmed with a large D-Cannon. Like their smaller cousins, the Falcon and its like, the Cobra is simply a new title for a different weapon. Designed to find and destroy enemy war machines, their protective force fields are little defence against the odious energies of the D-Cannon. The Cobra has few other weapons, for its only role is to seek out and destroy the largest enemy war machines, for its weapon is capable of destroying super-heavy tanks or small Imperial Titans in a single shot.

The Cobra has the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities. The D-Cannon uses a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)} and has the \textbf{Psychic Attack}, \textbf{Damage (+1)}, and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/2)} abilities.

\subsection{Scorpion}

The Scorpion is one of the largest vehicles recorded capable of anti-grav movement. This Engine of Vaul combines sophisticated and powerful weapons with the grace and speed that have come to be associated with Aeldari vehicles, who regard the enormous machines of the Imperium or the Orks as crude and unaesthetic compared with the grace of a Scorpion leaping into combat. It is equipped with two Pulse Lasers but above all with 2 Pulsars that have two firing modes.

The Scorpion has the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities. Each Pulsar has the \textbf{Turret} ability and may, independently of the choice made for the other, either make a Concentrated shot which then inflicts 1D3 hits, or a Diffuse shot.

\subsection{Storm Serpent}

This antigrav tank embodies the swiftness of Aeldari warfare, for it can create a stable Warp portal allowing Eldar units rapid access to the battlefield's vital points and also a strategic withdrawal when needed. Unlike other Aeldari Super-heavy vehicles the Storm Serpent has a Holo-Field in place of the main weapon.

The Storm Serpent has the \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Holo-Field}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Ghost Portal}, and \textbf{DR (6+)} abilities.

\subsection{Vampire}

The Vampire is the largest atmospheric aircraft ever encountered, although as a dropship it can also operate in the vacuum of space. It serves mainly to transport Asuryani assault forces, who descend from a spacecraft in orbit to strike their targets before withdrawing rapidly.

The Vampire troop transport has the \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Flyer}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, and \textbf{Transport (8)} abilities. Its Shuriken Cannons have the \textbf{Turret} ability.
