\section{Special Abilities}

\subsection{Synaptic Overload}

Once per turn, a base with this ability may cause a detachment within its Synapse radius to automatically pass a Hive Mind Test.

The use of this ability must be declared before making the test.

\subsection{Bio-Toxin}

Weapons with this ability cannot inflict damage upon allied Tyranid bases.

\subsection{Subterranean Assault}

When a Trygon uses its \textit{Deep Strike} ability to enter the battlefield, one Hormagaunt swarm may emerge with it.

Place the Hormagaunt detachment within 6~cm of the Trygon's arrival point. Both detachments are placed on the battlefield simultaneously.

\subsection{Spore Pods}

Spore Pods release clouds of toxic particles around the Bio-Titan. A Bio-Titan may purchase no more than one Spore Pod.

During the Combat Phase, every base within 15~cm of the centre of the Bio-Titan is hit:

\begin{itemize}
    \item On a 4+ if the Bio-Titan has an Advance or First Fire order.
    \item On a 5+ if the Bio-Titan has a Charge or Forced March order.
\end{itemize}

Spore Pods have AP~0 and possess the \textit{Reduces Cover (3)} and \textit{Bio-Toxin} abilities.

Spore Pods function even if the Bio-Titan is pinned in an assault. In this case, resolve the Spore Pod attacks before resolving the assault. Any damage inflicted counts towards the assault's combat result.

Spore Pods do not suffer a To-Hit penalty when the Bio-Titan is engaged in an assault.

\subsection{Slave (Instinct)}

Slave detachments follow their Instinct order if either of the following applies:

\begin{itemize}
    \item They are outside the Hive Mind radius of a Synapse base during the Orders step.
    \item They have failed a Hive Mind Test.
\end{itemize}

All movement required by an Instinct order is performed normally during the Movement Phase.

\subsubsection{Hunt}

During the Movement Phase, a detachment with this Instinct moves up to its normal Movement characteristic towards the closest enemy detachment that is not already engaged in an assault. If it makes contact, it engages that detachment in an assault.

If the detachment is not engaged in an assault, it must shoot at the closest valid enemy detachment during the Combat Phase.

\subsubsection{Devastation}

During the Movement Phase, a detachment with this Instinct must move up to twice its normal Movement characteristic towards the closest enemy detachment.

If the closest enemy detachment is already engaged in an assault, the Tyranid detachment may instead move towards the second-closest enemy detachment.

If it makes contact with an enemy, it engages that enemy in an assault. A detachment following the Devastation Instinct never makes ranged attacks.

\subsubsection{Nest}

A detachment with this Instinct does not move. During the Combat Phase, it must shoot at the closest visible enemy detachment.

This is not a First Fire order. The detachment therefore cannot:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Perform Overwatch Fire.
    \item Reroll results of 1 on its To-Hit rolls.
    \item Perform Indirect Fire.
\end{itemize}

The detachment must shoot at an enemy even if that enemy is engaged in an assault.

If a detachment following an Instinct order uses a template weapon, centre the template over the closest enemy base.

\subsection{Semi-Synaptic (Instinct)}

Detachments with this ability may receive orders normally, even while outside a Synapse radius.

If they fail a Hive Mind Test, they act as Slave creatures and follow their listed Instinct.

\subsection{Synapse (X cm)}

Detachments with this ability receive orders normally.

They may also control Slave detachments if at least one base from the Slave detachment is within X~cm of a Synapse base.

Detachments arriving through the \textit{Tunneller} or \textit{Mycetic Spore} rules may receive an order if they are within the Synapse radius of a base when they arrive.

The Synapse radius functions even if the Synapse base or the Slave bases are inside transports.

A Synapse base attached to a detachment grants the Synapse ability to the entire detachment.

A detachment following an Instinct order loses its Synapse ability.

\subsection{Spore Mine}

When an attack is made with a Spore Mine weapon, it creates a hazardous area in addition to resolving the normal effects of the attack.

Leave the template in the position where the attack was resolved. It remains in play for the turn in which it was fired and for the following turn, after which it is removed.

The area counts as \textit{Dangerous Terrain (4+/AP 0)} with the \textit{Bio-Toxin} ability.

The Spore Mine area may be targeted by shooting attacks. Each successful hit removes one Spore Mine from the area.

\subsection{Mycetic Spore}

Mycetic Spores follow these rules:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Spores are divided into groups. Each group consists of one or more detachments that purchased Mycetic Spores.

    \item During Off-Table Arrivals, determine the group's arrival point in the same manner as another off-table arrival. Mycetic Spores are not affected by the \textit{Limited Assault} rule.

    \item The Spores enter during the Movement Phase and may be targeted by Overwatch Fire. For range and line-of-sight purposes, they are considered to be at altitude directly above their arrival point.

    \item Make one saving throw for each successful hit and remove destroyed Spores randomly. Troops transported inside Spores destroyed either in flight or on the ground cannot make Emergency Exit Tests.

    \item Place all surviving Spores in coherency and within 12~cm of the arrival point. A Spore cannot be placed on an enemy base or within Impassable terrain.

    \item The transported troops then disembark according to the normal Transport rules.

    \item As soon as a Spore is empty, it is removed and considered destroyed. Empty Spores have no zones of control and cannot contest or secure objectives.

    \item Each Mycetic Spore may transport one Class 1 or 2 base.
\end{itemize}

\section{Psychic Powers}

\subsection{Norn Queen}

\subsubsection{Bio-Resistance}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} The Norn Queen improves its Regeneration by 1 and gains \textbf{Dodge (5+)} for the remainder of the turn.

\subsubsection{Psychic Scream}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} The Norn Queen gains the \textbf{Dread ($-1$)} and \textbf{Psychic Abomination} abilities for the remainder of the turn.

\subsubsection{Psychic Projectile}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, Shooting]:} Choose a target within 45 cm and in the Norn Queen's Line of Sight. The target is hit on a 4+. This is a \textbf{Psychic Power}.

\subsubsection{Warp Field}

\subsubsection{Synaptic Beacon}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} The Dominatrix extends the range of its Synapse Radius to 60 cm for the remainder of the turn. In addition, during the End-of-Turn Effects, detachments acting on Instinct within 60 cm may attempt a Hive Mind test. If successful, remove their Instinct tokens.
