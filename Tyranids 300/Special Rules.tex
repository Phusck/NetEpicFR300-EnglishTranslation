\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Army Creation}

Tyranids select their troops differently from other armies and do not follow the standard army-building rules. Instead, detachments of Synapse creatures generate Command Points, while other formations consume them.

Before another Synapse Formation may be selected, all Command Points generated by the previously selected Synapse Formations must have been spent.

Tyranid Titans are relatively rare. The army may include no more than one Tyranid Titan for every 5 Command Points generated.

\subsection{Morale}

Tyranids are not affected by morale in the same manner as other armies and never make Morale Tests.

Their Morale value instead represents their connection to the Hive Mind. When a Tyranid detachment becomes Broken, it must make a Hive Mind Test. This test is resolved in the same manner as a Morale Test.

If the test is failed, the detachment follows its Instinct, as described by its \textit{Slave} ability, and receives an Instinct counter. If the test is passed, there are no further consequences.

A detachment activated while it has an Instinct counter automatically removes the counter after completing its activation.

A detachment with an Instinct counter suffers a $-1$ AF penalty to represent its disorganisation.

\subsection{Adaptations}

Tyranids excel at adapting to their opponents. To represent this, after seeing the opposing player's army list but before the battle begins, the Tyranid player selects adaptations.

Every Tyranid army has 10 points to spend on adaptations from the following list:

\begin{description}
    \item[Bio-Acid (7 points):]
    For every complete 2,000 points in the army, one attack die from one weapon may gain \textit{Damage (+1)} and improve its AP by 1. The use of this effect must be declared before making the To-Hit roll.

    \item[Bio-Targeting (7 points):]
    Every unit in the army may reroll results of 1 on its ranged To-Hit rolls.

    \item[Endless Swarm (7 points):]
    During the End-of-Turn Effects step, each Hormagaunt, Gargoyle, Ripper, Termagant, or Barbgaunt detachment recovers 1D3 previously lost bases.

    The recovered bases must be placed in coherency with their detachment and outside all enemy zones of control.

    \item[Velocity (7 points):]
    Increase the total movement of every base in the army by 5~cm. Apply this bonus after doubling or tripling the base's movement according to its order.

    \item[Camouflage (5 points):]
    Class 1 and 2 bases gain a 10~cm Infiltration move. If they already possess an Infiltration move, increase it by 10~cm.

    \item[Carapace (5 points):]
    For every complete 1,000 points in the army, the controlling player may reroll one saving throw per turn. This effect may only be used once per detachment during a given turn.

    \item[Assault Expert (5 points):]
    Every base in the army may reroll results of 1 on its assault dice.

    \item[Psychic Scream (5 points):]
    Every enemy detachment with at least one base within a Synapse radius suffers a $-1$ penalty to its Morale value.

    \item[Shadow in the Warp (4 points):]
    Before deployment, for every complete 2,000 points in the army, one Synapse creature may be given the \textit{Dread ($-1$)} ability.

    \item[Acidic Entrails (3 points):]
    If a Tyranid base loses an assault duel after rolling at least one double on its assault dice, it inflicts one AP~0 hit against the base that defeated it.

    \item[Overwhelming Swarm (3 points):]
    Each outnumbering bonus adds 1D6+1 to AF instead of 1D6.

    \item[Subterranean Surge (2 points):]
    When entering the battlefield, a Trygon may bring two Hormagaunt swarms with it instead of one.

    \item[Enhanced Synaptic Link (2 points):]
    Increase the Hive Mind radius of every Synapse creature by 10~cm.

    \item[Synaptic Nodes (2 points):]
    For every complete 3,000 points in the army, the \textit{Slave} ability of one detachment may be replaced with \textit{Synapse (10~cm)}.

    \item[Bio-Synaptic Instinct (1 point):]
    For every complete 2,000 points in the army, the \textit{Slave} ability of one detachment may be replaced with \textit{Semi-Synaptic}.

    The Semi-Synaptic creature retains the same Instinct as the original creature.

    \item[Adrenaline Surge (1 point):]
    For every complete 2,000 points in the army, increase the AF of one detachment by 1.
\end{description}
