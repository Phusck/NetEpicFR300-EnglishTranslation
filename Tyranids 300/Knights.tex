\section{Knights}

\subsection{Barbed Hierodule}

Like the Scythed Hierodule, the Barbed Hierodule has spread throughout the Hive Fleets. During the infernal fighting on Dantris, twenty-six Hierodules were recorded as having been destroyed.

On Beta Anphelion IV, Inquisitor Lok's forces encountered at least four Hierodules of both types. Inquisitor Lok personally destroyed one, while another was brought down by the concentrated heavy-weapons fire of the Cadian troops defending the Theta laboratories. Those same troops were later overwhelmed by a Tyranid swarm led by one of the surviving Hierodules.

The Barbed Hierodule has the \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{Slave (Nest)}, and \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)} abilities. Its Bio-Cannons have the \textbf{Damage (+1)} ability.

\subsection{Scythed Hierodule}

The Scythed Hierodule was first recorded during the invasion of Hamman, a small Imperial colony. During the battle, the planet's garrison mounted a heroic defence but was ultimately defeated.

The Scythed Hierodule played a major role at the forefront of the Tyranid assault, leading swarms of smaller Gaunts against the forts and ferrocrete defensive structures erected by the Planetary Defence Forces.

The Scythed Hierodule has the \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Damage (+2) in Assault}, and \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} abilities. Its Pyro-Acid Jet uses a \textbf{Flame Template} and has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

\subsection{Trygon}

The Trygon is a serpentine creature so colossal that it towers over even the enormous Carnifex. It is entirely covered by a thick carapace of bony plates.

As the Trygon moves, these plates grind against one another, generating a bio-static charge that travels along its body and gathers around its forelimbs. By releasing an ionised breath through its vents, the Trygon can discharge this energy as a high-voltage electrical blast capable of burning its prey down to the bone.

A Trygon's claws are not only formidable weapons but also allow it to burrow through almost any material. When a Trygon detects an enemy upon the surface, it erupts from the ground in a shower of debris and throws itself upon the nearby soldiers and vehicles.

The Trygon has the \textbf{Character}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Underground Assault}, and \textbf{Synapse (15 cm)} abilities. Its Bio-Shock has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} ability.

Its Lightning inflicts 1D3 hits upon bases in contact with the Trygon immediately before resolving the assault. The Tyranid player chooses how these hits are allocated. Lightning does not suffer the To Hit penalty caused by the Trygon being engaged in an assault.

\subsection{Tyrannofex}

There can be no doubt that the Tyrannofex exists solely to spread destruction, for it is built to withstand the full brutality of the battlefield.

Striding towards its prey, this giant deals death from afar and breaks enemy lines with volleys from its merciless bio-cannon. The only way to halt its relentless advance is to bring the beast down, but the Tyrannofex possesses the endurance of a living fortress and pays little heed to anything but the heaviest weapons.

Protected by layers of ablative chitin, the Tyrannofex is as resilient as any war machine produced by conventional industry. Its carapace is so thick and its endurance so great that it can rival the most powerful engines of war.

Spine launchers embedded among the chitin plates on its back provide effective anti-personnel protection, but it is the enormous bio-cannon mounted upon its forelimbs that has earned the beast its sinister reputation throughout the galaxy.

There are two versions of the Tyrannofex: the Assault Tyrannofex and the Support Tyrannofex.

\subsubsection{Assault Tyrannofex}

\subsection{Norn Queen}

Some Tyranid Hive Ships contain immense chambers hundreds of metres high, occupied by creatures so vast that their bodies fill almost the entire space. These creatures are the Norn Queens, the progenitors of the Hive Mind.

Norn Queens are enormous biological factories whose sole purpose is to create Tyranid organisms. They can absorb biomass and manipulate it in countless ways, continually adapting their ``offspring'' to the requirements of each conquest.

Every organism within a Hive Fleet, from the smallest creature to every species and individual variant, owes its existence to the Norn Queens.

The Norn Queen has the \textbf{Character}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Synaptic Overload}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, \textbf{Forward Observer}, and \textbf{Synapse (30 cm)} abilities.

In addition, the Norn Queen may use one of its psychic powers each turn.
