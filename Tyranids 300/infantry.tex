\subsection{Hive Guard}

During the final stages of a Tyranid invasion, strange alien structures appear upon the surface of the prey world. Enormous spore chimneys erupt from the ravaged ground to belch their toxins into the air, while capillary towers rise to transmit a slurry of predigested biomass to the hive ships.

These structures are essential to the fulfilment of the Tyranids' objectives, and so the Hive Mind has created specialised bioforms dedicated to their protection: the Hive Guard. Stocky and heavily armoured, these organisms advance ponderously to form a phalanx before the enemy, bracing themselves before unleashing a devastating hail of projectiles from the massive bio-cannons fused to their arms. A deluge of vicious barbs tears through the enemy ranks, piercing armour and bone alike and raising a mist of blood.

They have the \textbf{Slave (Nest)} ability.

\subsection{Gargoyles}

Gargoyles form the first wave of Tyranids seen at the beginning of a battle. Their primary role is to spread terror and confusion throughout the enemy lines, throwing their prey into disarray and disrupting them as the rest of the swarm strikes.

A Tyranid attack is therefore often preceded by the beating of thousands of pairs of membranous wings as flights of Gargoyles descend upon the enemy, darkening the sky with their numbers and spitting death from their Flame Jets. The psychological impact of such an aerial assault is often enough to break the morale of ordinary combatants.

Unfortunately, fleeing is the worst possible choice for those facing a flying swarm of Gargoyles. These predators excel at isolating panicked targets before cutting them down with their biomorphs, or carrying them into the air and dropping them back to the ground with bone-shattering force.

Gargoyles have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Jump Packs}, and \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} abilities. Their weapon has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

\subsection{Alpha Genestealer}

The product of the Genestealers' constant evolution, Alpha Genestealers are the ultimate close-combat warriors. Terrifyingly strong and fast, their diamond-hard claws can tear through adamantium as if it were paper.

These organisms are generally deployed at the forefront of an attack, leading swarms of Genestealers to infiltrate enemy positions. Their role is to launch devastating attacks against those enemies that pose the greatest threat to the rest of the horde.

Genestealers are not only highly intelligent, but also possess a form of telepathy that allows them to act independently of the Hive Mind's directives.

The Alpha Genestealer has the \textbf{Synapse (15 cm)}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.

\subsection{Genestealers}

The Tyranid Hive Fleets contain countless terrifying creatures, but one among them has earned a prominent place in the legends of hundreds of worlds. It is a stalker in the shadows, a clawed herald of death. It is known as the Genestealer, and it is one of the great scourges of the galaxy.

A swift and powerful predator, its claws can pierce even the strongest armour. Its broad, bulbous head contains an agile mind with remarkable powers of adaptation and a form of telepathy that allows it to act independently of the Hive Mind's directives. Thanks to this autonomy, it can destroy through cunning what brute force cannot overcome.

The Genestealers' powerful survival instinct continually drives them ahead of the Hive Fleets in search of spacecraft fleeing the surfaces of doomed prey worlds. A single Genestealer can easily conceal itself within a cargo hold, remaining hidden from the sight and hearing of the crew until the vessel reaches another planet. The arrival of just one Genestealer on a world can ultimately lead to the extinction of its entire population.

Genestealers have the \textbf{Semi-Synaptic (Devastation)}, \textbf{Infiltration}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.

\subsection{Tyranid Warriors}

Of all the species produced by the Hive Mind, Tyranid Warriors are among the most adaptable. They are creatures of nightmare: relentless killing machines with razor-sharp claws and black eyes gleaming with malevolent intelligence.

A Tyranid Warrior stands twice the height of a man, and its carapace is covered in impenetrable chitin. A creature so large and robust might be expected to move slowly, but a Tyranid Warrior strikes with the speed of a cracking whip.

Unlike most creatures controlled by the Hive Mind, Tyranid Warriors possess the intelligence required to use a wide variety of symbiotic weapons. They can therefore be encountered at every level of a Tyranid horde, fighting on the front line with boneswords and lash whips or providing supporting fire with Deathspitters and other heavy weapons.

Whatever its equipment, a Tyranid Warrior is a relentless opponent capable of identifying and exploiting its enemies' weaknesses with the insight of a born warrior. Worse still, its consciousness is intimately connected to the Hive Mind, whose knowledge it can draw upon whenever its own proves insufficient.

Tyranid Warriors have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)}, and \textbf{Elite (2)} abilities.

\subsection{Rippers}

As soon as the Tyranids descend upon a planet, they release countless swarms of organisms whose purpose is to devour everything in their path. The most numerous of these feeder-beasts are known to the Adepts as \textit{Minoris Omniphagem}, but are more commonly called Rippers by Imperial forces.

A Ripper Swarm is a mass of worm-like organisms driven by an endless appetite. These small creatures have serpentine bodies approximately half a metre long, terminating in broad heads above clusters of vestigial grasping claws.

Each head is split by an enormous mouth filled with rows of exceptionally sharp fangs and razor-edged bony ridges. A Ripper's powerful jaws can crush flesh and bone with alarming ease, and these creatures have even been known to gnaw through plasteel to reach food.

On the rare occasions when Rippers encounter obstacles they cannot overcome by consuming them, they burrow beneath them, their instincts guiding them towards their prey.

Rippers have the \textbf{Free Deployment} and \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} abilities.

\subsection{Hormagaunts}

The Hormagaunt is a highly specialised evolution of the Termagant and is extremely widespread throughout the Hive Fleets, which can deploy millions of these creatures.

A Hormagaunt possesses four razor-sharp claws designed to penetrate flesh and metal with equal ease. Its heavily muscled hind legs allow it to hurl itself towards its prey in a series of prodigious leaps.

Hormagaunts possess extraordinary tenacity and pursue their targets tirelessly, ignoring fatigue and injury until they have caught and slaughtered them with their scythe-like claws.

After bringing down its prey, the Hormagaunt rapidly begins to feed, tearing large pieces of flesh from the carcass with its fang-filled mouth. Such meals are quickly consumed, for the Hormagaunt's metabolism constantly drives it to seek new victims.

Hormagaunts have the \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} ability.

\subsection{Lictor}

The Lictor is a highly specialised evolution of the Tyranid Warrior, adapted to serve as a scout for the Hive Fleets. Lictors form the vanguard of the swarm, constantly searching for pockets of resistance to eliminate and indigenous life forms to absorb.

They can move quickly and silently through almost any type of terrain. The unique properties of their carapaces camouflage them so effectively that they become practically invisible to the naked eye.

Lictors can remain hidden for days, motionless and undetected by their future victims. They are the unseen heralds of the disaster to come.

The Lictor's aggressive instincts are relatively underdeveloped, as it was created primarily to locate prey. Lictors are opportunistic hunters that prefer to stalk the shadows and avoid direct confrontations that might turn against them.

A Lictor usually kills its victims after they have become separated from their companions. It may then remain hidden for days before resuming its predations.

Lictors have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Semi-Synaptic (Hunt)}, and \textbf{Advanced Camouflage} abilities.

\subsection{Termagants}

Termagants are fast, agile, and cunning. They are among the smallest creatures of the Hive Mind, measuring little more than two metres in length. They were originally created to patrol the passageways of the bio-ships and defend them against intruders.

During planetary invasions, Termagants accompany Tyranid Warriors, running at their feet upon four limbs while unleashing torrents of anti-personnel fire. They are commonly armed with Fleshborers held in the claws of their forelimbs.

A Termagant's mind is cunning but limited, and the creature may become confused if separated from the Hive Mind. When this occurs, its survival instinct takes control, causing it to abandon the fighting and seek shelter.

For this reason, an army that has repelled a Tyranid assault must advance cautiously. Every cellar, building, or patch of woodland may conceal a nest of Termagants that will defend itself with unimaginable ferocity if discovered.

Termagants have the \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} ability.
