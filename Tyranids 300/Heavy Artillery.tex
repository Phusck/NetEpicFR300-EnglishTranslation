\section{Heavy Artillery}

\subsection{Off-Table Artillery}

Off-Table Artillery represents either long-range bio-weapon batteries deployed at a safe distance behind the combat zone or orbital or naval artillery. See the General Rules for further details regarding its use.

The \textbf{Bio-Plasma Shot} uses a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)} and has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability.

The \textbf{Spore-Mine Shot} uses a \textbf{Template (12 cm)} and has the \textbf{Spore-Mines}, \textbf{Bio-Toxins}, and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} abilities.
