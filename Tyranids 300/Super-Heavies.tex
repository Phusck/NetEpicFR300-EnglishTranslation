\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Dominatrix}

The Dominatrix is a truly colossal Tyranid creature. Its sheer mass and arsenal of bio-weapons allow it to overcome an entire squadron of Imperial super-heavy tanks.

Furthermore, it is far from being a mindless monster. Its intelligence is equal to that of the Hive Tyrants, while its empathic connection to the Hive Mind surpasses even that of those powerful creatures.

As long as lesser Tyranid creatures remain close to a Dominatrix, they become extensions of its will. A swarm directed by a Dominatrix therefore operates with unparalleled efficiency and tactical brilliance.

The Dominatrix has the \textbf{Character}, \textbf{DR (6+)}, \textbf{Wounds (5)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{Psychic Save (2+)}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Synaptic Overload}, \textbf{Forward Observer}, and \textbf{Synapse (45 cm)} abilities. Its Bio-Plasma Cannons have the \textbf{Turret} ability.

In addition, the Dominatrix may use one of its psychic powers each turn.

\subsection{Harridan}

Harridans are the largest flying Tyranid creatures capable of atmospheric flight ever encountered by the Imperium during its relentless war against the intergalactic threat. They are gigantic monsters comparable to the wyverns and dragons of ancient legend.

Circling high above the battlefield and gliding upon the warm thermal currents of war with its vast leathery wings outstretched, the Harridan watches patiently before diving upon the battlefield to strafe its enemies with its ventral Bio-Cannons.

The Harridan has the \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)}, \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{Floater}, \textbf{Infiltration}, and \textbf{Transport (5 Gargoyles)} abilities.

Its Spore Cloud has the \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)}, \textbf{Bio-Toxins}, and \textbf{Bombardment (1)} abilities.
