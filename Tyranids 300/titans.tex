\section{Titans}

\subsection{Alpha Hierodule}

Like all Tyranid organisms, Bio-Titans are characterised by their rapid adaptations. Much like the bio-ships that created them, Bio-Titans are amalgamations of numerous organisms fused together until they resemble a single living creature.

Bio-Titans are notoriously difficult to destroy, even with super-heavy weapons. They are protected by overlapping chitin plates angled to deflect incoming fire. Even if this carapace is penetrated, the composite nature of these monsters means that a fatal blow against one symbiotic organism is unlikely to destroy the creature as a whole.

Only the concentrated firepower of a Titan or an entire artillery company can hope to bring down a Bio-Titan before it spreads destruction throughout the enemy ranks.

The Alpha Hierodule is the smallest but also the most widespread Bio-Titan.

The Alpha Hierodule has the \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Regeneration (4+)}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Damage (+2) in Assault}, and \textbf{Semi-Synaptic} abilities.

\subsection{Hierophant}

The Hierophant Bio-Titan is an immense, hideous, spine-covered creature that towers over the battlefield. It is among the largest bio-creatures ever encountered within the swarms of the Hive Fleets and is comparable in size and power to an Imperial Titan.

A brood of Hierophants is invariably found at the forefront of a major Tyranid attack. Advancing with enormous strides, they draw enemy heavy-weapons fire away from lesser creatures and absorb tremendous punishment before crashing into the enemy lines with devastating force.

The Hierophant is the Tyranids' largest shock creature. It is armed with a massive Bio-Cannon, numerous claws, and chitinous blades. Its body is protected by a thick chitinous hide that releases toxic spores through the gaps between its armour plates.

The Hierophant has the \textbf{Wounds (9)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{Regeneration (4+)}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Transport (5)}, \textbf{Damage (+2) in Assault}, and \textbf{Semi-Synaptic} abilities.
