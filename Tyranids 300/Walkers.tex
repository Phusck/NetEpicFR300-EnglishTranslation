\section{Walkers}

\subsection{Carnifex}

Carnifexes are living engines of destruction: monsters covered in thick armour and possessed of phenomenal strength. They are among the most resilient and deadly war beasts of the Hive Fleets, having been created to spearhead assaults.

Although the Hive Mind has spawned other, larger creatures, few can rival a Carnifex in terms of sheer strength and destructive capability.

Carnifexes have the \textbf{Fear}, \textbf{Slave (Devastation)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Regeneration (5+)} abilities.

\subsection{Winged Hive Tyrant}

A relatively recent addition to the Tyranid arsenal, Winged Hive Tyrants can be seen directing Gargoyle raids deep within enemy territory.

Like their more common counterparts, Winged Hive Tyrants drive nearby swarms onward and spread the influence of the Hive Mind throughout the seething hordes.

Winged Hive Tyrants have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Elite (2)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Antigrav}, \textbf{Fear}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, and \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)} abilities.

\subsection{Hive Tyrant}

Hive Tyrants are the commanders of the Tyranid swarms and the executors of the Hive Mind's will. They are invariably immense and heavily muscled creatures, standing almost as tall as a Dreadnought.

They possess extraordinary strength and can tear through plasteel and ferrocrete with their claws. Every part of a Hive Tyrant's body appears to have been designed to maim or kill, including the pointed layers of chitin covering its limbs.

A Hive Tyrant is a formidable opponent at any distance, equally dangerous when armed with a venom cannon or stranglethorn cannon as it is with a bonesword or its bare claws.

Hive Tyrants have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Elite (2)}, \textbf{Fear}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, and \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)} abilities.

\subsection{Venomthrope}

The Venomthrope is a gaunt creature whose tentacular limbs and knotted body exude alien toxins. It releases clouds of spores resembling sickly yellow vapours, which contain voracious organisms hostile to all non-Tyranid life.

Even brief exposure to this gas causes nausea and uncontrollable muscular spasms, leaving the victim at the mercy of the Venomthrope and nearby Tyranids. If the prey has the misfortune of remaining in contact with the gas for even a minute, the organisms begin burrowing into its body.

The Venomthrope has the \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} and \textbf{DR (5+)} abilities.

\subsection{Zoanthrope}

Zoanthropes were created solely to channel the psychic potential of the Hive Mind, and their bodies are entirely dedicated to this function.

A Zoanthrope's connection to the synaptic network is so powerful that, by focusing even the smallest fraction of its mind, it can project a blast of force powerful enough to disintegrate adamantium and boil flesh.

The Zoanthrope has the \textbf{Semi-Synaptic (Nest)}, \textbf{Protection (4+)}, and \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)} abilities. Its Warp Blast is a \textbf{Psychic Attack} and has the \textbf{Anti-Aircraft against targets at Altitude} ability.
