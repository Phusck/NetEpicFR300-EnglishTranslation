\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Dominatrix}

The Dominatrix is a truly colossal Tyranid creature. Its sheer mass and arsenal of bio-weapons allow it to overcome an entire squadron of Imperial super-heavy tanks.

Furthermore, it is far from being a mindless monster. Its intelligence is equal to that of the Hive Tyrants, while its empathic connection to the Hive Mind surpasses even that of those powerful creatures.

As long as lesser Tyranid creatures remain close to a Dominatrix, they become extensions of its will. A swarm directed by a Dominatrix therefore operates with unparalleled efficiency and tactical brilliance.

The Dominatrix has the \textbf{Character}, \textbf{DR (6+)}, \textbf{Wounds (5)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Terror}, \textbf{Psychic Save (2+)}, \textbf{Psyker}, \textbf{Synaptic Overload}, \textbf{Forward Observer}, and \textbf{Synapse (45 cm)} abilities. Its Bio-Plasma Cannons have the \textbf{Turret} ability.

In addition, the Dominatrix may use one of its psychic powers each turn.

\subsection{Harridan}

Harridans are the largest flying Tyranid creatures capable of atmospheric flight ever encountered by the Imperium during its relentless war against the intergalactic threat. They are gigantic monsters comparable to the wyverns and dragons of ancient legend.

Circling high above the battlefield and gliding upon the warm thermal currents of war with its vast leathery wings outstretched, the Harridan watches patiently before diving upon the battlefield to strafe its enemies with its ventral Bio-Cannons.

The Harridan has the \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)}, \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{Floater}, \textbf{Infiltration}, and \textbf{Transport (5 Gargoyles)} abilities.

Its Spore Cloud has the \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)}, \textbf{Bio-Toxin}, and \textbf{Bombardment (1)} abilities.

\subsection{Trygon}

The Trygon is a serpentine creature so colossal that it towers over even the enormous Carnifex. It is entirely covered by a thick carapace of bony plates.

As the Trygon moves, these plates grind against one another, generating a bio-static charge that travels along its body and gathers around its forelimbs. By releasing an ionised breath through its vents, the Trygon can discharge this energy as a high-voltage electrical blast capable of burning its prey down to the bone.

A Trygon's claws are not only formidable weapons but also allow it to burrow through almost any material. When a Trygon detects an enemy upon the surface, it erupts from the ground in a shower of debris and throws itself upon the nearby soldiers and vehicles.

The Trygon has the \textbf{Character}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Integral Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Subterranean Assault}, and \textbf{Synapse (15 cm)} abilities. Its Bio-Shock has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} ability.

Its Lightning inflicts 1D3 hits upon bases in contact with the Trygon immediately before resolving the assault. The Tyranid player chooses how these hits are allocated. Lightning does not suffer the To-Hit penalty caused by the Trygon being engaged in an assault.

\subsection{Tyrannofex}

There can be no doubt that the Tyrannofex exists solely to spread destruction, for it is built to withstand the full brutality of the battlefield.

Striding towards its prey, this giant deals death from afar and breaks enemy lines with volleys from its merciless bio-cannon. The only way to halt its relentless advance is to bring the beast down, but the Tyrannofex possesses the endurance of a living fortress and pays little heed to anything but the heaviest weapons.

Protected by layers of ablative chitin, the Tyrannofex is as resilient as any war machine produced by conventional industry. Its carapace is so thick and its endurance so great that it can rival the most powerful engines of war.

Spine launchers embedded among the chitin plates on its back provide effective anti-personnel protection, but it is the enormous bio-cannon mounted upon its forelimbs that has earned the beast its sinister reputation throughout the galaxy.

There are two versions of the Tyrannofex: the Assault Tyrannofex and the Support Tyrannofex.

\subsubsection{Assault Tyrannofex}

The Assault Tyrannofex has the \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{DR (4+)}, \textbf{Regeneration (5+)}, \textbf{Fear}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in Assault}, and \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} abilities.
