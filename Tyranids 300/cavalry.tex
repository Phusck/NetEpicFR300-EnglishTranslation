\subsection{Raveners}

Raveners are little more than mindless predators whose clawed, serpentine bodies are driven by a consuming hunger that has few equals, even among the ravenous Tyranid race. Their knotted muscles grant them unimaginable speed across almost any type of surface.

They can effortlessly leap over smaller obstacles, weave swiftly through ruins, and even race across swamps at terrifying speed. However, this is neither their primary means of approach nor the one most feared by their prey, for when a Ravener charges openly, its victim at least has an opportunity to see it coming.

A Ravener's immense physical strength allows it to burrow for great distances beneath the surface of a world before erupting from the ground beneath an enemy position in an explosion of earth, its claws lashing in every direction and its chest-mounted weapons spitting death.

Raveners receive orders normally when they arrive on the battlefield, provided they are within the Synapse radius of a Synapse base when orders are assigned.

Raveners have the \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Walker}, and \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} abilities.
