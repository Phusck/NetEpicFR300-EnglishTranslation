\section{Infantry}

\subsection{Acolyte Iconward}

The most intelligent and capable of the Acolytes are entrusted with a sacred task: to bear the colours of the cult in battle. Although the cult's symbols and slogans spread as it gains influence, they remain cryptic, their meaning known to few. When the Primus and the Magus raise their troops for the final insurrection, however, the most privileged Acolytes present themselves with standards lovingly fashioned by the infected creatures that gave them birth. In rituals, these banners receive the cult's icon, no longer hidden, but displayed in vivid colours.

It has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Charismatic (12 cm)}, and \textbf{Attached Character} abilities.

10 cm & -- & +2 & Attached & Bolt Pistol & 20 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Aberrant}

Misshapen, simple-minded, and possessed of superhuman strength, Aberrants are a repulsive offshoot of a brood's cycle. Their protective instinct towards their brothers in the cult makes them precious assets. In battle they drag themselves towards the largest pockets of resistance. Then they raise heavy industrial tools and mutter praises to the Patriarch as they charge, inflicting impressive carnage through brute strength and determination.

They have the \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} ability.

\subsection{Acolytes}

Because of their obviously xenos traits, Acolytes do not blend into human society as the third and fourth generations do, but infest the forgotten underways of urban and industrial sprawls. There they learn the arts of stealth and ambush by falling upon those who venture into their territory. They do not kill with impunity, for nothing must alert the ruling classes of their world to the underground threat until the hour has come. Intruders who are not torn apart are dragged into the darkness to meet a less enviable fate: the dreadful embrace of a Genestealer.

They have the \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} ability.

10 cm & -- & +1 & 8 & Lasgun & 45 cm & 1 & 5+ & 0
\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Biophagus}

Masters of genetic manipulation and bio-alchemy, Biophaguses are charged with industrialising the processes of indoctrination and infection. They concoct new means of contaminating their victims, altering bodies and minds to make them more susceptible to the cult's influence. Biophaguses can pass themselves off as medicae specialists in slum hospitals, or in macro-alchemical distilleries and medi-paste factories. Established in highly placed posts, they test the local population with impunity and conduct experiments upon their own kind.

It has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Genomic Enhancements} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
10 cm & -- & +3 & Attached & Injector & -- & -- & -- & --

\subsection{Infantry Cannon}

This heavy weapon consists of an automatic cannon firing dense, solid, large-calibre projectiles at high muzzle velocity that pierce armour. It can annihilate light and medium vehicles. The Autocannon is regularly employed by the Imperial Guard. A single operator is enough to fire it, but two are better for moving and setting it up.

It has the \textbf{Slave (Nest)} ability.

\subsection{Genestealer Assassin}

Genestealer Assassins are terrible unseen agents, able to strike their enemy and vanish before it can react. They travel the underhives without being spotted and can infiltrate the most secure places unnoticed. Their mission is to strike enemy leaders or installations of strategic importance, sowing chaos in the opponent's chains of command or supply so that it is less able to react when the cult launches its final assault.

They have the \textbf{Genestealer Assassins} ability.

\unitimage{genestealer-assassin.png}

\subsection{Alpha Genestealer}

The product of the Genestealers' constant evolution, Alphas are the ultimate close-combat warriors. Terrifyingly strong and fast, their diamond-hard claws can tear through adamantium as if it were paper. These organisms are generally deployed in the vanguard, at the head of Genestealer swarms, in order to infiltrate enemy positions. Their role is to launch devastating attacks against those adversaries that pose the greatest threat to the rest of the horde.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{Synapse (15 cm)}, and \textbf{Damage ($+1$) in assault} abilities.

\subsection{Genestealers}

As the first Tyranid organism discovered by the Imperium, Genestealers are one of the species composing the Hive Fleets' armies about which the most information has been gathered. Until now, the majority of those encountered have displayed very similar behaviour and physiology, but some specimens possess evolutionary biomorphs that prove categorically that their species does not conform to a strict biological model. One of their strangest faculties, to which they owe their name, is this ability to assimilate the genetic heritage of their hosts.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Semi-Synaptic (Devastation)} abilities.

\subsection{Genestealer Cultists}

The Genestealer Curse is a parasitic infection of mind, body, and soul that gnaws at the heart of the Imperium. Hybrids of xenos and humans, gifted with limitless patience and an implacable hatred of the Imperium, these zealots of the Genestealer Cults prepare their offensive across several generations before erupting from the darkest corners of Humanity's domain. Thousands of cultists pray to their Patriarch, a living god from beyond the stars, and when the grim and glorious day of a Genestealer Cult's uprising comes, its warriors already hold a tenfold advantage.

They have the \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} ability.

\subsection{Hybrid}

Repulsive, cruel, and gifted with animal cunning, Hybrids are the principal disciples of the Genestealer Cult. They are the offspring of the first infected hosts. Although in appearance the Hybrids' parents are healthy, part of their mind has been stolen by the Genestealer Curse. Their love for their hideous progeny is more intense than the usual bond between parents and children. The first generation born is altered by the germs implanted in the parents; these Hybrids resemble Tyranids more than modified humans. Those who reach maturity then reproduce, in turn spawning new generations.

They have the \textbf{Semi-Synaptic (Devastation)} ability.

\subsection{Locus}

Stoic and of inhuman patience, the Locus can pass in a flash from threatening immobility to a whirlwind of motion. This sudden eruption of violence is as shocking as it is deadly. Like a Lictor bursting from the darkness, the Locus strikes swiftly to behead the enemy, its sabres glittering across the battlefield to shear the head from its victim. Gifted with a Genestealer's reaction speed, the Locus's martial prowess equals that of an Aeldari Exarch. The Locus excels in the role of bodyguard, on the battlefield as at the negotiating table: faced with the threat of its mere presence, few dare to thwart its master's ambitions.

They have the \textbf{Camouflage} ability.

\subsection{Magus}

Maguses possess psychic gifts and supernatural charisma. Their control over the Hybrids that surround them is total. Prophets of their Patriarch, their word is law, and their psychic abilities are powerful enough to impose it. Yet despite their presence and mental faculties, each Magus is only an extension of its Patriarch's will, just as the latter is the incarnation of the great Hive Mind.

It has the \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Charismatic (30 cm)}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Synapse (40 cm)}, \textbf{Psyker}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities. The Magus may use one of its powers each turn.

\subsection{Mole Mortar}

This is a light artillery piece that, instead of firing a shell into the air, sends it underground to its target, exploding beneath it. If a Mole Mortar hits a Titan or a Praetorian, roll a D6. On a 6 the target is trapped by the crater formed by the explosion. Trapped bases may shoot normally, but if they wish to leave the crater they must be on a Charge order.

The Mole Mortar has the \textbf{Artillery}, \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-2$)}, \textbf{Slave (Nest)}, and \textbf{Subterranean Fire} abilities.

\begin{unitstatstable}
5 cm & -- & $-1$ & 8 & Mole Mortar & 100 cm & 1 & 4+ & 0

\subsection{Patriarch}

At the centre of the web of every Genestealer Cult infestation sits the Patriarch, father of a hidden dynasty. As tall as two Space Marines and possessing enough strength to reduce an Ogryn to a heap of meat, this creature is a nightmare of fangs and claws. In a series of blows from diamond-hard talons, the Patriarch can carve through platoons of warriors in heavy armour, or even vehicles. Yet it is the hypnotic spell with which it binds its hosts that marks it as the most dangerous Tyranid ever identified by the Imperium. Its supernatural powers make it a formidable puppeteer, a threat so great that it possesses its own xenocode designation.

It has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Regeneration (4+)}, \textbf{Charismatic (40 cm)}, \textbf{Elite (2)}, \textbf{Synapse (40 cm)}, \textbf{Psyker}, and \textbf{Character} abilities. The Patriarch may use one of its powers each turn.

\end{unitstatstable}

\subsection{Neophytes}

Neophytes are the cultists of the third and fourth generations. They form the line infantry and the bulk of the cult's warriors. They are fiercely devoted to the cult and in particular to the Patriarch at its heart, and dedicate every moment of their existence to the fulfilment of its dark designs. The mental and spiritual bond to the cult is so strong that they are happy to launch themselves into suicide attacks or to give their lives in exchange for the survival of their elders. If their leaders demand that they work for years to gain the trust of a human organisation, they will do so without complaint.

They have the \textbf{Slave (Nest)} ability.

\subsection{Primus}

Primuses are grandiloquent generals in time of war, but so long as the cult remains hidden their task is to spread the Genestealer Curse to other planets. Their gift for command and coordination ensures the rapid militarisation of the cult, while they oversee the diversion of vehicles, or even of ships. Tough champions and adored heroes among the Hybrids, Primuses emerge only when their cult has reached a position of strength. They appear in an early generation, but generally not before the third cycle.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)}, \textbf{Charismatic (12 cm)}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.
