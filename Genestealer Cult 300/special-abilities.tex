\section{Special Abilities}

\subsection{Slave (Instinct)}

Slave detachments follow their Instinct order if either of the following applies:

\begin{itemize}
    \item They are outside the Synapse radius of a Synapse base during the Orders step.
    \item They have failed a Hive Mind Test.
\end{itemize}

All movement required by an Instinct order is performed normally during the Movement Phase.

\subsubsection{Hunt}

During the Movement Phase, a detachment with this Instinct moves its normal Movement characteristic towards the closest enemy detachment that is not already engaged in an assault. If it makes contact, it engages that detachment in an assault.

If the detachment is not engaged in an assault, it must shoot at the closest valid enemy detachment during the Combat Phase.

\subsubsection{Devastation}

During the Movement Phase, a detachment with this Instinct must move twice its normal Movement characteristic towards the closest enemy detachment.

If the closest enemy detachment is already engaged in an assault, it may instead move towards the second-closest enemy detachment.

If it makes contact with an enemy, it engages that enemy in an assault. A detachment following the Devastation Instinct never makes ranged attacks.

\subsubsection{Nest}

A detachment with this Instinct does not move. During the Combat Phase, it must shoot at the closest visible enemy detachment.

This is not a First Fire order. The detachment therefore cannot:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Perform Overwatch Fire.
    \item Reroll results of 1 on its To-Hit rolls.
    \item Perform Indirect Fire.
\end{itemize}

The detachment must shoot at an enemy even if that enemy is engaged in an assault.

If a detachment following an Instinct order uses a template weapon, centre the template over the closest enemy base.

\subsection{Semi-Synaptic (Instinct)}

Detachments with this ability may receive orders normally, even while outside a Synapse radius.

If they fail a Hive Mind Test, they act as Slave creatures and follow their listed Instinct.

\subsection{Synapse (X cm)}

Detachments with this ability receive orders normally.

They may also control Slave detachments if at least one base from the Slave detachment is within X~cm of a Synapse base.

Detachments arriving through the \textit{Tunneller} or \textit{Mycetic Spore} rules may receive an order if they are within the Synapse radius of a base when they arrive.

The Synapse radius functions even if the Synapse base or the Slave bases are inside transports.

A Synapse base attached to a detachment grants the Synapse ability to the entire detachment.

A detachment following an Instinct order loses its Synapse ability.

\subsection{Genestealer Assassins}

Assassins are not represented by a model on the battlefield. They are used once during the game, complete their mission, and withdraw. Assassins are used in the Combat Phase. The same Assassin formation may be used only once per game. Because they cannot be destroyed in the usual manner, the Assassin is considered Broken when it is used. There are 3 types of assassin that may be employed. Once army lists are known but before deployment, you must tell your opponent which type of assassin you choose. You cannot take the same assassin again until you have taken one of each.

\subsubsection{Sanctus Hybrid Assassin}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, counts as an activation]:} Choose an HQ base that is not inside a transport. Immediately resolve an assault between the target and the Genestealer Assassin (AF $+5$). If the target is already engaged in an assault, the activation triggers the assault in its entirety. The Genestealer Assassin receives the bonuses for previous attackers and also counts among the previous attackers for subsequent opponents of its target.

\subsubsection{Reductus Saboteur}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, counts as an activation]:} Place a 7.5~cm template anywhere on the table. It inflicts a hit on a 3+ with AP~$-2$.

\subsubsection{Sanctus Sniper Assassin}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, counts as an activation]:} Choose an enemy HQ or Character base that is on the table and that can be chosen as a shooting target, ignoring the usual target-selection restrictions and the HQ rule. The target is hit by a shot that hits on a 3+ with AP~$-2$, has the \textit{Reduces Cover ($-1$)} ability, and may reroll results of 1 on its rolls as if it were on First Fire.

\subsection{Genomic Enhancements}

Genomic Enhancements allow you to choose one enhancement for the detachment attached to the base with this ability, chosen from the following list:

\begin{itemize}
    \item A $+1$ bonus to Assault Factor.
    \item A 5~cm increase to base Movement.
    \item Regeneration (5+).
\end{itemize}

Choose the desired effect before the battle begins, at the moment of deployment.

\section{Psychic Powers}

\subsection{Magus}

\subsubsection{Hypnotic Gaze}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} The Magus gains the \textit{Dread ($-1$)} ability until the end of the turn.

\subsubsection{Mental Domination}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} All detachments from this codex within 40~cm of the Magus may reroll failed Morale Tests for the remainder of the turn.

\subsubsection{Mental Lightning}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, Shooting]:} Choose a target within 45~cm and in Line of Sight. The target is hit on a 4+. This is a Psychic Attack. If the target has a hit-location template, make a Damage roll against the Head or Bridge.

\subsection{Patriarch}

\subsubsection{Hypnotic Gaze}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} During activation, choose an enemy detachment within 30~cm and in Line of Sight. The detachment must pass a Morale Test at $-2$. If it fails, the detachment loses its order and rolls 1 fewer die in assault.

\subsubsection{Frenzy}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} Choose a detachment from this codex within 45~cm. That detachment gains 5~cm to its base Movement, a $+1$ bonus to its AF, and rerolls its assault dice until the end of the turn.

\subsubsection{Mental Lightning}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, Shooting]:} Choose a target within 45~cm and in Line of Sight. The target is hit on a 4+. This is a Psychic Attack. If the target has a hit-location template, make a Damage roll against the Head or Bridge.

\subsection{Hybrid Psyker}

\subsubsection{Psychic Overload}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} The Hybrid Psyker gains the \textit{Leader} ability until the end of the turn.

\subsubsection{Swarm Telepathy}

\textbf{[Movement Phase, upon activation]:} The Hybrid Psyker acts as a Forward Observer. However, it may use the Line of Sight of an allied model within 45~cm instead of its own. That model need not be in Line of Sight of the Hybrid Psyker.

\subsubsection{Mental Lightning}

\textbf{[Combat Phase, Shooting]:} Choose a target within 45~cm and in Line of Sight. The target is hit on a 4+. This is a Psychic Attack. If the target has a hit-location template, make a Damage roll against the Head or Bridge.
