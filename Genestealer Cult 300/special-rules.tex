\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Blip Counters}

For every Company Formation in the Genestealer Cult army, you receive 2 Blip counters. Blips are placed before deployment, immediately after objectives are placed, in the Genestealer Cult player's own half of the table. These Blips may be revealed in several ways:

\begin{itemize}
    \item At the Genestealer Cult player's choice during the Movement Phase or the Combat Phase.
    \item If an enemy detachment ends its movement within 20~cm of a Blip.
\end{itemize}

When a Blip is revealed, the player may choose to associate a detachment in reserve with it, or simply remove it with no further effect. If a detachment is associated with the Blip, place the detachment's bases within 6~cm of the counter and outside every enemy zone of control (as for \textit{Deep Strike (2)}). The detachment may have any order (including First Fire) and does not lose movement if it arrives via a Blip.

Any number of Class 1 detachments may be placed in reserve and may enter the table via Blips. Another way to bring reserve troops onto the table is to place them in contact with a table edge during the Initial Phase, during Off-Table Arrivals. According to the turn, the following table edges are available:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Turn 1: the edge of the player's deployment zone.
    \item Turn 2: a table edge of the player's half of the table.
    \item Turn 3 and later: any table edge except the enemy deployment zone.
\end{itemize}

Once the detachment is placed on the table, it acts as any other detachment, according to its order. Blips capture objectives for as long as they remain unrevealed.
