\section{History of the Genestealer Cults}

The Genestealer Curse is a parasitic infection of mind, body, and soul that gnaws at the heart of the Imperium. Hybrids of xenos and humans, gifted with limitless patience and an implacable hatred of the Imperium, these zealots of the Genestealer Cults prepare their offensive across several generations before erupting from the darkest corners of Humanity's domain. Thousands of cultists pray to their Patriarch, a living god from beyond the stars, and when the grim and glorious day of a Genestealer Cult's uprising comes, its warriors already hold a tenfold advantage. Saboteurs have wrecked supply lines, hidden agents have assassinated the principal commanders, and evacuation routes have been cut by demolition teams and industrial mining charges. Ammunition crates have been looted, fuel reserves run dry, transports have been diverted, and the support fleet, scuttled, drifts in orbit.

Experts in ambush, they strike from nowhere to sow ruin with firepower or with lightning raids. Guided by a monstrous Patriarch, this armada of zealous worshippers, industrial machines converted into engines of war, devastating xenos hybrids, and cunning leaders gifted with special powers has every means of securing victory. Armed transports surge from civilian dwellings to carry their monstrous passengers to the front, while charismatic leaders and agents possessed of supernatural faculties dismantle the enemy armies. When the cult unleashes the xenos monstrosities that serve as its shock troops, the enemy is already surrounded and trapped, weakened at leisure in preparation for a massacre planned long in advance.

Humanity is assailed from every side by xenos raiders and the nightmarish forces of Chaos. Each day, billions of lives are sacrificed upon the altar of war to hold the foe at bay. Yet the most insidious threat to Humanity's survival may already have entered the Imperium's bloodstream. Nested within the infrastructure of countless seemingly loyal worlds, Genestealer Cults wait, spreading their corruption through the population until the day they are ready to launch their bloody insurrections. On that day they pour forth, armed with stolen Imperial weapons and industrial tools turned to ignoble ends. Discovered upon the moons of Ymgarl, Genestealers were at first thought to be a unique species. After a series of searching investigations, the Ordo Xenos realised that they formed the vanguard of the Tyranid Hive Fleets, deployed ahead of their approach to sow discord and fear in their wake. Robust and armed with razor-sharp claws capable of shearing through armour, Genestealers serve as shock troops for the Hive Fleets.

When a Genestealer reaches a prey world, its macabre work begins. Within a few years it will have abducted hundreds of civilians and infected them with Tyranid genetic material. In time its victims will spawn filthy xenos-and-human Hybrids. The descendants of the first infected bear clearly identifiable xenos traits: broad, bulbous heads and sharpened talons. As the corruption spreads, later generations can pass for human and melt into the population, even into Imperial organisations such as the Adeptus Arbites, the Munitorum, and the Astra Militarum.

As it develops, the cult creates more specialised Hybrids to serve the Genestealer, now worshipped as a Patriarch. Gifted with psychic powers and corrupted by the Patriarch's will, Maguses are tasked with converting highly placed targets within the planet's army and government. Crowds of Hybrids are led by Primuses, generals and ambush specialists charged with coordinating the coming uprising. With an incomparable eye for detail and contingency, Nexoses provide tactical support to their Primuses' strategies. Sanctuses serve as assassins and Loci as bodyguards, while Clamavuses preach the cult's word and cut enemy communications. Atalan Jackals race into battle on dirtcycles and Wolfquads, harrying the enemy flanks, while Genestealers and Aberrants---sometimes reinforced by gene-alchemists called Biophaguses---serve as shock troops.

With uncontrollable violence, the cultists' uprising falls upon those who represent the greatest threat to their plans. Deafening discharges of seismic cannons shatter bone, rock drills plunge into flesh in a spray of blood, and explosives are used as makeshift grenades. The cult at last unveils its banners and seals: Acolyte Iconwards brandish its standards and worm-shaped totems, their presence driving the broods to new peaks of savagery.

Often the trigger for this uprising is the approach of a Tyranid bio-fleet, regarded by the cult as saviours. When the Hive Fleet vomits its swarms of warrior-creatures through the world's atmosphere, the cultists raise fervent prayers to their liberators. Even as the Tyranids exterminate and devour every source of biomass on the planet, the cultists continue to believe that these voracious xenos will elevate the faithful and help them transcend their mortal weaknesses, eagerly awaiting the community they were promised. For a time at least, Tyranids and cultists fight together, the Hive Mind ensuring that the former do not fall upon the latter. Maguses project illusions that warp and lacerate enemy minds, driving them to turn upon one another with sadistic pleasure. Directing their troops with remarkable skill, the Patriarch's generals sacrifice thousands of their warriors to open a path for the Tyranid assault. In this last exalted hour the Patriarch itself joins the melee, and its faithful sink into a frenetic trance as they watch their prophet massacre the infidels with claw and fang.

As soon as the last defenders are overwhelmed by this wave of chitin and razor-sharp claws, the Hive Mind subjects the Patriarch to its consciousness. It becomes an anonymous cell in the Tyranid swarm, and the gestalt called the Broodmind that united its cult is severed. In that tragic instant the cultists at last understand the truth. These stellar creatures they regarded as gods are revealed as the instrument of their death, for the Tyranids have only contempt for flesh.
