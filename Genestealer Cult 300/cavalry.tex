\section{Cavalry}

\subsection{Achilles Ridgerunner}

Employed as exploration vehicles by mining guilds and geologists, these all-terrain vehicles venture into hostile territory in search of promising deposits, whose coordinates they relay to their base. The mining lasers mounted on these vehicles serve to uncover rich veins of ore and precious metals, but these powerful energy emitters can pierce armour and flesh as readily as they cut rock, giving the all-terrain vehicle a potent weapon against enemy infantry or light armour.

The Ridgerunner has the \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} ability.

\subsection{Acolytes on Bikes}

Acolytes on Bikes roam the deserts and tunnels, their engines roaring as they race at full speed into the heart of the melee. They choose the ideal position from which to execute a decisive assault, using their bikes to cross obstacles and leap onto rooftops and gantries. They hunt as a pack, sharing a psychic bond so close that they can race through a disused tunnel at full speed without ever colliding, and which also allows them to fight as a single entity.

The Acolyte on Bike has the \textbf{Slave (Devastation)} ability.

\subsection{Primus on Bike}

Primuses are grandiloquent generals in time of war, but so long as the cult remains hidden their task is to spread the Genestealer Curse to other planets. Their gift for command and coordination ensures the rapid militarisation of the cult, while they oversee the diversion of vehicles, or even of ships. Tough champions and adored heroes among the Hybrids, Primuses emerge only when their cult has reached a position of strength. They appear in an early generation, but generally not before the third cycle.

They have the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Synapse (20 cm)}, \textbf{Charismatic (12 cm)}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.
