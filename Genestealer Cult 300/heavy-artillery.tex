\section{Heavy Artillery}

\subsection{Off-Table Artillery}

This also represents a long-range mass psychic attack deployed by Psykers inhabited by the Hive Mind. The Psychic Barrage has a cost in Destruction Points instead of an army points cost and may be used only by Forward Observers. See the General Rules for further details.

The Psychic Barrage uses a \textbf{Template (7.5 cm)} and is a Psychic Attack.
