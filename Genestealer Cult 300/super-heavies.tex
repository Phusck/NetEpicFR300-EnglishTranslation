\section{Super-heavies}

\subsection{Baneblade}

Bristling with weapons, the Baneblade is more akin to a walking fortress than a tank. A symbol of the Imperium's armoured supremacy, a single one of these war machines can serve as the spearhead of an Astra Militarum offensive, or as an impregnable defensive point during the most stubborn resistances. Gifted with firepower as versatile as it is formidable, exceptionally thick armour, and a generator of almost unlimited endurance, the Baneblade has been deployed on every theatre of war for millennia. When a Baneblade arrives on the battlefield, it invariably becomes the centre of the Imperial battle line. Guardsmen and other armour remain in its reassuring shadow while the enemy flees at the sight of the steel monster.

Its Baneblade Cannon and Lascannons have the \textbf{Turret} ability, while its Demolisher Cannon has the \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} ability. The Baneblade has the \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, and \textbf{Slave (Nest)} abilities.

\subsection{Macharius}

The Macharius belongs to a variety of so-called ``second-generation'' Baneblades. These are built on Forge Worlds that wish to obtain large military contracts, but that do not possess the STC data required to construct true Baneblades. Only a handful of Forge Worlds still manufacture Baneblades of the same design as those used during the Great Crusade, and the Adeptus Mechanicus of Mars keeps a close watch on the Forge Worlds authorised to access these data.

The Battle Cannon has the \textbf{Turret} ability. The Macharius has the \textbf{DR (6+)}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, and \textbf{Slave (Nest)} abilities.

\subsection{Shadowsword}

While many super-heavy vehicles excel in short-range battles, where their thick armour allows them to withstand the enemy's attacks, the Shadowsword is designed to annihilate its targets swiftly. Equipped with a Volcano Cannon, one of the most formidable weapons ever mounted on a tank, the Shadowsword is so feared that its mere appearance on the battlefield is often enough to put the enemy to flight. As a Shadowsword advances, the roar of its engines is drowned by the scream of the Volcano Cannon's generators as they power up. The red beam that pierces the dust of the battlefield shears through infantry, armour, and xenos monstrosities alike.

The Volcano Cannon has the \textbf{Damage ($+2$)} ability, and the Lascannons have the \textbf{Turret} ability. The Shadowsword has the \textbf{Wounds (3)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, and \textbf{Slave (Nest)} abilities.

\subsection{Mole}

The Mole is a mid-sized tunneller capable of transporting up to 60 men from underground and emerging at the heart of the enemy ranks to disgorge troops as close as possible to the fighting. Once it has broken the surface, it can provide fire support to the disembarked troops thanks to its medium-range armament.

Its Multiple Launcher has the \textbf{Artillery} ability. The Mole has the \textbf{Attached Transport}, \textbf{Deep Strike (2)}, \textbf{Wounds (2)}, \textbf{Transport (12)}, and \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} abilities.
