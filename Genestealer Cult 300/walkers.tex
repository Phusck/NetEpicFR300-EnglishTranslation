\section{Walkers}

\subsection{Armoured Sentinel}

Armoured Sentinels march alongside tank columns or at the head of Astra Militarum infantry formations, devastating the enemy without slowing their pace. The Scout Sentinel is not designed to fight on the front line. The open crew compartment is intended to offer an optimal field of vision, but it leaves the walker vulnerable to small-arms fire. In order to remain mobile, the walker's armour also lacks the layers of ferro-steel that protect battle tanks. Sentinels become excellent assault units when they receive reinforced armour, allowing them to advance through a hail of fire that would have finished a lighter walker. The mechanical legs of an Armoured Sentinel are modified with recoil compensators in place of gyrostabilisers, so that the walker can be given greater firepower at the expense of speed.

Armoured Sentinels have the \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} ability.

\subsection{Scout Sentinel}

Employed principally for reconnaissance and infantry-support missions, Scout Sentinels are used by many Astra Militarum regiments to locate and destroy enemy strongpoints, venturing far ahead of the main force to lay ambushes and conduct lightning raids with surprise attacks. Scout Sentinels are equipped with sophisticated gyrostabilisers in order to negotiate the steepest slopes and the craters of the battlefield. Their articulated legs allow them to pass silently through ruins or undergrowth, while remaining capable of rapid acceleration in open terrain.

They have the \textbf{Infiltration}, \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)}, and \textbf{Slave (Hunt)} abilities.
