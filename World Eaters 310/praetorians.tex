\section{Praetorians}

\subsection{Mastodon}

The Mastodon is one of the heaviest assault transports in the arsenal of the Astartes Chapters, and the one reserved for the most heavily fortified positions. Larger than a Land Raider, its transport compartment can carry up to 80 Space Marines across the most perilous battlefields, and protect them even against enemy Titans. The Mastodon's principal role is to carry these troops directly into the breach created by the Siege Melta mounted on its armoured prow. Few fortifications can withstand this formidable weapon, and it is very rare for an enemy occupying such positions to survive its blinding fire, the Astartes passing through molten stone, steel, and flesh to spread directly into the heart of the enemy position. Although every Chapter possesses Mastodons, they are used only with moderation. Their deployment is a major operation in itself, and they are therefore used only in prolonged siege operations, where one or more entire Chapters have amassed an overwhelming force to destroy an enemy that no other can face.

Its Siege Melta has the \textbf{Damage (+1)}, \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)}, and \textbf{Damages Buildings (AP $-4$/2)} abilities. Its Skyspear Missile Launcher has the \textbf{AA} ability, and its Flamers have the \textbf{Turret} and \textbf{Reduces Cover ($-3$)} abilities. The Mastodon has the \textbf{Agile}, \textbf{All-Round Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (2)}, \textbf{Assault Module}, and \textbf{Transport (12)} abilities.

\subsection{Stormbird}

Stormbirds are planetary transport craft, also endowed with great firepower. This spacecraft can rapidly carry Space Marine forces from ships in orbit into the heart of a battle, while providing supporting fire against enemy ground or air targets. Stormbirds are bulkier and larger than the Thunderhawk, but less agile in flight. They can transport up to 100 Space Marines or the equivalent in cargo, which is well beyond the Thunderhawk's carrying capacity. Stormbirds are piloted by two Space Marines, accompanied by hard-wired servitors charged with operating the craft's various weapons.

The Stormbird has the \textbf{All-Round Armour}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Transport (20)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (2)}, and \textbf{Flyer} abilities. Both of its weapons have the \textbf{Turret} ability.
