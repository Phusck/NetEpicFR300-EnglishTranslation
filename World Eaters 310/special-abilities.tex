\section{Special Abilities}

\subsection{Jamming}

All off-table arrivals whose arrival point is within 12~cm of a base with this ability cannot be placed on the table this turn, as if arriving on Impassable terrain.

\subsection{Rapid Intervention}

Detachments with this ability may perform Indirect Fire even with an Advance order.

\subsection{Fury of the Legion}

All bases with this ability reroll failed To-Hit rolls if they have a First Fire order.

\subsection{Primarch's Inspiration}

The detachment linked to a base with this ability is considered Fearless. In addition, any class 1 or 2 detachment that has a base within 12~cm of a base with this ability (including the detachment containing the base with this ability), and the base with this ability itself, may reroll 1s on their save, assault, and shooting rolls.

\subsection{Adaptability}

All bases with this ability in your list gain one ability chosen from: improve AF by 1, Urban Combat, or gain 5~cm of movement and Jump Packs. This ability is chosen before the armies are deployed. The ability is the same for every base with this ability in the list.

\subsection{Mortificator}

A base with this ability may be attached to a Dreadnought detachment, and the HQ rule functions with Dreadnoughts. In addition it has the Leader and Mechanic abilities, which apply only to Dreadnought detachments.

\subsection{Volkite Deflagration}

When a base with this ability shoots, draw a single imaginary line from the base to the aimed point. All bases crossed by that line are hit, whether the centre is hit or not.

These weapons cannot make Intercept Fire. For a structure, half of the bases present in garrison are hit. Due to its nature, it stops at the first structure it encounters as well as the first class 5 or higher obstacle.

\subsection{Red Butcher}

Red Butchers must always charge an opponent if they can, and may only receive Charge orders.

\subsection{Orbital Strike}

Detachments with this ability arrive on the table according to the following procedure:

\begin{itemize}
    \item During the Off-Table Arrival phase, the arrival point is determined as with the Deep Strike (2) ability, without applying the Limited Assault rule.
    \item The modules arrive during the Movement Phase, counting as a normal activation. The opponent may make Intercept Fire normally. The bases are considered to be at Altitude above the arrival point for range and line of sight.
    \item Bases destroyed by Intercept Fire are determined randomly among all the bases of the group (troops embarked in modules destroyed in flight or on the ground do not benefit from an Emergency Exit).
    \item Then place all surviving bases in coherency and within 10~cm of the arrival point. A base cannot be placed on an enemy base or on Impassable terrain.
    \item Bases with this ability make an attack on their arrival point. Centre a template on the base and resolve an attack against the bases under the template as described on the profile.
    \item Embarked troops disembark as from a transport.
    \item Walkers may be transported by Module, each Walker counting as 2 spaces.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assault Ram}

Bases with this ability are reinforced, and if they are destroyed in flight, the transported troops are entitled to an Emergency Exit roll even without Jump Packs.

Bases with this ability may attempt to destroy a building by ramming it. They must land in contact with it to do so. If they choose this action, they make a damage roll on the building with the Damages Buildings (AP $-2$/2) ability for each base attempting to destroy the building at the moment of contact. In addition, enemy troops in the structure are shaken and roll 1D6 less in assault this turn.

\subsection{Plasma Weapons}

Because these weapons use a large amount of energy, only one Plasma weapon is allowed per titan.

\subsection{Devotion Bell}

All Daemons within 25~cm have a $-2$ penalty to their Instability tests. In addition, no base within 25~cm may receive or use Chaos Reward cards.

\subsection{Ursus Claw}

A weapon with this ability reduces the AF of one target (and only one) in contact by 3.

\subsection{Reconnaissance Land Speeder}

The Land Speeder is a separate detachment and need not maintain detachment coherency with the titan. If the Titan is destroyed, the Land Speeder is removed from the table. The Land Speeder cannot capture objectives.

\subsection{Landing Modules}

Landing Modules follow the following rules:

\begin{itemize}
    \item The Modules are divided into groups, each group consisting of enough Drop Pods to transport a detachment, one Assault Cannon Deathstorm, and one Missile Deathstorm.
    \item During the Off-Table Arrival phase, the arrival point is determined as with the Deep Strike (2) ability, without applying the Limited Assault rule.
    \item The modules arrive during the Movement Phase, counting as a normal activation. The opponent may make Intercept Fire normally. The Modules are considered to be at Altitude above the arrival point for range and line of sight.
    \item Modules destroyed by Intercept Fire are determined randomly among all the Modules of the group (troops embarked in modules destroyed in flight or on the ground do not benefit from an Emergency Exit).
    \item Then place all surviving modules in coherency and within 10~cm of the arrival point. A Module cannot be placed on an enemy base or on Impassable terrain.
    \item Place a Template (12~cm) centred on the Missile Deathstorm, which hits on a 4+ with AP 0. The other Modules of the Group are not hit by the Missile Deathstorm's shots. Then remove the Missile Deathstorm from play; it is considered destroyed.
    \item Embarked troops disembark from the Modules as from a transport. Then remove the Drop Pods; they are considered destroyed.
    \item The Assault Cannon Deathstorm remains in play and has an impact on the game (blocking line of sight, capturing objectives, control zone, and so on).
    \item Walkers may be transported by Module, each Walker counting as 2 spaces.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Vortex Missile}

The Vortex Missile uses a Template (7.5~cm) which, once placed, scatters 3D6~cm. The Vortex Missile does not hit targets at Altitude. It cannot make Intercept Fire. Any base hit by the template (even if the centre is not under the template) suffers the effects of the Vortex Missile. If the target has shields, the vortex may destroy them. The target immediately loses 2D3 Energy Shields and Energy Fields, or 1D3 Deflector Shields and Organic Metal (with no Psychic Save linked to these protections).

Bases that passed their Psychic Save or that still have active shields suffer no damage. All bases are pushed to the edge of the template.

All bases hit suffer 1D3 hits that are Psychic Attacks, and structures are hit by a Damages Buildings (AP $-5$/3) attack.

During End-of-Turn Effects, the vortex disappears on a 5+. If it remains in play, it may be activated in the Combat Phase and then scatters 3D6~cm, hitting everything in its path.

\subsection{Harpoon Missile}

If the Harpoon Missile hits its target and the target fails its armour save, it remains lodged. While it is lodged, its target suffers a penalty of 3 to AF and a penalty of 1 to To-Hit with all its weapons. In addition to the initial damage, it causes a damage during End-of-Turn Effects while it is lodged. After causing this damage, it dislodges on a 4+ on the first turn it is lodged, 3+ on the second, and 2+ on subsequent turns.
