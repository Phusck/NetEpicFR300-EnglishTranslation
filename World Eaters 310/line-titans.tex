\section{Line Titans}

\subsection{Reaver Titan}

The Reaver is a very old Titan pattern and, if it is not as heavily armed as the Warlord (which forms the backbone of the Battle Titans of the Titan Legios), or as fast and agile as the Warhound, it remains a powerful war machine in its own right. It is protected by thick adamantium armour plates and endowed with devastating firepower. Like its larger cousin, the Reaver is considered a divine engine of destruction, an incarnation of the Machine God's power, thrice blessed by an Adeptus Mechanicus Tech-Priest before committing to battle. Each Reaver is commanded by a Princeps, who is connected directly to the Titan's Mind Impulse Unit. He is assisted by two crew members, holding the rank of Moderati, who control the Titan's movement and weapons. The Reaver's power source is its plasma reactor, which is tended by a Tech-Priest who carefully manages the Titan's available electrical supply and is in turn assisted in this complex task by Servitors connected directly into the Titan's systems. The Reaver has three weapon mounts. Its carapace weapon is the equivalent of a smaller Warhound-sized weapon, while its arms carry the heaviest weapons --- up to the size of a Warlord weapon. The largest Titan weapons, such as Quake Cannons, are still beyond a Reaver's load capacity. The Mars pattern is said to be the oldest Titan archetype still in service, its origins even predating the Great Crusade.

The Reaver Titan must buy three weapons (two arm weapons and one carapace weapon) and may buy 1 Banner option and/or 1 Head. The Reaver Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (6)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (4)} abilities.

\subsection{Warbringer Titan}

The Warbringer Nemesis Titan is an immense war machine. It is designed to destroy enemy Titans at range and to serve as a fire-support platform. It sits between the more common Reaver and Warlord classes. Its mass and firepower often place it on the front lines of the battlefield alongside the various classes of Battle Titans. Its defence batteries protect it adequately against aerial attacks. Within the Titan Legios, it has a reputation for having a restless Machine Spirit. Sensor artefacts and other aberrant phenomena are commonplace aboard such a divine engine. The Tech-Priests who serve these machines praise the complex mysteries that led to this creation, and many among the Magi of the Machine God consider it an honour to be assigned to such a machine.

The Warbringer Titan must buy two arm weapons and choose one carapace weapon (either a Volcano Cannon or a Quake Cannon; the cost of this back weapon is already included in the Titan) and may buy 1 Head. The Cerberus Cannon has the \textbf{AA} ability. The Warbringer Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (7)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (5)} abilities.

\subsection{Warlord Titan}

The Warlord Battle Titan has always been the heart of the Titan Legios; indeed its design predates the Imperium itself. Some Warlords have been in service since the Dark Age of Technology, and many others date from the Age of Strife. These ancient war machines are carefully preserved and maintained by the Tech-Priests, so that energy still surges through their powerful limbs when the proper litanies are performed. The Warlord is a heavy Titan intended to fight on the most active front lines. It is protected by six shield generators and one of the thickest armours that can be seen on a Battle Titan. The Warlord can mount up to four independent primary weapon systems and several secondary weapon systems to increase its short-range firepower. The great longevity of the Warlord Titan has led to a plethora of specialised equipment designed for it. Some Titan Legios are composed almost entirely of Warlord Titans fitted with siege-warfare devices and are used only to demolish fortresses and cities that resist the Emperor's will.

The Warlord Titan must buy four weapons (two arm weapons and two carapace weapons) and may buy 2 Banner options and/or 1 Head. The Warlord Titan has the \textbf{Wounds (9)}, \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Damage (+1) in assault}, and \textbf{Energy Shields (6)} abilities.
