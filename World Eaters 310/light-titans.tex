\section{Light Titans}

\subsection{Dire Wolf Titan}

The Dire Wolf class Titan is a heavy scout Titan, placing it between the Warhound and Reaver class Titans. It was deployed by both loyalist and traitor Titan Legions during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. The Dire Wolf carries its massive main weapon at the rear of its carapace, sacrificing some of its mobility for the greater stability needed to bear the recoil and weight of that weapon. The Dire Wolf Titan is particularly favoured by Legio Audax, a traitor Legion that fought during the Horus Heresy.

The Dire Wolf Titan must buy 1 carapace weapon (a Neutron Laser). It may take a Conversion Beam as an arm weapon. The Dire Wolf Titan has the \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{Extended Coherency (25 cm)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (2)}, and \textbf{Agile} abilities.

\subsection{Warhound Titan}

Warhounds form the smallest class of Titans. They are the eyes and ears of the Titan Legios and, during campaigns, range ahead of the main groups. Faster and more agile than Reavers or Warlords, they lack their armour and firepower, however. Like all Princeps, Warhound commanders perceive and react through their Titan's control sensors and neural links. Their daring is proverbial. Warhounds often operate in pairs, so that they can outpace and outflank larger Titans they may encounter. In a major engagement, Warhounds operate on the flanks and seek to engage enemy tanks and infantry rather than other Titans, where their Vulcan Mega-Bolters, Inferno Guns, and Turbo-Lasers (all lighter Titan weapons) can still inflict immense damage.

The Warhound Titan must buy 2 arm weapons. The Warhound Titan has the \textbf{DR (5+)}, \textbf{Wounds (4)}, \textbf{Extended Coherency (25 cm)}, \textbf{Energy Shields (2)}, and \textbf{Agile} abilities.
