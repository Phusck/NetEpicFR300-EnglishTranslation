\section{Special Rules}

\subsection{Allies}

You may spend up to 25\% of your points on allies from the Solar Auxilia Codex and the Titan Legion Codex.

\subsection{Variable Strength}

Legion Formations have variable strength. When you purchase a formation, you must decide which strength to take. When you choose a strength, the associated cost is determined as follows: the first strength is associated with the first cost, the second strength with the second cost, and so on.

\subsection{Variable Strength and Elite Rerolls}

The number of Elite rerolls of a detachment depends on its strength. The smallest strength uses the Elite value shown on the profile; for the second strength that number is doubled, and for the third it is tripled. If the smallest detachment has the Elite (0) ability, then it grants no Elite reroll for the first strength, 1 reroll for the second strength, and 2 rerolls for the third strength.

\subsection{Variable Strength and Battery Weapons}

Detachments with bases that have the Battery ability obtain one template if the number of remaining bases is less than or equal to the first strength, two templates if it is less than or equal to the second strength, and three templates if it is greater than the second strength. You may distribute the detachment's bases as you wish among the different templates within the Battery rule.

\subsection{Transport Limits}

Rhinos are limited in their transport capacity. They cannot transport a base with the Jump Packs ability, or a Terminator base.
