\section{Walkers}

\subsection{Contemptor Dreadnought}

The Contemptor-pattern Dreadnought dates from the Unification Wars and is the fusion of a war machine and an interred Space Marine. Contemptors are often seen fighting with the Space Marine Legions, where they serve in the vanguard of many victories. The Contemptor incorporates numerous technological systems drawn from the Legio Cybernetica. When it functions correctly, even Lascannon shots are turned aside in glittering flashes by the energy shield. The Contemptor has incredible strength and also the ability to make bursts of speed unthinkable given its imposing mass and thick hull.

They have the \textbf{Reroll 1s in Assault} and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.

\subsection{Deredeo Dreadnought}

The Deredeo Dreadnought is the fruit of the project to improve the fusion of Terran and Mechanicum technologies, and it gave birth to the first Dreadnoughts of the Legiones Astartes. It shares many components and core systems with the hugely successful Contemptor class. Rather than being a generalist assault unit, the Deredeo is a heavy-weapons platform intended to combine superior firepower with the durability of a Dreadnought chassis. Deployed in limited numbers in each of the Legions, the Deredeo is treated as a specialised unit because its production and maintenance require great resources. Thanks to its survivability and its murderous firepower, it is in high demand from the few Forge Worlds capable of producing them.

Both variants of the Deredeo Dreadnought have the \textbf{Elite (1)} ability.

\subsection{Saturnine Dreadnought}

The Saturnine Dreadnought is the largest, most resilient, and best-armed Dreadnought used by the Legiones Astartes. Its pauldrons are also fitted with teleportation synchronisers, and its heavy armament made it a true walking engine of destruction. Only Legionnaires of exceptional will, focus, and discipline can successfully pilot Saturnine armour. In terms of armament, the Saturnine Dreadnought has two known configurations, allowing them to face the most heavily armoured opponents.

Both variants of the Saturnine Dreadnought have the \textbf{Elite (1)} ability.

\subsection{Leviathan Siege Dreadnought}

The Leviathan Dreadnought was conceived in the greatest secrecy, not on Mars but on Terra itself. Its massive frame incorporates ancient technologies, some of which date back to the Age of Strife. Designed for siege warfare, it is principally equipped with close-combat and support weapons capable of destroying buildings and vehicles. The Leviathan's oversized atomic reactor allows it to carry more powerful weapons than other classes of Dreadnought, and it also feeds reinforced energy fields that surpass those developed for Contemptor-pattern Dreadnoughts. That power comes at a cost, however, and the Leviathan's systems exert a deadly strain on the mind of its occupant.

Both variants of the Leviathan Siege Dreadnought have the \textbf{Elite (1)} ability.
