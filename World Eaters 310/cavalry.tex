\subsection{Land Speeder Proteus}

Staples of most Space Marine Legion armouries, Land Speeders are reconnaissance and rapid-attack craft that use anti-gravity technology to achieve extraordinary mobility and speed on the battlefield. Land Speeder squadrons provide the Space Marines with a flexible core of rapidly deployable firepower. They make highly effective tank hunters and close-support units. As a result, Land Speeder formations are often called upon to operate as a rapid-reaction force, held in reserve to intercept enemy counter-attacks.

They have the \textbf{Elite (1)} and \textbf{Antigrav} abilities.

\subsection{Bikes}

A Space Marine Bike is of robust construction, of necessity, for it must be able to carry an armoured Space Marine at high speed while still allowing combat manoeuvres. Even at low speed, the impact of so heavy an armoured bike is enough to smash through most of the obstacles that litter battlefields. Experienced Space Marine bikers are even able to pass through plascrete walls without slowing or taking damage in return. This mobility, combined with their prowess in assault, makes them an asset for rapid flanking moves and surgical strikes.

They have the \textbf{Reroll 1s in Assault} and \textbf{Elite (1)} abilities.

\subsection{Recon Bikes}

Recon Bikes are tasked with carrying out reconnaissance missions and launching attacks behind enemy lines. These specialised troops are the eyes and ears of their Legion in the field. The principal advantage of these bikes is their speed, which allows them to cover ground far more quickly than infantry and to go where heavier vehicles cannot.

They have the \textbf{Reroll 1s in Assault}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, and \textbf{Infiltration} abilities.

\subsection{Scimitar Jetbikes}

Scimitar Jetbikes are mounted on Imperial jetbikes, the pinnacle of anti-grav technology. They are feared attack units, endowed with considerable firepower combined with exceptional speed and manoeuvrability. Given the materials required for their manufacture and maintenance, Legion demand has far exceeded production capacity. Scimitar Jetbikes are heavily armed and also have good capabilities in assault. This versatility allows them to fulfil many roles on the battlefield.

They have the \textbf{Reroll 1s in Assault}, \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{Antigrav}, and \textbf{Lightning Attack} abilities.
