\section{Infantry}

\subsection{Angron}

He grew up on a violent world where he was enslaved and surgically mutilated to become a gladiator. He then became the undefeated lord of the arenas, a peerless killer. He rose with the other slaves, but their fight was doomed. The Emperor saved him from death and placed him at the head of his Legion, but Angron would never forgive the Emperor for not saving his companions. He never wanted to remove his cortical implants, which heighten his already terrifying aggression. Angron never loved his gene-sons, on whom he practised decimation when he was not massacring them himself during his frequent losses of control. He imposed psycho-surgery implants on the World Eaters. The Butcher's Nails amplify anger and rage, gradually transforming the World Eaters into bloodthirsty, psychotic berserkers, less and less able to control themselves. No affection grew between him and his brother Primarchs. Angron quickly joined Horus in his betrayal, but his only true master is the rage and bloodlust that reign within him.

Angron has the \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, \textbf{Psychic Save (4+)}, \textbf{Jump Packs}, \textbf{Dodge (4+)}, \textbf{Elite (3)}, \textbf{Leader}, \textbf{Charismatic (30 cm)}, \textbf{Reroll Assault Dice}, \textbf{Primarch's Inspiration}, \textbf{Damage (+2) in Assault}, and \textbf{Duellist} abilities.

\subsection{Apothecary}

The Apothecaries of a Legion are charged with the most sacred duties: the care of the dead and the safeguarding of the Legion's future. As healers on the battlefield, an Apothecary's task is to aid the wounded and restore them so that they may return to the fight. For those who are condemned or already dead, they bear the heavy duty of recovering the Legion's genetically modified zygotes for implantation into future generations of Space Marines. These tasks demand dexterity, medical knowledge, and above all courage. It is one thing to fight an enemy, and another to turn from the battle to tend the dead and wounded under enemy fire, with no thought for one's own safety.

They have the \textbf{Elite (1)}, \textbf{HQ}, \textbf{Attached Character}, and \textbf{Medic} abilities.

\subsection{Red Butcher}

The Red Butchers are ferocious warriors, forerunners of the Berserkers of Khorne. The psycho-surgery inflicted on the Red Butchers by the cybernetic implants known as the Butcher's Nails hammers regular waves of pain through their skulls. The Red Butchers can be controlled only by the use of implants, and they are imprisoned in their Terminator armour. Between battles, the Red Butchers are chained in the holds of the World Eaters' warships, frothing and howling with rage, until they are once more hurled into the enemy ranks.

\subsection{Tactical Legionnaires}

Every Legion of the Adeptus Astartes must regularly recruit new soldiers in order to survive. The recruitment process varies from Legion to Legion, and most Legions enlist mainly from specific worlds affiliated to them. These aspirants will always be young men, for a body that is too old will be unable to endure the implantation of the organs destined to transform it into a Space Marine. One of the greatest assets of a Space Marine Legion is the sustained and coordinated bolter fire that Tactical Squads can provide. The firepower of this storm of explosive shells is such that enemy infantry formations and even light vehicle squadrons are reduced to fragments by a single volley of bolters. Tactical formations are extremely flexible, able to assault heavily fortified positions, to take and hold strategic objectives, or simply to massacre the enemy on almost any terrain and in any situation.

\subsection{Techmarine}

Techmarines are the engineers and technicians of a Legion, charged with tending its arsenal. Each Legion possesses its own foundries and armouries, as well as its own artisans. Some have centuries-old agreements with Forge Worlds that supply them with ships and weapons, which the Techmarines then modify to match the demands of the Astartes. This practice varies among the Legions, but few of them have the means to build their own vessels, most of which are manufactured in the shipyards of Mars and Jupiter. The Marine armourers are extremely skilled artisans, whose chief is called the Master of the Forge, a prestigious and highly respected title.
