\section{The Tyranid Swarm}

The Tyranids travel through the intergalactic void in vast Hive Fleets. These fleets consist of millions of living ships, each carrying thousands of creatures spawned within breeding chambers whose walls are coated in organic mucus. All these creatures are connected to the immense entity that is their ship, which in turn exists only to serve the Hive Fleet as a whole. The Tyranids therefore possess a collective intelligence that allows them to act and perceive their surroundings as a single being, granting them complete control over every component of the Hive Fleet.

From the tentacled monstrosities that are the Tyranid ships to the smallest protozoan, every creature is subject to the relentless will of the Hive Mind. Giving each of these organisms a sufficiently developed brain to act independently would be a waste of biological resources. The smallest creatures are therefore controlled by organisms created specifically for this purpose.

They function in perfect symbiosis, coordinated through psychic commands transmitted naturally from one creature to another. At the cost of many lives, it has been discovered that when the influence of a higher Tyranid organism is removed, the creatures dependent upon it tend to revert to instinctive behaviour. For this reason, neither the Hive Fleets nor their swarms possess a commander-in-chief in the conventional sense. Instead, commands and information are transmitted through complex psychic connections.

All Tyranid organisms capable of relaying the Hive Mind's commands are powerful Psykers. They communicate with their kind not through language, but by means of an innate form of telepathy. That such a concentration of Psykers can exist without drawing the Daemons of Chaos into the material universe is evidence either of the immense power of the Tyranids' psychic abilities or, more simply, of the creatures' lack of souls.

The Tyranids do not manufacture their weapons and ships from metal or plastic. Instead, they use genetic manipulation and bio-construction, employing biological materials harvested from the worlds they have consumed. Every gland, bio-weapon, and projectile is a Tyranid organism in its own right, from the spores that contaminate a planet's atmosphere to the twisted spires that rise into the sky and announce the presence of the Tyranids upon a world.

To face the Tyranids is to confront a race encompassing everything from the microscopic to the colossal, every organism presenting a deadly threat.

The space-dwelling Norn Queens and the formidable Dominatrices are among the most highly evolved Tyranid organisms. They give birth to endless hordes of creatures, vessels, and organic weapons. In battle, the Tyranids overwhelm their enemies beneath an avalanche of claws and fangs. Their destructive potential is enormous, but the physical threat is only one aspect of the danger they pose to the other races.

A Tyranid invasion is a terrifying spectacle, and it is not uncommon for defenders to take their own lives or descend into madness upon witnessing the scale of the carnage. When the invasion begins, the sky takes on a reddish hue as the atmosphere becomes saturated with poisonous spores. It then darkens as the first winged organisms launch their attack, spreading terror and confusion.

Genestealers emerge from hiding and assault enemy command posts while Lictors prepare ambushes. They then consume the brains of their victims, passing information concerning the enemy's strengths and weaknesses to the rest of the swarm.

Thousands of Mycetic Spores fall from the sky and split open upon impact like overripe fruit, releasing swarms of creatures amid torrents of viscous fluid. These creatures rapidly gather into an unstoppable tide, and within a matter of days the entire surface of the planet is covered by Tyranid organisms of every imaginable size and form.

The Tyranids' microscopic spores establish themselves so effectively that even the native plant life is irreversibly altered before being consumed. They germinate rapidly, allowing the Hive Fleet to absorb the planet's biomass once all resistance has been defeated.

During the final stages of the invasion, swarms of Rippers consume everything in their path before plunging their bloated bodies into the acidic fluids of the digestion pools. Once broken down, the biomass is transferred to the ships of the Hive Fleet through Capillary Towers. Upon reaching the interior of the vessels, it is reshaped to create new organisms. In this way, the bodies of the planet's defenders, along with its native fauna and flora, are recycled.

Even before an invasion begins, the defenders must contend with despair. Hive Fleets generate a psychic presence known as the Shadow in the Warp, which makes astropathic communication—and therefore any attempt to call for assistance—impossible. The Hive Fleet thus isolates every world in its path before consuming it.

Relentless and inexorable, the Tyranid race represents the greatest threat that Humanity and the other races of the galaxy have ever faced.

The Tyranids are not native to this galaxy. They travelled through the interstellar void for millennia, crossing these dark expanses for centuries before reaching the edge of the \textit{Segmentae Majoris}.

No one knows whether they began this perilous journey because they had already consumed all life in their galaxy of origin or because they were fleeing an enemy even more formidable than themselves. It is possible that they have fed upon planets since time immemorial and that this galaxy is merely the latest to suffer their depredations.

Whatever the truth, they must have expended unimaginable quantities of energy and demonstrated an inconceivable determination to survive such an arduous journey. During their voyages of thousands of years through the void, they entered a state resembling hibernation.

Now they have awakened, and they are hungry. The survival of every living creature is at risk.
