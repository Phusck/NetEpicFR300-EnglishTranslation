\section{Game Turn}
A game of NetEpic is played over several turns, each divided into four phases: the Strategy Phase, the Movement Phase, the Combat Phase, and the End Phase.

The following is a brief summary of each phase:

\begin{description}
    \item[Strategy Phase:] Both players place some or all of their off-table forces onto the battlefield. They then assign orders to each of their detachments present on the table \textit{(Inc)} using order counters. Orders are assigned secretly by placing the counters face down. Finally, initiative is determined.

    \item[Movement Phase:] The players alternate moving their detachments.

    \item[Combat Phase:] Combat is resolved during this phase. One player chooses either one of their detachments to fire or an assault to resolve, after which their opponent does the same.

    \item[End Phase:] Titans and Praetorians are repaired. Both players then check the victory conditions of the chosen scenario. \textit{(Inc)} If either player has fulfilled these conditions, that player wins the game. Otherwise, a new turn begins. Finally, detachments that are falling back may attempt to rally.
\end{description}
