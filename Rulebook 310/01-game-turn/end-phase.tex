\section{End Phase}

All actions have now been resolved. It is time to resolve any remaining effects, check the victory conditions, and finally make Rally Tests.

\subsection{End-of-Turn Effects}

All end-of-turn effects are resolved at this stage, such as repairing Titans. Further details are provided in the descriptions of the relevant abilities.

\subsection{Capturing Objectives}

To control an objective, a player must have a base capable of securing objectives within 10~cm of the centre of the objective marker. \textit{(Inc)}

If both players have eligible bases within 10~cm of the objective's centre, add together the remaining Wounds of each player's eligible bases within range.

A single base may capture only one objective in a given turn. If several objectives are in range, choose one.

A base within a terrain feature that provides a cover save, or which is garrisoned within range of the objective, counts as having one additional Wound for this calculation.

If one player has an advantage of at least two Wounds, that player captures the objective. Otherwise, the objective remains under the control of the player who controlled it during the previous turn. If it was not controlled during the previous turn, it remains uncontrolled.

\paragraph{Securing Objectives}

All bases can control objectives except: \textit{(Inc)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Bases at altitude.
    \item Bases with a Fall Back order.
    \item Bases embarked within a transport.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Checking the Victory Conditions}

Check the victory conditions and calculate each player's Victory Points according to the scenario.

If the victory conditions have been fulfilled, the battle ends. Otherwise, a new turn begins.

\subsection{Rally Tests}

If a detachment has a Fall Back order and is permitted to make a Rally Test, for example due to the \textit{Charismatic} ability, the test is made at this stage.

The player with the initiative makes the first Rally Test. Their opponent then makes one, and the players continue alternating until all Rally Tests have been resolved.
